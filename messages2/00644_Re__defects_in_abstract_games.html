i like this line, that's a favorite book of mine, 4 feet away infact,
....we could state it as

_Matt's_Theorem_:
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| The system of rules for any non-trivial game, including purely abstract |
| games, must contain at least one defect. |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+

so, for example, my 5-in-a-row game on a hexgrid, with only 3 directions
to make the 5-in-a-row, must be "trivial", since the rules are but 2 and
are:
1) each player takes turns placing a new stone of their
color upon an unoccupied hexpoint.

2) the game is ended and won by the first player to
make 5-in-a-row in any of the 3 possible directions.

so even though i have no idea what the triviality is, i can take umbrage
in the Theorem that it must exist. or...is there a defect in the rules?
