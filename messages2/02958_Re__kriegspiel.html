> Can someone explain this game a bit.

Basically, it's almost-blind chess with two players and a referee.
Each player can only see their own pieces. Before a player moves, the
referee tells that player whether their king is in check, and from
where (row, column, short diagonal, long diagonal). Before moving,
the player may ask whether there are any possible pawn captures, but
if there are, that player must attempt to make at least one. Finally,
if a player attempts to make an illegal move, the move is retracted
and that player must try again until a legal move is made. I think
that that is it.

It can be played with two boards and a screen.
