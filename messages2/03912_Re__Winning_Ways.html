Depends what you're interested in. As the title says it, volume 2
describes ``games in particular''. It will tell you how to play and
win at a great number of games (in particular). If that's what
interests you, you can pretty well do without the first volume as long
as you know what a Nim-sum is. Even the concept of a number is used
sparingly (essentially only in a breve discussion about the game of
fox and geese).

However, if it's the theory that interests you mainly - or if you want
to know all the different aspects of the question - or if you just
can't stand (as is my case) having half a book - then you need the
first volume. Getting it is extremely difficult as it is out of
print. So if you want it, you have three options: (1)find a copy in a
library somewhere and photocopy it (this is of course illegal but it
is morally tolerable for an out of print book), (2)find a used copy
through the 'net, or (3)if you can read German, buy the German
edition (the editor is Vieweg, the german title is ``Gewinnen'', and
it's in four volumes which can be ordered seperately for about DEM50
each).

In any case, I suggest you write a letter to the editor and to the
authors (as I did) to point out how important that book is and how
useful a reprint would be. If enough people do this, there is some
hope that they will give in.
