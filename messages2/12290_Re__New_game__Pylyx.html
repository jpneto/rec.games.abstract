There _is_ an additional step. In the extended pie rule, player 1 sets
up a position and player 2 chooses sides. In Pylyx's mechanism, player
1 sets up a position, then player 2 chooses a side or turn order, then
player 1 chooses a turn order or side.

I'm not saying it's a bad mechanism, just that it can be made simpler
while keeping the same functionality.
