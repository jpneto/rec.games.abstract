Survival is a win for player 1. I solved it by exhaustive search
two years ago. It took 36 hours on a pentium.

Entropy is a draw. Either player can force a draw simply
by keeping his pieces all together. He cannot win that way,
but neither can his opponent.

Neutron might be interesting to try to solve. Experience indicates
that there is a very large first move advantage, and games are
always fairly short.
