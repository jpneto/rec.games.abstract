Here are general startegies for games that have a first turn advantage.
Note that the second needs to be modified for a first turn disadvantage
game.

(A) Play two games simultaneously, on opposite sides. Great fun
especially with games like chess.

(B) One player makes a move. The second has to choose either to
respond to that move or take on the role of first player.
E.g. in chess, make a 'poor' opening move.

(C) Both of the above (for ultimate in whackiness)
