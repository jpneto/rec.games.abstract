Morella Star systems, based on Mars, have vacancies for a further
6 crew members aboard their latest product, "The Traveller".

The Traveller is a revolution in starship design incorporating
new technologies from the 4 known races of the galaxy.

The positions open are medical / science officer, engineer,
pilot, navigator, communications officer and security officer.
Applicants are requested to access cyberspace for application information.

Become part of the new era of exploration and colonisation - join
Morella Starsystems today.

----
The Void is a new Play by Email RPG. It centers around the crew of
an experimental starship called the Traveller, and is set at a time
when interstellar travel is in its infancy and mankind is just
beginning to colonise other solar systems.

It is intended to be a mixture of sci-fi and horror and I am designing
it to appeal to mature audiences. Therefore players must be over 18.

We already have a captain but we are still looking for a navigator,
science officer, engineer, communications officer and security
officer. There is an application for the role of pilot pending but any
other submissions for this role will be looked at.
