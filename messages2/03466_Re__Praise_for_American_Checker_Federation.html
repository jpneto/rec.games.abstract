The point at which to stop allowing a particular computer to
play, is when it can not show any improvement against humans.
So if a computer won the tournament, but lost a game, it
should be allowed to play in future tournaments. In Checkers
we have reached this point. In othello we seem to have reached
this point, although it has not been tested. In chess, we don't
really know how well computers play against humans because they
are not allowed to play in tournaments. Until this changes
everything is speculation. In Go things are worse because even
computer tournaments don't allow supercomputers to play.
