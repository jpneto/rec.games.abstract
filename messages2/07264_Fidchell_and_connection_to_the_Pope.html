The Book of Lismore of the 15th century describes a gift of the game
of Fidchell given to Pope Boneface in the 7th century and that it was
a game that the "youths of Rome" would play.

I think that Gwyddbwyll and Fidchell was one and the same game but
that it was not a hnefatafl type of game.
