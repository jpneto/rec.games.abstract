Try to get the book "A Mah Jongg Handbook" by Eleanor Noss Whitney
PAN BOOKS London & Sydney ISBN 0-330-29244-7
It's about 5 Pounds sterling.

This book is great, you have chinese rules, japanese rules (the ones I play since they make the most
competetive game), some variants, examples, strategies (you can play this game really strong if
you read this).

Actually the rules and examples (you will definitely need them!) are too massive to be mailed here.
