The 3'rd annual email LOA tournament is over, and the result
is a 3-way tie for first place.

ddyer (5-2) defeated dpriddy and lost to engleberg and hthordsen (4-3)
dpriddy (5-2) defeated engleberg and lost to ddyer and ottavio (4-3)
engleberg (5-2) defeated ddyer and lost to dpriddy and ottavio (4-3)

There may be some nit-picking way to decide a winner based on style
points,
but for my money, this is as even as it could possibly be.

Another interesting point is that the robot player LOA-AI easily
qualified
for the final round, by defeating almost everyone in its section, but
completely washed out in the final round, finishing 0-7. I take this
as an encouraging sign, that the robot has reached a level of competance

comparable to a reasonable player.
