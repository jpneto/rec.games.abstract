1998 Trax World Championship Tournaments
========================================

***** A reminder - entries close on 31 March, 1998. *****


Call for participation:

The Trax World Championship consists of a series of 3 tournaments, and is
held every second year. Information on the tournament may be found at
http://ia-spare.massey.ac.nz/trax/tourneys.html


Regional Qualifier Tournaments:

There are three zones, with each zone providing 2 qualifiers. To enter,
contact the tournament director of your zone as soon as possible. The
qualifier tournaments are open to any Trax player who registers by the
specified closing date. To register, or obtain further information, contact
the tournament director for your geographical region.

* Zone 1: North and South America
Tournament director is Mel Nicholson.
To register, contact Mel at mel@csua.berkeley.edu by March 31, 1998

* Zone 2: Europe, Africa & Middle East
Tournament director is Martin Pedersen.
To register, contact Martin at martin.pedersen@person.dk by March 31,
1998

* Zone 3: Australia, New Zealand, Asia, and Pacific Islands
Tournament directors are Donald Bailey and Tom Siegenthaler.
To register, contact Donald at D.G.Bailey@massey.ac.nz by March 31, 1998

The regional tournaments should be completed by May 31, 1998.

The tournament format is at the discretion of the individual tournament
directors, although most games will be played live over the table or via an
internet connection.
