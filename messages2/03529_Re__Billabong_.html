Epaminondas is by Robert Abbott, a development of his game Crossings
which first appeared in _A Gamut of Games_.

Eric Solomon has published at least one collection of pencil-and-paper
games, including a p&p adaptation of Sigma File. The title is _Games
With Pencil and Paper_, currently in print from Dover Books, ISBN
0-48-627872-7, $4.95. Highly recommended.
