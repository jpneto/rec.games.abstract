My thanks to M.Nicholson@pioneer.arc.nasa.gov for solving a problem we
had - how to pictorially describe a Trax layout in ansi characters! The
problem with the standard method of describing games is that too many
people still get their left & rights mixed up. If you'e interested in
the official notation (I don't know if it was included in the overseas
sets (by the way, Trax was invented in New Zealand 1980)) here it is
for Mels contrived example game.

o o 2. A2S If played under. playing to left (A1n)
