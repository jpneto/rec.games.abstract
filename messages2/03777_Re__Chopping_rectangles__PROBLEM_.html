There are several threads on this in geometry.research.
One way is to cut a circle into 4 pie shaped slices.
Put two on the bottom with the round edge pointing down.
Then fit the other other two slices into the wedges.
You will end up with something like a rounded rectangle.
Some people think that if you keep doing this you will get a rectangle.
