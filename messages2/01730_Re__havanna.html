You can also win by connecting two vertices of this order-8 (or
order-10 for more advanced play) hexagon. It was invented by Christiaan
Freeling and can be found in New Rules for Classic Games, by Wayne Scmitt-
berger. For those who are interested, the book is published by John Wiley
& Sons, Inc. (1992) and costs $9.95 plus tax.
