I think you are refering to the 30 Mhz arm2 (or 3). This
is a chip which uses very little power and is quite cheap.

Although it is pretty fast, it can not compete with something
like e.g. a 60 Mhz pentium.

If you really want to play othello on a chip, you should
design a chip especially for it. In chess, IBM did this;
their program will examine 3 to 4 million positions per second.

For othello you might get a even hiher speed.
