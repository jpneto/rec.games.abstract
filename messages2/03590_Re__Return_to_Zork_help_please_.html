Well, the first suggestion is that you ask on rec.games.int-fiction,
since that's where the adventure game people hang out. :-)

(Yes, you couldn't have known that it stands for "interactive fiction"
and that's the grownup name for adventure games.)

Second, you need to find the secret entrance to the missing half of
the village. Spoilers below:

SPOILERS


Go to Boos Miller's house, and accept his drink, then click the glass on
the plant -- "Empty drink into plant" is an option that you should accept.
Then, show the empty glass to him and he will refill it and go on to
the second part of the drinking game. After the third try, you should
ask him for his keys, and the fourth time he drinks he will pass out,
revealing a trap door. Go down the trap door and use the keys on the
door to find a much bigger part of the map.
