In the Chieftains' version of the game "Destiny" (Stonehenge Rituals),
chieftains bid years of their lives (= the playing currency) to achieve
certain privileges in their game-lives.

Among these privileges the main ones are the doubling in value of the
rmaining years-of-life and deciding the order in which players will play
around the board.

An over ambitious player who bids too much risks losing years of life and
dies prematurely.

In this version of "Destiny" bidding is the essence of the game.
