matt wrote:
> Could someone clarify this query please?
> In the game of Pool, if the cue ball is potted by the opponent, where
> can you place the cue ball, in the D or behind the line?

Since american pool tables lack a "D" (that is a snooker-ism) it'd have to be
behind the line (aka "in the kitchen").

Exceptions exist depending on the variant of pool, however. In 9-ball, it
becomes "ball in hand" anywhere on the table. In 8-ball, it's "in the kitchen".
