Nonsense. I could cross-post this to la.eats, and every time that
someone in rec.games.backgammon responded, then the people in la.eats
would see the article. But this doesn't make it relevant to Los
Angeles restaurants. It's not that people want a confrontation, it's
just that it would be nice if posters could figure out the difference
between multiple posting and cross-posting.

If I separately post an article to ten groups, then responses to that
article remain in the groups in which I post it. If I post a message
about PC games to la.eats, and no one is interested in PC games in
la.eats, then the article will die.

However, if I cross-post the same article to ten groups, even if
discussion dies out in nine of those groups, whenever a person in one
of those groups responds, then that response is posted to all ten
groups.

Therefore, your statement that "if people respond to it, then it's
relevant" is not true. Moreover, your rudeness is completely out of
place in what is, after all, a discussion about games.
