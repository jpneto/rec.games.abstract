Luzia Wrsch from Switzerland won the 7th decentralized World Domino
Championship on Saturday December 29th 2001. 60 players (Germany 24,
Switzerland 20, Norway 11, Estonia 4 and Iceland 1) took part.

2. Ulli Bnkemeyer, Germany
3. Rolf Melcher, Switzerland
4. Florian Klee, Switzerland
5. Daniela Ratzek, Germany
6. Bodil Aas, Norway
7. Christian Grussendorf, Germany
8. Rainer Siedelberg, Germany
9. Anne Neukamm, Germany
10. Ena Mets, Estonia
11. Ole Stein, Germany
12. Gudny Osk Scheving, Iceland
13. Harry Jrgensen, Norway
14. Anita Risl, Norway
15. Stefan Kauschke, Germany
