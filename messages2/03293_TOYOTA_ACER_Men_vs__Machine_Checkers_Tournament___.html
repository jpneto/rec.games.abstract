TOYOTA-ACER Men vs. Machine Checkers Tournament - Round 5 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 27 05 1996 00:00:00 GMT


Four A class players including Jack Francis, a regular and top performer
at the U.S. nationals and who has been awarded a life membership with the
ACF, are taking on the latest version of the Cornell Checkers program in the
TOYOTA-ACER Man vs. Machine Checkers tournament in Bridgetown, Barbados. The
tournament features a round-robin followed by a play-off with the top human.

Round 5 play on Saturday May 25th saw Jeffrey Clarke defeat Theodore Massiah
2-0 while Massiah and Lindsay Worrell drew 0-0.

Round 4 play on Saturday May 18th saw Jack Francis and Cornell Checkers play
to 4 draws. They will meet in the final. Jeffrey Clarke defeated Lindsay
Worrell 1-0 in the other match.

Round 3 on Saturday May 11th saw Cornell Checkers defeat Lindsay Worrell 4-0
and Jack Francis defeat Jeffrey Clarke 1-0.

Round 2 on Saturday May 4th saw Cornell checkers maintain the lead with a 3-1
victory over Theodore Massiah. Jack Francis remained in contention with a 2-1
victory over Lindsay Worrell.

Round 1 on Saturday April 27th saw Cornell Checkers take an early lead with a
2-1 win over Jeffrey Clarke. Jack Francis drew 1-1 with Theodore Massiah.

Final standings after five rounds:
