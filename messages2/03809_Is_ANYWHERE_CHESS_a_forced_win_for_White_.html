The chess variant ANYWHERE CHESS is very simple. Kings
move and capture as in standard chess. Pawns and pieces
capture normally but may move to any empty square on the
board. (Pawns may not move to the 1st rank and must make
a normal move or capture to reach the 8th rank. For example,
White could start the game with:


r n b q k b n r 8
p p p p p p p p 7
. . . N . . . . 6
. . . . . . . . 5
. . . . . . . . 4
. . . . . . . . 3
P P P P P P P P 2
R . B Q K B N R 1

a b c d e f g h

1: b1N-d6 check


Is their a simple forcing line for White? What is it?
