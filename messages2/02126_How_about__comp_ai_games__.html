From: sje@world.std.com (Steven J Edwards)

After following the rec.games.abstract, rec.games.chess, and
rec.games.go newsgroups for some time, I feel that there is a real
need for the creation of a new newsgroup specifically intended for
computer treatment of these and other games. Such a new group would
relieve the forementioned groups of non-recreational comments and
threads. Furthermore, because computer treatment of any one zero sum
adversarial two player games is of general interest to implementors of
any other such game, I propose including them all in a single group.
There are also researchers at sites that do not receive the rec
hierarchy, and so a concentration of these discussions in the comp.ai
tree would allow them access.

The rec.games groups would still be around for popular discussion
about playing computer opponents while the comp.ai.games group would
discuss theory and construction.

There is also the possibility of moderating the proposed group,
something which may not be desirable for any of the rec.games groups.

Perhaps someone familiar with the newsgroup creation process (RFD,
CFV, voting) could assist here. I could assemble a proposed charter
as my contribution.
