Rec.games.abstract is not as inspecific as you might think. Most of
the discussion is about two-player, themeless, strategy games, kind of
like chess or go but not as popular (since those two have their own
newsgroups). There's talk about proving whether certain games are
guaranteed wins for the first player (as Connect Four is) and about
finding mathematicians' theses on various game theory. Talk can get
even more abstract as we wind up chatting about Nomic.

There isn't really a strict set of games that can and can't be
acceptable, but I can't imagine that a lot of folks here would be a
good audience for discussions about Rogue Spear, Rainbow Six, or any
other first-person squad-leading shooter game. You'll probably find
folks more receptive and enjoyable elsewhere in rec.games.* (like
rec.games.computer.*) or perhaps in comp.games.*. Good luck.
