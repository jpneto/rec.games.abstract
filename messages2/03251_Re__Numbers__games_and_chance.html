While working on Go, I had thought about this possibility. The main
problem I came across was that very few games are comparable. So all
this stuff about dominated options and reversible moves becomes
useless. What's worse, there's no group structures, because games
don't have negatives (or if they do, it's hard to picture them adding
to zero). These two problems seem challenging (but VERY worthwhile)
to overcome.

(You may wonder what this has to do with Go. I was interested in
modeling the uncertainty of a position too complicated to analyze by
probability. The eventual goal was to apply the theory to positions
much earlier than the endgame.)
