Balanced doublemove <anything> is a decent variation-- one of the more
popular chess variants is balanced doublemove.

I've seen a number of go variants with multiple moves. Wayne
Schmittberger described (and may have invented) a generic omino go, where
each player places up to four stones in a turn, but they must be
orthogonally connected; late in the game, it becomes necessary to place
few than four stones to get into tight spaces.
