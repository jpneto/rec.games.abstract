Imagine my surprise to see a big stack of Abalone boards in my local
game store! At $30, not cheap. The board is a solid hunk of black
plastic; nubs form guideways so you can slide the marbles. The marbles
are oversized glass (so no other marbles will do & you have to buy
the third color separately.)
