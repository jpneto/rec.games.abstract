Every 4 turns sounds very Diplomacy-like. Risk would be every turn. So

> 4. Unlike Risk, we will not force a territory to have an army. You
> may choose to vacate a territory on a move.

This again feels, umm... Diplomatic (?). Indeed, this is one of the
major strategic considerations in Risk. That calculation of 'am I
strong enough to prevail' has gone awry numerous times because of a
miscount of the territories involved.

> 5. The final sticking point is... Schmittberger's combat system
> seems flawed ... Therefore, I'm looking for a replacement.

I think that you'll really be losing the flavor of Risk if you have a
completely deterministic combat system. The roll of the die becomes an
important tactical factor in long campaigns. Yes, it does all even out
in the end, but I have seen games won/lost because of the dice.

The difficulties with n-way combat are not surprising. There also is a
reason why few (have there been any?) real-world battles are three-way
(and higher) fights. Fundamentally, it's always going to be in
somebody's interest to either form a temporary alliance for the
duration of battle or to wait and attack the (weakened) victor. If you
can playably model this in your system I think you will have done
something cool.
