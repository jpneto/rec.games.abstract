Eric Roush <edroush@acpub.duke.edu> wrote:

I realize that I've come in on this thread late, and this idea may
already have been discussed, but if it were up to me, I'd just use one
card = one hex, thusly.

Yes, this would seem to be an elegant solution. However, a significant
disadvantage of such an approach is that, if a large number of hexes were
desired, a large number of cards would be required, resulting in an
enormous playing area. The cards that Torben and I proposed would result
in a playing area roughly half the size. Also, the one card equals one
hex doesn't place any limitations on what cards may be played adjacently,
which could be made a factor in card play.
