Well, it's that time of year again. Time to flip those double sided
tiddly-winks until one colour dominates all.

Saturday, February 27 ... 9:00 registration, 10:00 first round

This year the othello tournament will be held at 22 Eby St. S. in Kitchener.
Phone (519-579-4219) ahead if you want more information. You may also send

circuit. Brand new this year, we are featuring a beginners' division: so
anybody afraid of the big boys will have a fair chance to climb into the
winner's circle.
