I have two sets of rules.

The ones copyright 1967, 1975 say that the winner is the first player to get
four four in a rows. You call out "score one" on your first and so on
until saying "score four" when you actually win. Listed as an alternative
was playing until the board was full and counting who has the most four in a
rows.

The ones copyright 1967 say that the winner is the first one to get four
in a row and claim it. This is how I have always played. I even wrote
a program in Univac 1100 assembly language to play the game (poorly)
for a class. This set also lists a way to play until all beads have been
placed, but different four in a rows are weighted differently. You add
1-4 points for each bead in a four in a row. The points are decreased
by one for each level up the bead is. (Beads on the bottom are worth 4.
Beads on the top are worth 1.)
