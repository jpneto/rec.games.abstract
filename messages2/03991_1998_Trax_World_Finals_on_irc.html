1998 Trax World Finals on irc

From: mel@soda.CSUA.Berkeley.EDU (Mel Nicholson)
Date: 06 11 1998 00:00:00 GMT

This Saturday at approximately 1:30pm Pacific, the final match of the
1998 World Trax finals will begin. The game will be played live in
California, and the moves will be broadcast via the #trax irc channel
and gamerz.net.

As usual, the #gallery channel will be available for pundits and
pontificators to second guess and analyze. We ask that comments
be kept on this second channel so the board-ascii drawings on
#trax are not cluttered.

For more information on the game trax, try

http://ia-spare.massey.ac.nz/trax
