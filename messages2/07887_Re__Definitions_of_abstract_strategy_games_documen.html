There are ways, even with heavily unbalanced sides, to be able to make
things fair, the top way is having people play both sides. For a game
with randomized openings, this would be a preferred approach also.

More thought is needed and a lot more work needs to be done. What is
likely to happen now is that a current tournaments going on for modern
abstracts can be linked together under the IAGO banner to get the name
out and run a "tour" of these games throughout the year, with points
being awarded for how well you do. At the end of the year, top points
leader is the champion for that year. Future year, last year's
champion could defend his/her title.

There will need to be a standards committee also formed also to get
things to happen at this point to.
