I don't know of anything about the game per se, but I saw an article in an
early *Jeux et Strategie* about a variation: allow leaps of any length
with a single leaped piece at midpoint. For example, in the sequence of
holes ABCDEFG a piece on A could leap a piece on D and go to G if BCEF
were empty, or B to F over D if C and E were empty. This variation has
been mentioned once or twice in r.g.abstract and there seems to be general
agreement that it significantly improves the game.
