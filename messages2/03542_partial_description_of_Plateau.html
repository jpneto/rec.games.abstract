-- Since I endorsed Plateau, it was suggested that I describe the game.

Plateau is played on a 4x4 board with 12 pieces per player; the
pieces are are essentially checkers with various combinations of
colored dots. The goal is to either build a stack of six of your
pieces, or capture a total of six opponent pieces. There are no
dice or other randomizing devices, but some of the pieces are colored
differently on each side, so their complete identity is not immediately
apparent. This hidden information greatly complicates straightforward
calculation, and therefore moves emphasis in the game toward strategy.

Types of moves:

- play-in a new piece (from the set of 12 to the board)
or
- chess-like move of a current stack, with pick-up and drop-off options
to build stacks, reveal new pieces, pin or capture opponent pieces.
or
- prisoner exchange

The net effect is that there are a very large number of possible moves,
built from a few simple rules. The emergent behavior is quite
complex.

The free game download does a very good job of illustrating the rules
using animated examples, can be played against or used to play
email games. http://iquest.com/~jwalbea/plateau.shtml
