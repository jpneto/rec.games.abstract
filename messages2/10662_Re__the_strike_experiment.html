White has a group of 6 and black has a single stone which is 4 spaces
away. Normally this would be too short of a distance to strike the
white group for black.
WIth the new rule black's other pieces come into play. If where black
lands is also next to a group of 2 other black stones or if it's next
to 2 single black stones then the strike value would become exactly 6,
thus giving black a chance to strike from a closer range.

The non-striking black stones which increase the strike values are
somewhat like helpers, although it can have a hindering effect if the
desired strike value is lower than can be made.
