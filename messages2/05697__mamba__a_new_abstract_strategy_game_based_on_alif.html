I'm the author of a new abstract strategy game mamba. I've seen a
message from Sacha about the game... just few things to add, yes the
game is based on alife aspects (ie the pieces are alive) and yes the
concept is more similar to Go than chess. But one important thing, is
that this game is a king of dynamic system to play with so after any
action we bring the balance back on the board... we discover very funny
tactics for the game... a kind of guerilla strategy is the better way to
play for the moment.

You can play online at http://www.mambagame.com

We have organize few tournaments... and we will continue to do so.
