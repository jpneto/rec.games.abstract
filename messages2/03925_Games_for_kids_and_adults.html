Games for kids and adults

From: Kristin Matherly <kmatherly@mindspring.com>
Date: 17 07 1998 00:00:00 GMT

I'm spending a significant amount of time this summer watching some
young relatives of mine and was looking for good activities,
particularly games.

I enjoy playing games and so have a lot of them, but most don't seem to
work well for this range of ages (6-12), especially games that I like
and find interesting.

The only game I've found so far that works is a card game called
Aquarius. I'd played it a lot with friends and just decided one day to
see if the kids would like it. I was sort of surprised at how quickly
they took to it. They even beat me at frequently. The artwork is really
colorful and interesting looking, which gets their attention to begin
with I think, but then they seem to like the mix of luck and strategy.
(I may never have to play Go Fish again, Yeaahhh!)

So anyway, I'm looking for other games like this. It should be something

that can keep the attention of kids (and adults) of a wide variety of
ages, something that has some luck involved but also some skill, it's
nice if they have to think a bit. It would help if it were easily
portable like Aquarius, but I suppose it's not absolutely necessary. The

games should also not last too long. Their attention seems to wander if
it takes too long to determine a winner.

Any ideas? Thanks in advance for your suggestions.

Kristin
