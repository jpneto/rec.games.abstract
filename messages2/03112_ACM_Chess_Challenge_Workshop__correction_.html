*********FINAL REMINDER**********
*********CALL FOR PAPERS*********
*******TO BE PRESENTED AT********

THE ACM COMPUTER CHESS CHALLENGE, February 10-17, 1996.
Location: Philadelphia Convention Center

A six game match between Garry Kasparov and Deep Blue will be held
that week. Five hundred thousand dollars in prizes are be awarded.
The event is being organized by the ACM Computer Chess Committee
under the auspicies of the ICCA. Mike Valvo will serve as Umpire.
The event is part of the ACM's Computing Week Festival which will
celebrate the 50th birthday of the modern electronic computer,
designed at the University of Pennsylvania.

For further information, contact Terrie Phoenix at the ACM
(212-869-7440 or phoenix@acm.org) or Monty Newborn
(514-398-7079 or newborn@cs.mcgill.ca)
-----------------------------------
In conjunction with the ACM Computer Chess Challenge, the Computer
Science Conference will hold a workshop on "Recent Advances in
Computer Chess." Papers are being solicited for presentation (Feb. 16)
(25 minutes) and publication in the Conference Proceedings.
Tony Marsland, ICCA president, will serve as Workshop Moderator.

Papers should be approximately ten pages in length. They must
be submitted to Monty Newborn by October 28th. 1995. A tight reviewing
schedule will yield decisions by November 6 and final, possibly,
revised copies of the paper are due by November 15th!

Papers can be sent to:

Monty Newborn, School of Computer Science, McGill University
3480 University Street, Montreal, Quebec, CANADA H3A 2A7.

Electronic copies can be sent to: newborn@cs.mcgill.ca
