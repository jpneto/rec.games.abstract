Hey There Guys (and Gals)!

I have a game for you to try!

I don't think this game has a title, but it appears to be non-trivial. I saw
it in an ancient issue of Science (Mathematical Games). We've been having a
blast playing it. It requires a checker board and checkers.

Setup:

X X X X X X
O O
O O
O O
O O
O O
O O
X X X X X X

Objective:
To have all your pieces in one contiguous group: i.e. for any of your men
there is a unbroken chain of vertically, horizontally, or diagonally connected
friends to all other friends on the board. Thus, if you only have one piece
left, you win. It's more fun to warn opponents of checks and checkmates.

Movement:
Players alternate turns, moving one piece at a time.
Choose one of the four directions of movement (n/s, e/w, ne/sw, se/nw).
Count all pieces in that row (including enemies and yourself). You must move
EXACTLY that many squares (no less, no more). Note how far you must move
depends on the direction you choose.

Restrictions:
You may NOT jump enemy pieces. You may not land on friendly pieces. You may
not move off the board.

Benfits:
You MAY jump friendly pieces. You may land on and remove enemy pieces from
play.

------------------------------------------------------------

Examples:
--------
Consider this row:

|X . . O . O . X|

All pieces must move 4 spaces in this direction. Neither X may move: they
would be forced to either move off the board or jump an O, both of which are
illegal. Both O's can move. The rightmost O can move to the left to a
vacant spot, jumping the friendly piece, but it cannot move to the right.
The lefttmost O can move to the right, jumping its friend and capturing the
X, but it can't move to the left.


Consider this formation:

O . . X
. O . X
. O O .

X is in check, and is attempting to prevent O from forming a group. The
upper X can move sw to capture the O, but it does no good as the O group is
still connected. The southern X, however, may move west, capture and
disconnect the O group.

Hope you like it as much as we did! Feel free to email or post questions,
comments, or strategies!
