I have just reread "The Shockwave Rider" by John Brunner, and was
reminded of the game of fencing described therein. I am trying to find
out if anyone has any experience of playing this game.

Summary description:

Played on a rectilinear grid of 101 by 71 points. On their turn each
player selects two points, one openly, one concealed. After the fifth
turn a player may, instead of choosing a concealed point, request
their opponent to reveal their concealed pieces and then attempt toi
form a non-degenerate triangle, with vertices at three of their own
revbealed points, and containing none of their opponents (now all
revealed) points. If they can do so they score the number of vertices
strictly within the triangle, if they cannot then they must reveal all
of their own concealed points, leaving their opponent in a very strong
position for his/her next move.
