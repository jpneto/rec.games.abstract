The variation I've seen in multiple places involves playing
on a 5x5x5, with 2 extra pawns and 2 unicorns.

The initial setup has white at the "bottom front" and black
at the "top back". The setup on level I of the white pieces is

RNKNR
PPPPP

And the level II setup is

BUQBU
PPPPP

The moves are as expected. White pawns can attack up-left, up-right,
and up-forward, as well as left-forward and right-forward.

Unicorns move similar to bishops except in all three dimensions
(whereas bishops are limited to 2-d moves, and queens are limited
to 1-d or 2-d moves).
