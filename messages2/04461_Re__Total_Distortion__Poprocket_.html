Abstract games are typcially games that have a few relatively simple rules
and (for good ones anyway) from which the strategies of the game are usually
not immediately obvious even to the designer. The resulting game play (for
the good ones) is beyond what the designer could have imagined. What's
interesting about abstract games is discovering strategies.

Though I've never heard of the game you mention, video adventure games are
bascially puzzles designed by the designer. The game does not exceed what
the designer intended. What's interesting about video adventure games is
discovering what the designer placed there for you to discover.

That's my take on it.

of newsgroups, the most appropriate to your game might be
rec.games.int-fiction or alt.games.myst (assuming your game is in the same
genre as myst) or alt.games.riven (assuming ...).
