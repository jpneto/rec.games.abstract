The Chinese call it "Shi-yang-chi."
"Shi-yang" literally means "west ocean" (The Atlantic Ocean is called the
"Da-shi-yang," or "big west ocean") and means "Western."
"Chi" means a game played with pieces on a board, or the pieces themselves:
"Wei-chi": "Surround-chi" (Go)
"Shiang-chi": "Elephant-chi" (Chinese chess)
"Wuu-ts-chi": "Five-thing-chi" (Go-moku)
"Tyau-chi": "Jumping-chi" ("Chinese" checkers)
"Shi-yang-tyau-chi": "Western-jumping-chi" (Checkers)
