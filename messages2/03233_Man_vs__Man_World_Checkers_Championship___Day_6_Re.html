Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship - Day 6 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 25 03 1996 00:00:00 GMT

Don Lafferty has a 4-2 lead over Ron King after 24 games of the 1996 Man
vs. Man World Checkers Championship being played in Bridgetown, Barbados.
Lafferty won two of the four games played on Saturday. Sunday is a rest
day and the match continues on Monday March 25. These updates have been
provided by Hallam Hope from Bridgetown.

More information may be found in http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/~chinook.
