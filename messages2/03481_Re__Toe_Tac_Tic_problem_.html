An interesting variation of the 4x4x4 game that I used to play is a
4x4x4x4 game. Yest that is right, up it to four dimenional. The play
board would be something like this

11 12 13 14
21 22 23 24
31 32 33 34
41 42 43 44

Each number represents a 4x4 grid. Each column would represent a
verticle tier, and each row a moment in time. I found the easiest way
to win was playing diagionally through time, the most difficult to
visualize. An odd thing that came out of this was that all four
diminsions are interchangable, so it helped me to visualize time as a
fourth diminsion much easier in physics problems.
