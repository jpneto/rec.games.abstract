Hi all:

I was browsing thru a catalog, and I came upon a new-to-me game.
It could be interesting, so I'll describe it here:

Play is on a 4x4 grid of squares. Each player has a supply of
pieces, black on one side, white on the other, like Othello.
The goal of the game is to make a 4-in-a-row of either color.
The 4-in-a-row can be on a diagonal.

Each turn, a player may either place a new piece anywhere, or
flip an existing piece. I think there must be some rule to avoid
perpetual flip... perhaps no piece may be reflipped until a new
piece is added.

Has anyone seen this?
