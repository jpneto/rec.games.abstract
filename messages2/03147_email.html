]
] I found the following game in the "Gamut of Games":
]
] Name: Call it
] Players: 2
] Equipment: one can and 2 sets of 10 chips numbered from 1 to 10.
] Rules: The two players agree on a number first (supposedly between 1
] and 20). Players put a chip into the can alternatively.
] If any player believes that the sum of chips in the can is
] equal to the agreed number, he call the number and stops the
] game. The absolute difference between the agreed number and
] actual sum in the can is added to the player who called it.
] Win: Player who has the min. accumulated penalty wins.
]

There are some problems with this game. First as stated there is no penalty
for not guessing so a perfect strategy for the game is to never stop the
game. Second there are some race conditions that could cause problems
playing the game. What happens when both players try to stop the game at
the same time? What happens when both players are waiting to see if the
other player is going to stop the game before stopping the game themselves?
