Hey everyone!
These games are not very complicated, but my friends and I have wasted
hours upon hours playing them :)
1) The movie game-- This has two versions. In the first one, someone picks two
actors/actresses and tries to connect them using movies that they have been in
with other actors. Example: Connect Mel Gibson to Gary Sinise. Mel Gibson
was in Lethal Weapon with Danny Glover. Danny Glover was in Predator 2 with
Bill Paxton. Bill Paxton was in Apollo 13 with Gary Sinise. Easy :)
The second version of the movie game goes like this. The first player names
a movie. The second player hasd to name an actor/actress in that movie. The
next player has to name a movie besides the one previously said that the actor
was in. And so on and so on. When one player cannot name an actor or a movie,
they can either guess or challenge. The player challenges the player before
him that gave him the movie or actor. They must name a movie or actor or
they get an out. Three outs and you're out. A sample game w/3 players:
Player 1: Ghostbusters
Player 2: Bill Murry
Player 3: Groundhog's day
Player 1: (doesn't know) challenge
At this point, player 3 must name an actor/actress in Groundhog's day or get
an out. Movies cannot be repeated twice in the same game, but actors can.

2)The word game. (you like these fancy names, huh? :) )
Player one will name a word, for example, fire. Going in a circle, players
have to name a word or phrase containing the word fire. Ex: firehouse,
firehose, light my fire, etc. Players have ten seconds each turn or they get
an out. When no players can think of a word/phrase, the round ends. Three
outs and you're out.

have fun guys, keep the games coming :)
