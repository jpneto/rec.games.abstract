You may already know that the Mind Sports Olympiad is taking place at
Loughborough University in the UK from the 14th-18th August. We are
organising the TRAX UK open tournament which will be held there on
Sunday 18th starting at 9am, entry fee 14 (7 concessions). There is
a prize of 100 for the winner. Runner-up will receive Trax
merchandise (eg. Tiles, a book or the Trax computer game.)

Prior to that Trax will be available for beginners to play and learn
the game from the 14th-17th with a beginner's tournament to be held
during the evening of Saturday 17th - starting at 6.30pm, entry fee
10 (5 concessions) which includes a Trax set to take away. There is
also a prize of 20 for the winner.

More information about the MSO is available at the MSO website
http://www.msoworld.com/ but if you have any questions specifically
about the TRAX tournaments, please contact me at dave@games-web.co.uk.
