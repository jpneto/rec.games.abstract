For those not in the know, Progressive Chess is a very simple concept:-

The rules are as in ordinary chess, except that white starts with one move,
then black plays 2 moves, then white 3, black 4, and so on. Check may not
be given except as the last move of a sequence, and must be relieved by
the first move of the next sequence. So checkmate is as usual.

Simple! But hot! (There are more details of course, but the full rules
will be presented in an accompanying post.)

2. f6 Nc6 (prevents that, & guards against a 3-move Q capture)
