The program asks you to think of a number, double it, add 16,
divide by 2 and then subtract your original number.

In algebraic terms, X is your number:

(2X+16)/2-X = 2X/2 - 16/2 -X = X-X + 8 = 8

when you doubled your number then divided by two and then
subtracted your number, you essentially eliminated your number
from the equation, so it doesn't matter what number you started
with.
