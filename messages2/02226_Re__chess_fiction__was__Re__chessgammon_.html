First, I would like to second the endorsement of the collection of short
sci-fi chess stories, _Pawn_To_Infinity._ Second, for a more serious
chess-related novel, look up _The_Tower_Struck_By_Lightning._ I believe
this was originally written in Spanish, and that the author was a grandmaster.
If anyone is interested, there is a Hollywood thriller called "Night Moves,"
which is tangentially related to chess.
