Chesschat is an email discussion list. If you are interested in
discussing chess with fellow chess enthusiasts, check out Artful
Dodger's ChessChat.

To subscribe go to http://www.hctc.com/~dtriplet and follow
thedirections given there. Or write to
ChessChat-subscribe@onelist.com


ChessChat is currently forming discussion groups and studying
"Reassess Your Chess" "My System" and "Zurich 1953" There is also
plenty of general discussion as well. Join the discussion of your
choice. Members can choose from individual messages to daily digest
subscriptions. Just a love of the game.
