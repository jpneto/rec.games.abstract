Monopoly is decidedly non-random. The two sides of the board between
Jail and Go To Jail (maroon, orange, red, and yellow) are visited far
more than the other two (green, purple, brown, blue). This is one
reason why the oranges (whose names I've completely forgotten) are a
far better business proposition than the greens (Regent Street, Oxford
Street, and Bond Street). If I remember rightly the reasons that the
second red property (I think its Trafalgar Square or The Strand) is
most visited are that: (i) it is in one of the most visisted areas of
the board, and (ii) there is a card which sends you directly to that
square.
