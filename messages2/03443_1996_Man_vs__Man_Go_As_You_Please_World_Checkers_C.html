1996 Man vs. Man Go-As-You-Please World Checkers Championship - Day 1

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 02 11 1996 00:00:00 GMT


The 1996 Man vs. Man Go-As-You-Please World Checkers Championship is being
held from November 1 to 7 at the International Checker Hall of Fame in Petal,
Mississippi. This 24 game match is between the defending world champion,
Ron King from Barbados, and challenger Jim Morrison, from the United States.
Don Lafferty, the current United States Checkers Champion, is the referee.

After two games, the match stands even at two draws. Four more games will
be played on Saturday November 2.

More information about this match (including complete game scores) may be
found either on the Chinook home page (http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/~chinook)
or the American Checker Federation home page (http://www.primenet.com/~krow).

This update has been provided courtesy of Bill Vilberg from the University
of Southern Mississippi.
