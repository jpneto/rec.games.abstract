Truly an awful game, even from Scarne's repertoire. A friend and I did
a state-space analysis of it a year or so back, and for the standard
opening (i.e. without pointing) it is in fact a draw.

'Advanced Teeko' - a variant where any orthogonal square is a winning
combination - is a first player win with the standard opening.

The book gives little insight into the game, but a great deal into
John Scarne.
