Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship - Day 9 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 28 03 1996 00:00:00 GMT

Don Lafferty leads 5-4 over Ron King after 36 games of the 1996 Man vs. Man
World Checkers Championship being played in Bridgetown, Barbados. King won
the 36th game early Thursday morning after refusing to grant Lafferty a time-
out at the end of game 34 for health reasons.

The first two games (33 and 34) were White Dyke or early Maid of the Mill
openers, with the second lasting a marathon four-plus hours. King at this
stage refused a request by Lafferty for the time-out which would have pushed
the other games into Thursday. With the match well ahead of schedule it
could have gone into Friday if necessary.

King stated at the break (4-2 down after 34 games) that his strategy would be
to wear down Lafferty, in short keep him at the board unnecessarily, and hope
to capitalise on a tiring opponent. Although King is technically right, this
has raised other questions, such as Lafferty's right to a timeout for health
reasons. In fact the schedule had been structured to handle such an
eventuality.

As it was, the third game (35th) was drawn after 36 moves, a normal Cross
opening (11-15, 23-18, 10-14). The last game of 41 moves went over two hours
and one analyst said Lafferty had three ways to win but lost, having been at
the table for virtually an entire day. Thursday is definitely the last day
with the final four games scheduled.

Despite his failing health - Lafferty has lung cancer - and apparent fatigue
Lafferty has shown tremendous courage and has belied the pundits with his
strong defence and ability to capitalise on errors.
