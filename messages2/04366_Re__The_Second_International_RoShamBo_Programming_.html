>Incorrect analysis. Because the other AIs are trying to seek patterns, they

>contest is in heats it would be even easier, you'd just need log2 n victories,

Scoring is based on how well your entry does against other entries and
a few sample entries. In other words, an entry that plays random choices
wouldn't score enough points to win. In any case, playing random choices
is not an optimal strategy against opponents that are playing non-optimally.
E.g. if the opponent plays just rock, then the optimal strategy is to play
paper all the time. Random choices is optimal if you don't have any a priori
information or don't consider your opponent's previous choices however that's
already known. The challenge is to create an entry that uses the opponent's
performance to predict what the opponent will choose in the next round.
