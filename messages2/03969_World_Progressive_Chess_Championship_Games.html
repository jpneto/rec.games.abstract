The World Internet Progressive Chess Championship
(WIPCC '98) began on July 20, 1998! Progressive chess is
the exciting chess variant where white gets 1 move,
black 2, white 3, black 4, and so on. Games can be
brutally fast, but there's as much chance for brilliance
in progressive chess as in regular chess!

Games are being played by email right now... The
preliminary round is underway. The top 2 players
from each of the 8 sections will advance to the
semifinals!

Follow the championship at
http://www.cs.utk.edu/~hyatt/wipcc98/
