The rules to the smaller version of Hnefatafl are also in
R.C. Bell's book: Board and table games from many civilizations,
published by Dover Paperbacks (but out of print now?). It has been
a while since I played it, but I liked it.

More recently I have seen a replica of the game, being distributed by
Past Times in the UK. This version looks nicely produced, with a
cloth board and resin peices looking similar to the pawns from the
Lewis chess set. The price is around 20 pounds. Contact me by email
if further information is required.
