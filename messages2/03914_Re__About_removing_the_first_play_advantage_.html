Well, the only "5 in a row" game I know of is Gomoku. It is
well known that the first player has a forced win in Gomoku.
The "equalized" version of Gomoku is Renju. I refer you to

http://www.gamerz.net/pbmserv/renju.rules.html

for the rules to Renju. Basically, Renju uses 3-move equalization,
which is also referred to as the "cake" rule elsewhere in this
thread. But there are further complications; The first player in
Renju must face many restrictions for the entire game regarding
what sort of patterns are permitted. The second player faces no
such restrictions. Also, the opening ritual is more complicated
than 3-meq usually is.

For connect four, I don't know for sure, but I suspect even 3-meq
would not be enough; any position with 3 tokens or less is probably
a win for one side or the other. From a practical standpoint, that
may not matter, though...

Several of the games on Richard's PBeM server have 1-move eq
implemented. After the first move is made, the second player has
the option of swapping sides. This is good enough to keep the
whiners (like myself) from making a fuss.
