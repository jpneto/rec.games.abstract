Here's a new (?) abstract gambling game I've come up with:

In a room of 100 people, 10 are picked as "cardholders". Everyone knows who the 10 are. One of the 10 is "It", and knows
this fact. The other 9 know that they are not It.

Now, the room of 100 (including the cardholders and It) can bet, horseraceing style, on who is It. Cardholders get 1% of
the money bet on them. ANY SORT of insider information, kickbacks, secret signals, lying, rumormongering, etc. IS
allowed. Bets may be withdrawn after being made. Money bet on each cardholder is updated instantly and publicly shown.
Betting is closed at a random time after which no other bets may be made or withdrawn.

Payoffs are made as in horseraceing.

Tell me what you think of It!
