Victor Allis claims in his dissertation

ftp://ftp.cs.vu.nl/pub/victor/gomoku.ps.Z

that he has solved the game of Gomoku (i.e. "free play Renju). He writes
that Goro Sakata and Wataru Ikawa have stated that Black has an "assured"
win, but didn't "proof" it or "substantiated" it by a "tree (of variations)".

I have now the book of Sakata and Ikawa just about 10 inchs in front of me
and do you know what I see?

1rst: These authors literally claim a "sure" win for Black.
2nd: They substantiate their claim on 32 pages containing 116 diagrams.
3rd: These diagrams show a tree of variations.

BTW it can be assumed, as this is a book for beginners, that Japanese
Renju literature about Renju Josekis give even more details proofing that
Gomoku is a win for Black.
