It has recently occured to me that the game might be playable
if the players bid down the colors, like in "I can name that tune
in # notes." That is, you could get a map of the U.S., and then
say "I can color that map with 7 colors." (i.e. offering to be Minimizer
with 7 crayons). "I can color that map with 5 colors" "O.K. -- Color that
map!" Or you could make it like the fair cake-cutting procedure.
One person could pick a number of crayons, and then the other player
could choose whether to be Max or Min.
