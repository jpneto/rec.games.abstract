About ItsYourTurn.com:

http://www.itsyourturn.com is a turn-based gaming web site, where you enter
moves when it's convenient for you. Turn-based gaming is similar to playing
by email, but instead of emailing text moves, you enter your moves via your
browser (point and click on the pieces). You can move as quickly or slowly as
you like-- the game stays on the server forever. You will be notified by
email when it's your turn to move. We currently support chess, checkers,
back- gammon, reversi, Battleship, Connect4, Go-moku, and several variations
thereof.

Our "Invite a friend" feature lets you invite any registered player on the
site into your game. Sign on with a friend and create a game between the
two of you. Remember, you don't have to be logged on at the same time to
play a game. And really, who has time these days to meet at a real-time
game site?

ItsYourTurn.com allows you to play whenever you have time, over weeks or
months. It also lets you play several games at once without having to mess
around with a board or chess software. No Java or downloads are required to
use the site, and it's WebTV compatible. All games are accessible and viewable
forever, even after you finish the game.

Put your name in the waiting room, and you'll have a game in no time.
Over 15,000 players have already signed up, and they're all waiting
to crush you.
