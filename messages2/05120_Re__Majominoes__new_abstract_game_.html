In a Hex variant I posted some time ago, the players alternate making double
moves but the two stones cannot be adjacent. I exhaustively examined boards
up to 6x6 I believe, and one or the other player had a win (of course) but
I could see no pattern. I called the game Scorpio due to the tactical
combinations
-- pincer-like or rapid extension (tail) -- seen during playtesting.

In the context of Majominoes, to add complexity I would suggest the
double-move
cannot be made in the same row, column, or region. Alternatively, to weaken
O's double-move why not have it occur at the very end -- O gets the extra
stone
but not the tempo.
