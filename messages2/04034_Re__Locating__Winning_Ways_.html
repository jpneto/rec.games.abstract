Claude Chaunier wrote:
> As a broad natural follow-up to these three books, with the same
> spirit and theory, and rich texts by John Conway, Richard Guy,
> Jonathan Schaeffer, Noam Elkies, Elwyn Berlekamp, Aviezri Fraenkel,
> David Wolfe and others, I warmly recommand Richard J. Nowakowski (ed.):
> "Games of No Chance"
> Mathematical Sciences Research Institute Publications, Volume 29, 1996,
> Cambridge University Press, ISBN 0-521-57411-0 (Hardback), 537 pages.

I'll second the recommendation, with the caveat that "No Chance" doesn't
cover much of the basic theory, except for two short surveys by Richard
Guy. Of the combinatorial games books in print, Berlekamp and Wolfe's
"Mathematical Go" is a better introduction.

Cambridge recently released a paperback version [ISBN 0-521-64652-9],
which I've seen in several bookstores for less than $30. You can
probably get it from some online bookstore for $20. (The hardback
edition costs about $50.)

There is also an older survey book called simply "Combinatorial Games",
published in 1991 by AMS Press [ISBN 0-8218-0166-X]. About half of the
papers in that book were revised and reprinted in "No Chance".

It's (long past) time for a new edition of Winning Ways!!
