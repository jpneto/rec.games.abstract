Rock, Paper, Scissors is pretty the same as rolling dice. People know how
to play the game so as to guaranty a tie on average. (Just roll dice to
see what to do.) If BOTH players want to try to engage in a meta game of
who can outguess the other, then they can aggree to do so. But either one
can opt out by choosing randomly. The pattern matching aspects of the meta
game are interesting and has uses outside of playing Rock, Paper, Scissors.

And even more complicated simove games have optimal strategies where players
should randomize their moves to have the largest expected value, no matter
what strategy their opponent uses.
