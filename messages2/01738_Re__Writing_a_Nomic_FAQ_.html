Hi Garth.

Nomic from VU is still creeping along.

RE MAO: As I know it, it's sort of a drinking game with cards. Well, it
works better as a drinking game anyway. Play is much like uno. Cards have
special play characteristics as defined by the game's leader or "Mao
Master". The object of the game is to get rid of all of your cards.

Example of a Mao rule: "On the play of a 9, the person who played it shall
say, in a loud voice, 'I am a great grey slug'. To which the Mao players
shall say, 'Yes, you are!'"

More confusing example: "On the play of a 6, play shall continue in the
direction that it was going for one turn and then reverse."
(Sound simple? Play two 6's in a row and watch the confused stares.)

I could probably get Fred Renken to send you a more complete list of the
"initial set" if you like.
