Okay, I suppose you could play a game based on the description of most
of those games, but I find that all too frequently crucial details are
left out, such as what the initial board set up is, how to handle
certain situations such as non-terminating games, etc. When I download
the file, I often find that these details are left out there as well,
so you would probably do just as well by reading the web page.

It's easy to make up the rules to a new abstract game. Making up the
