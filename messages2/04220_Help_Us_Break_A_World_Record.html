Help Us Break The World Record.

Australia's First International Live Net Streaming Broadcast
plans to set new benchmark
while supporting
Children's Cancer Institute Australia


Streaming Media Australia will broadcast live over the Internet CAROLS IN THE STADIUM on the 19th December at 7:30pm Australian Eastern Summer Time at - www.carols.com.au
The Global Internet Community will be encouraged to log on and attempt to record the highest number of Simultaneous Streams broadcast over the Net.
A full production crew of six cameras will make this an amazing Net experience for all viewers. A special production unit has been set up for this event. With emphasis on net friendly coverage it will make it possible for viewers to clearly view the event at it's best.
Carols in The Stadium will mark the first time that the power of the Internet, will be channelled to such a humanitarian use in Australia, and the energy and impact of world class local artists coming together for such a worthy cause
Streaming Media Australia will utilise Windows Media Technologies, which sets the new standard for audio and video quality on the Internet.
"The Windows Media platform is about enabling new opportunities," said Microsoft Spokesperson Frank Arrigo. "We're excited that Streaming Media Australia is using Windows Media to deliver a better experience for their customers."
Current Benchmark
A recent episode of the Drew Carey show was streamed over the Internet sending out over 650,000 streams. The Carols Event organisers, Ace Global Enterprises, are attempting to set a new benchmark for the number of simultaneous streams during the Carols concert.
Charity: Children's Cancer Institute of Australia
Tax deductible donations via credit card will be taken from the event website leading up to and during the event in aid of the Children's Cancer Institute of Australia. Any amount over $2.00 dollars will be welcomed; receipts of payment will be supplied for all donations.
Secure transaction services have been donated by Web Pay Australia in support for the charity theme of the event.
