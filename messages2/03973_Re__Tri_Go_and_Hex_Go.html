There's a game called Rings (available for play on Richard's PBeM Server)
which is built around this mechanic. The board is a radius 6 hexagon of
hexagons, pieces played on corners. A capture is made by having more of
your pieces in a particular hex than there are defenders (with blank
spaces counting as defenders); if you get four pieces on a hex, it's
yours. When you capture a hex, all of the spaces (or pieces) there are
converted to your pieces, and those pieces can never be captured; this is
called a ring. If you have a ring adjacent to a hex, that counts as one
"influence" in that hex, making it easier to capture. The game is over
when no more moves can be made (or all players pass), and the winner is
the player who has the greatest amount of influence in the most hexes.

I've played a few games, and it either goes slowly, with each player
working on their own territory and gradually coming into contact, or
wuickly, with players making moves right next to each other, until one
player makes a critical move which chain-reaction captures most of the
board. I'm playing a 6-player game now, which may be finished in less
than 6 months.
