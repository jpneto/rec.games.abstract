GIPF COMPETITION IN ANTWERPEN, BELGIUM

GIPF is not only played on the internet. You can also play it "life"
face-to-face.
Every first sunday of the month a GIPF competition is held in Antwerpen,
Belgium.
The tournament is held at Stoofstraat 4, near the river "De Schelde"
It's starts 13.30, be there 13:15.
Everybody, from beginner to olympic champion(he is there too) is welcome
!
GIPF is a great strategic game. Visit the GIPF site for more
information.
