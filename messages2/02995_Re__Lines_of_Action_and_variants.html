Here's a couple of games I played with Philip Cohen, LOA master.
Philip runs the Chess Variant and Non-chess games sections of
the PBM club NOST.

(The standard way to write a capture is like this: "d8:e8")
(A "+" is a threat to connect. Multiple "+"s are multiple threats.)
