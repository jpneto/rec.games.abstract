Hmmm. "Rendevous With Rama" was a good sci-fi novel, but though it
offers great scope for high-tech special effects, it seems an unlikely
candidate for a Hollywood movie. Even more than with "2001: A Space
Odyssey," there's no plot per se, no good guys or bad guys, no central
conflict with a dramatic resolution. Instead it's a team of scientists
tackling a series of scientific questions and mysteries, only some of
which are ever answered. I'd hate to see Fincher ruin it by, say,
adding a monster or gang of villainous aliens.
