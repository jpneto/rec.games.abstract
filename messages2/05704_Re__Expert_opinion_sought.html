You probably didn't understand what abstract means. This group is for
discussion of abstract board games, and computer implementations there-of.
An abstract board game is a game involving little or no element of chance,
typically, but not necessarily for 2 players Such games include chess, go,
othello etc., but the 2 first games have specific newsgroups devoted to
them.

Try rec.games.computer, or possibly rec.puzzles, although i think they would
redirect this post somewhere else too.
