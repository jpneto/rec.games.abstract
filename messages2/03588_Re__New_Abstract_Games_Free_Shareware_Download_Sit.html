And that's not all (grin). We invite abstract game inventors to submit
their game rules to us for consideration. If accepted we will program it
up as a DOS shareware Tommy's Toy (TM) and release it to the general
public, with credit given including a web site url. The game must not be
patented, and we will pay no royalties, however, thousands might end up
playing and enjoying it that never would have otherwise. We will use
your name for the game if you own the trademark and grant us permission
via email, else we will make up another name and just reference your name
in the docs If you want to sell physical (boxed) versions of your game
then this shareware simulation of the game can stimulate business for
you.

Remember our software products are all 5 bucks each for the full
non-shareware version, and are thus NOT packed with tons of extra fancy
graphics and sounds, are NOT optimized for speed at the bit-level, and
will NOT necessarily beat the world champ (grin). They will be worth
what people pay for them though, and more... Earthlings love them!

And do visit! We just released Tommy's Binaryon <tm>, a cool 2-player
abstract board game for people who like binary math. Play against the PC
or another human at the same keyboard or via email by passing save files.
Download it from our web site at http://members.aol.com/TOMTOYS/
