Buying _Encyc. of Chess Variants_ in USA

From: rwhe@ludism.org (Ron Hale-Evans)
Date: 16 Feb 2001 00:04:56 -0800

I live in the US, and tried to order _The Encyclopedia of Chess
Variants_ by David Pritchard a couple of months ago from W.H. Smith
<http://www.bookshop.co.uk/>. Their web page said it would take about
two weeks for them to order it from the publisher. Three weeks or so
later I wrote to ask what was going on. They said the publisher was
out of stock, and it would be several more weeks before they could
send out my order. (They never changed the ETA on their web page.)
Contacting the publisher directly, OTOH, was a nuisance; Games &
Puzzles Publications takes credit cards, but they have no phone or
email. And so on.

Finally I did a web search to locate someone who sells the book in the
US. It turns out American Postal Chess Tournaments does
<http://correspondencechess.com/apct/>. I placed a phone order late on
a Wednesday and received the book Saturday. It was significantly
discounted, too.

The guy I spoke to at APCT says he has only 3 or 4 copies left, and
he's selling the business soon. However, if the APCT supply dries up,
try doing a web search for "Globe Pequot Press"; my copy came with a
sticker saying they distribute the book.

I have no connection with David Pritchard or any of the commercial
entities mentioned in this article, except as a satisfied or
unsatisfied customer, as the case may be.
