There are also very important differences between the problems
associated with Linear A and Khipus.

We know next to nothing about the language of the Minoans (except that
it wasn't an indo-European language...).

On the other hand, we know a great deal about Quechua (the language of
the Incas), including some aspects that would make it _easier_ to
decipher the khipus (if only more attention given to this project).
Quechua utilizes relatively few sounds (both consonant and vowels).
Further suffixes play a key role in modifying the meaning of Quechua
words.

Yes, it's a challenge, but then challenges can be fun!
