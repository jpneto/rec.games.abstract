You have no idea what I suggest requires because you have no
idea regarding what I speak of. I seek the best decisions
for a solitaire Yahtzee game to maximize score. That means,
for example, if I roll 1 1 2 3 5 for my first roll of a game,
I must not only compute possible scores for all categories
that turn, but *for every subsequent turn for each of the
categories*. That requires many, many more calculations
than what would produce denominators of 7776 or even 7776^2.
For example, a decision whether to try for 3-of-a-kind or
4-of-a-kind, or Small Straight on the 6th game turn with 3 3
4 5 5 *if such (even) occurred* affects the decision whether
to keep only 5 on the 2nd roll of the game or 1 2 3 5 and
try for a 4 for Large Straight (which might best, placing 1
or 2 in 1's if such fails). Some of such decisions depend
on many other potential game rolls, and I am quite certain
that if anyone could accurately calculate them, a few
decisions would be decided by an average of about 10^-10
points or less. Because so many calculations are required,
an initial approximation accurate to 10^-8 might only be
accurate to 10^-2 when all are finished.
