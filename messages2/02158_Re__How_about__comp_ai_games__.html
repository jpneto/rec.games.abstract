This is a good idea. I am writing a chess program, too, and I would really
like such a forum. We would avoid the problem that people who are designing
game tree searchers have to post in game specific newsgroups (the "hey, this
is not our game" yell comes to mind) and that the brute force evangelists are
continously flamed by some <beep> ;-)

I always asked myself why there are so few articles about computer game playing
(I am talking about perfect information zero sum games here) in the net. This
maybe due to

(a) the little number of people who are working on such beasts
(b) the competitive factor in developing such programs
(c) many netters show strange behaviour after such an article is posted and the
poster looses interest in this form of communication.

Obviously (b) and (c) are good reasons to prefer email (hi Bob ;-). Nevertheless
such a forum would be great. By the way, any ideas how to determine how many people
are currently working on such projects? Just curious...
