ANN: New 2-player game based on Conway's "Life"

From: gerryq@indigo.ie (Gerry Quinn)
Date: 04 05 2000 00:00:00 GMT

Just out today! A new abstract game based on Conway's "Life" cellular
automaton. Many have tried, could this be the one to catch on...?
Requires Windows 95 or later.

Download from: http://bindweed.com/zen/zen.htm

It's a 2-player game with strong computer opponents. Cells are Red,
Green or neutral, but neutral cells die off soon leaving a head to head
between the two real players. You place a cell of your colour and the
board evolves one cycle. Simple!

- Great graphics...
- Boards up to 60x60...
- Various rule options...

The free version allows a full but fairly short game on a 24x24 board.
No time limits. For freestyle play you have to register (price $14.95,
instant online registration available) but you can try everything for
free.

Anyway, I hope to hear from some of you abstract game mavens with regard
to improvements and strategies...
