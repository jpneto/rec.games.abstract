There's a game like this called Attaxx which involves both moving and
placing pieces. On a 7x7 board, each player starts out with 2 pieces in
opposite corners. On any turn, you can either place a piece adjacent to
one of yours, or move a piece 2 spaces horizontally, vertically,
diagonally, or knight's move. In either case, any opponent's pieces
adjacent to the newly occupied square (the piece just placed or the
destination square of the move) are flipped. You must move if you can; if
not, you pass. Game ends when all 49 squares are filled, and is won by
the player with more pieces.
