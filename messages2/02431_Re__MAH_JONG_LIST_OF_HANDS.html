I agree, I dislike American-type rules (mind you I'm not American :-))
in which it's easy to get a limit - they should be fairly, although
not impossibly, rare. Otherwise if you don't look like getting one you're
forced to keep chowing to get a fast MJ so you don't end up paying out.

But I don't see any point in getting cross about Dave Warner's posting, it was
nice of him to make the information available, and for those people who are
into American type versions (presumably there are some, e.g., those who also
like gin rummy) it will I guess be a helpful resource.
