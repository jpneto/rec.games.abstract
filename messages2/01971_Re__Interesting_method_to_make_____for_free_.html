Now wait just a minute there. Let's deal with this in a true
Rec.Games.Abstract style. It might be possible that a few people get rich
in this fashion, but there's no room for everyone to do so. While it might
sound reasonable that there are 60,000 people in the world willing to sent
you $1, let's consider the perspectives of each of those 60,000. They are
doing it for the same reason that you are. For the moment, let's ignore the
fact that in the process of sending you $60,000 these people will be
spending $15,000 on postage.

I worked out the numbers on a spreadsheet assuming full participation at
each level that the mailing propagates. I got such a letter that told you
that about 20% of the people that you mail to do not respond. The numbers
work out just about the same in that case. The fraction spent on postage
just increases.

I'll also assume, for the moment, that no one gets caught in the chain
twice. That is, you'll never get a request from a person that is responding
to your request.

To see how much is being spent on postage, just divide the above numbers by
4. However, if you consider that much of the mail would have to be sent
overseas, there's a good chance that some pieces of mail would cost $2 or
more. Many rural tribes have no postal delivery. Nor do I know where they
would get their hands on a pile of dollar bills.

Also, consider the situation in the Soviet Union. Many of them are now
making only $15 a month. I don't think that you could expect them to spend
their weekly food budget to get a dollar bill and pay the postage to send it
to you.

If you want to make $600,000 dollars this year, take a $50 bill and sit down
over a beer with a friend. Tell each other stories for $50 each. The
faster you can pass the bill back and forth, the more income you earn.
Please don't come crying to me when the IRS wants $300,000 of your so called
earnings. Just tell them that you spent it on entertainment.
