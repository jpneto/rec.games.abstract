A square board should be odd sized to prevent draws.
I think Nick the win condition in itself isn't problematic in this day
and age. It's easy to keep track of the score in an applet. Yet I feel
Nick has a point - an intuitive point - about the goal.
Despite the 2-3 moves per turn, this game has a slower pace than
Symple. However, it has ample opportunities for combinations because
it isn't bound to extending existing groups by one stone, so it
behaves more flexible in this respect.

Territory can be claimed much the same way as in Symple: you can't
'invade' if it requires a new group that can't grow enough to
compensate for its penalty. So basically its Symple at a slower pace
and slightly different tactics. If I had a sense of humor, I'd say the
slower pace gives you more time to count, but if you follow Nick's
intuition, there may be a far less tedious counting procedure at hand:
what if you added your own XYodd to the mix in terms of a connection
goal?
