Chinese chess end-games may deserve a second thought. Chinese chess is
already a popular game, especially in East Asia. Commercial Chinese chess
programs play checkmate problems very well. But there seems to be no good
program for solving brinkmate problems. (Brinkmate means play a preparatory
move first, and checkmate in the next move against all possible defences.)
AI chess playing programs can play against itself to solve practical
endgames like R vs K, Q vs N etc. I wonder if there has been any researches
on practical Chinese chess endgames such as kNight vs Advisor, chaRiot vs
kNight & 2 Advisor, etc. Playing complete Chinese chess games is very
complicated. But specialising in a part of it can be an exciting research
topic.
