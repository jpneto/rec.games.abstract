How about Snort:

Draw a bunch of dots, and connect them with some lines so that the whole
figure is in one piece (in the jargon: a connected graph).

this is the board. Then players take turns coloring the dots their color (one
is red, one is blue, or whatever). The only rule is that you can't color a
dot your color if it's connected to a dot of the other color by a line. The
first player who can't move when called upon to do so loses.
