You are hereby invited to come play-test Bot Planet, a new turn-based
multiplayer version of Conway's Game of Life. As a player, you join a
team and place up to 3 cells per hour on the grid. Every hour on the
hour, a new pattern develops from the cells you and others placed. Will
your cells multiply or perish? It's not a fast-paced game, but not
nearly as boring as it sounds IF you have a head for geometry and
strategy. Best of all, it's FREE!
