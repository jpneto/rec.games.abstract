The one I have played (here in New Zealand) is called Tantrix, but there are
other versions available with similar names. It only involves 3 colours of
paths (red, blue and yellow), so is only a three player game. Each player has
their `set` from which they can select any.... as opposed to drawing from a
bag, they hold their entire set for the game.

Quite an interesting game (for a short while :)

I wouldnt know of anywhere you can get hold of it outside of New Zealand
obviously.
