Exactly! It's been 4 months since I took our first analysis module (logic
and proof, real numbers, sequences and series) I'm only now beginning to
understand it, but now we've sat the exam for that module and are onto the
next (continuity, differentiation, integration and power series). I learn a
bunch of concepts, and repeat them in the exam with little understanding of
what they mean. Only now is it becoming clear. For example, the (now) easy
examples like

E > 0 is given, find d > 0 st the implication is valid:

e.g. |x-3| < d => |x^2 - 9| < E

is so much easier now I understand the purpose of epsilon and delta (luckily
in this case it became apparent just before the exam). Before it was just a
case of doing what they did and assume it'll all work out. That's not
learning, it's copying!
