Hi everyone,

I would like to take this opportunity to invite you to join the ukbet
mailing list.

Ukbet list is a discussion forum on tips, ideas, form and the search
for value and moneymaking bets. Argument and discussion are more than
welcome and the topics include racing, golf, snooker and well, just
about anything that you can place a bet on.

Both Racing and Sports betting are heavily featured and I suspect that
the coming British General Election may contain a word or two about
where value may be found.

Join the argument, discussion and predictions on UKBET. It is a free
and open list.

To join, send an email to:

majordomo@ipw.com

there is no need for a subject line, and in the body of the message
write:

subscribe ukbet

and you will immediately receive a reply acknowledging your message
and telling you about the list. From then you will receive by email
all messages posted to the list. You can post your own message either
in reply or to start a new thread by posting email to:

ukbet@ipw.com

If you want to unsubscribe simply send an email to:

majordomo@ipw.com

and in the body of the message write:

unsubscribe ukbet

and that is all there is to it.

So come and join us on a superb free private mailing list where you
can hear about all the action and join in the discussion when and if
you wish - without the junk mail.
