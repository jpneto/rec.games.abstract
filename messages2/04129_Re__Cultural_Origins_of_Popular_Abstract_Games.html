It's very difficult to trace most very old games to their exact origin
due partly to their age (hundreds to thousands of years) and partly to
the fact that virtually none are still played in their original forms -
they have all evolved, spawned numerous variants, etc. History is often
rewritten as new or rediscovered information/artifacts, etc. come to
light.

As to Germany, according to Bell (Board and Table Games from Many
Civilizations)

The Courier Game (a chess variant) and
Schimmel (Bell and Hammer - a dice game with special cards)

both originated in Germany.

Keep looking - I'm sure you can find more.
