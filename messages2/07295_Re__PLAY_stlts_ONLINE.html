Thanks for giving it a go Pete. Yes, beating the computer is a sinch
once you employ that strategy. I tried doing a bit of tinkering to
nudge the computer into playing the same way but it took it a bit
literally ending up with one gigantic piece that it didn't have the wit
to put to great use and it also had the side effect that it took an age
to search through all the moves that became available as a result of this!

It tends to even out in 'real' games. It certainly seems to be the best
strategy to get a powerful piece on the go, but your opponent can do the
same and even if he doesn't it can turn into an 'eggs in one basket'
situation where the big piece is eventually hunted down, often by a
couple of moderate pieces, and then the balance flips.

If you ever want to try playing a real person, this one can give you a
game pretty much any time!
