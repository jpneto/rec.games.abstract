History
During Mind Computers Olympiads by David Levi it was competitions
for Gomoku and Renju Computer programs.
In 1989 in London "Stonesystem" by N. Alexandrov , M. Trukhan and
A. Grigoriev was the winner of Renju match .
Then in 1990 in London "Stonesystem" won again.
In 1991 Moscow "Vertex" won friend match against Japanese program
of Mr. Matsuura 2:1
In 1991 in Maastricht (Netherlands) "Vertex" by A. Shaposhnikov and
A. Nosovsky was won.
After that First World Championship of Gomoku and Renju was held in
Moscow 7 participants from 5 countries took part in it.
"Vertex" by A. Shaposhnikov and A. Nosovsky won again.
In 1992 in London won "Neuron" by A.Golubev and A. Melnikov but "Vertex"
did not participate.
During all this years the programs from England, Netherlands, Finland,
Sweden, Russia, Latvia, Ukraine, Japan, Armenia took part in Renju
competition.
After that it was a break.

COMPETITION

So, it will be two tournaments of 2-nd World Computer Championship in
Renju.
First is solving problems in Renju.
10 problems from Organizers and one from each participant will consist the
1-st Tournament in Solving problems.
Second Tournament - Real games between computer programs.

Rules of Opening and the Game are according RIF see it at
http:\\www.lemes.se\renju

World Championship Renju rules

First Tournament - Solving problems
One hour for each position.
Computer have to suggest the way for sure win.
Victory must be shown before the end of the hour.
Anyone can try to protect any victory of computer.

Second Tournament
1. This tournament system is ROUND-ROBIN - system .
2. Time : 50 moves/120 minutes, and 10 moves/ 10 minutes .
NB 50 moves/ 120 minutes : Black must make 99 -th move , and White
must make 100-d move in time, then moves 119 and 120 are critical.
3. Opening Rules : Black Option and Reverse of White and Black Choice.
a. Black have to play first move to the center of the board.
b. Black made White move direct or indirect relativly first black move.
c. Black made their second black move ( N 3 in the game) on the place of
12 Renju Opennigs.
d. White can change colour and play this position as Black.
e. White make their second move (N 4 in the game)
f. Black have to suggested two variants of their third move (N 5 in the
game)
g. White chose one of Black moves, another suggested move delete from
the board.
4. The match consists of two games.
