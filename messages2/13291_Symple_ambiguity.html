Just when you thought Symple couldn't get any more complicated, Christian Freeling writes in the Arimaa forum:
"This note is included to clarify of what 'all possible groups' means. This isn't something that can be determined a priori: it depends on the group configuration and order of placements. It includes the particular case in which a group cannot grow at all because all its liberties are shared with a group or groups that have already grown..."
