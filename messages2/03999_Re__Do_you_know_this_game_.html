This would be Nomic, invented by Peter Suber. See
http://www.earlham.edu/~peters/nomic.htm for a fair amount of information
about it.

The actual act of playing Nomic is so much like arguing over the rules of a
game that you're in the middle of playing as to be virtually
indistinguishable from it. Yet quite a few people seem to like it. Go
figure.
