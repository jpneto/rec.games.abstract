Well, to return to the top of the thread, Ketchup is indeed one of
them because the move protocol and the object provide a continuous
balance by negative feedback, so the opening is no exception.
By the same token, if there is a turn order advantage in Yodd and

so the status of every position in the tree is either a win or a loss.

where humans and sufficiently large boards are concerned.
