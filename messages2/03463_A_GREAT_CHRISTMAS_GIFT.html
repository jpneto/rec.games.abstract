This solitaire board game originated in the prisons of
france in the 16th century. Prisoners would carve the board from wood
and it kept
them occupied, helping them cope with their burdens. The game survived
into the
20th century taking various forms with pegs and holes and other things.
My mom had
a carved wooden one on the table for years, One day 5 or 6 years ago I
decided to
replicate the game in my shop. We handcraft the game on an acrylic board
1/2" thick.
It measures about 10" x 10". The board is cut to an octogon shape and
all edges are
nicely beveled. Blue marbles are used in grooves which are cut into
board. The result
of our efforts is a very attractive handcrafted board game which looks
great when
displayed on coffee or dining room tables etc. (we attach 3 half round
acrylic balls to
bottom to prevent scratching) We have sold about 500 over the years and
people
keep commenting on how nice it looks and that the game keeps kids and
grandpa
real busy trying to solve the puzzle! This makes a great Christmas gift!
