I have written a small game in C++ based on the well known "life game"
created by John Conway in the 70's.
It's only the first version, but I think it can be a lot of fun.
It is not an ordinary game: you don't have to win against a foe, you just
have to place some cells on a grid and watch them multiply or die.
The first version can be downloaded on my homepage if you are interested:
