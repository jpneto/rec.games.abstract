From: jte@primenet.com (Edward Jackman)

Does anyone have any information about GO played on a hexagonal,
triangular,
pentagonal or other non-orthogonal boards?

I recently started playing some GO variants on a hexagonal board.
Stones
are placed on spaces rather than points. A single stone on a non-edge,

non-corner space has 6 liberties rather than 4. Games can be played
on the vertices instead -- then there are 3 liberties per point. Has
anyone played GO on boards like this or other non-standard geometries?

Do the games work as well as standard Go or are there some problems?

and pentagonal arrays on which GO and GO variants can be played.

with 120 and 60 degree angles.)

Each non-edge space is adjacent to 4 others. Each
non-edge vertex is connected either 3 or 6 others.
