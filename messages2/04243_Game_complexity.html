I know not whether this is the right forum for this, but:

Does anyone know of a measure for the complexity of a game's rules?

I could think of two possible measures: One is the Kolmogorov-complexity
of an adequate formulation of the rules (for a human). The other (which I
think would be better) is the length of the shortest such computer program
that can determine whether or not an action is legal in a given position
of the game.

This (of course) applies only to abstract games, and I thought one of you
guys would know something about it.
