I can't claim *any* expertise on the game, but since no one else has
responded, I'll give it my best shot. POG is a game like marbles, or
baseball card flipping in that the object is to collect other people's
pogs. A pog is a cardboard "coin" (about the size of an Eisenhower dollar).
On one side there is a design, the other side has the word "pog" on it.
Players place pogs, design side up, in a stack, then one of the players
throws a heavier (metal?) pog "disc" (I don't know the technical term for
this piece) directly on top of the stack. The throwing player then collects
any flipped over pogs, the rest stay in the "pot".

Apparently, the attraction of this game is to collect a wide variety of
pogs with attractive designs. If I recall correctly, the game originated
in Hawaii, and was played with milk bottle caps. The game caught on there,
spread to the U.S. west coast, and is gaining popularity all over the
US. "Official" POG playing materials are now being manufactured, so I
guess that game manufacturers are think that the game will be around
long enough for them to cash in.

Note: I've probably made some errors in the above, as it's purely from
my (rather faulty) memory.
