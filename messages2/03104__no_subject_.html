CALL FOR PAPERS

ADVANCES IN COMPUTER CHESS 8

June 27-28, 1996

Maastricht, the Netherlands


The eighth conference of the triennial "Advances in Computer Chess" series will be held in
Maastricht, the Netherlands, on June 27-28, 1996, at Castle Vaeshartelt, 5 km from Maastricht and
in the suburbs of that city. It will report on the latest research results and work-in-progress in
the field.


SUBMISSIONS

Contributions are invited from authors who have new results or ongoing work to report on any
aspect of computer chess or related theory. Papers should ideally be 3,000 to 4,000 words in
length and will be refereed by an accelerated procedure. The accepted papers will be published in
book form as the 8th triennial volume of Advances in Computer Chess, to be edited by Jaap van den
Herik and Bob Herschberg.

Submissions (3 hard copies or one E-mail copy (postscript greatly preferred)) should be sent to:
