Felix Lee wonders when one gets to place bridges.

I believe you get to place them as they come into existence. Thus,
if a player places a piece at the center of a circle of his/her pegs
(imagine a peg at each possible knight's move) then s/he gets to
place 8 bridges.

I seem to recall that 3 or 4 were the theoretical maximum that a peg
could hold, however....
