One grid that I've always wanted to play on is the square tesselation of
the hyperbolic plane. It's simply a tiling that iscompletely symmetric,
but six squares meet at each vertex instead of four. Admittedly, such a
board is hard to draw, but it gives a nice twist on most square grid games
without having to change the rules radically.
