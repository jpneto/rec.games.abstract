Another way of doing it is to have bumps an dents so the pieces
fit together like jigsaw pieces. The edges marked 'A' are given
a single bump, 'AA' get double bumps, 'B' get dents and 'BB' get
double dents:

For the purposes of playing Go on the tiles, the edge
matching restriction could be lifted, but then it isn't
a Penrose tiling anymore and you might as well use ANY
rhombus' that fit together -- for instance, you could
use squares and rhombus' with 45 and 135 degree angles.
Or you could use rhombus' with 60 and 120 degree angles
all by themselves.
