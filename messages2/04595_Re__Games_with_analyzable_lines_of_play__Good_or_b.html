Personally, I don't like games where the players can analyze the system.
This is particularly true if the person tries to analyze during his or her
move, thus bogging down the game. A friend of ours gets really frustrated
when this happens and preaches "play the game, not the system." And that's
so true. It's supposed to be entertainment, so if one turns it into a
scholarly ritual of highest-rated possibilities, it's no longer fun. I say
I want a game you can play quickly without super analysis and ride it out
to see who wins.
