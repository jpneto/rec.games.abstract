"Exactly" means "on average in the long run".

I can ride a bike over a mile long straight line, and end exactly
where I intend. But along the way I will make those little sways that
are implied in keeping one's balance while riding a straight
trajectory.

Symple has a balancing mechanism that is much more sophisticated in
its workings than a pie, because it will in the long run _implicitly_
converge to a 50/50 rate. No other mechanism I know has this feature.

It serves Sygo too, though Sygo, as mentioned, can end in a draw and
thus falls outside the "mythical" category discussed here.
