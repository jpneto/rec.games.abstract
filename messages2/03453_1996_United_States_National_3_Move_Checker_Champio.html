1996 United States National 3-Move Checker Championship

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 13 11 1996 00:00:00 GMT


The 1996 United States National 3-Move Checker Championship is underway
at the Howard Johnson Inn in Danville, Virginia. This tournament features
a strong contingent from the United States, Canada, Barbados, and the
United Kingdom. Complete round-by-round scores for the Master division
may be found from the Chinook home page (http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/~chinook).
