If you're prepared to lower your standards and accept a game
with a theme very thinly painted over a basically abstract
game, then Durch die Wuest, by Reiner Knizia, qualifies.
Players place camels on a hex grid with the aim of
surrounding territory, and the effect is rather like multi-
player Go. Someone else has suggested Billabong, by Alex
Randolph, and I'd second that suggestion. In the same vein
is Elephantenparade, author's name forgotten; it's "about"
leapfrogging elephants. Hase und Igel/Hare and Tortoise is
a decent game with modest interaction with its theme, though
there players usually have the option of going for a chance
card or die roll and hoping, Micawber-like, that something
will Turn Up.
