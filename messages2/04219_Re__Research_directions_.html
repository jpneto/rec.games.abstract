As I mentioned earlier on this newsgroup, the game of Mastermind has not
been solved, although a strategy has been found which produces the least
expected value given a uniform distribution of codes. This strategy is
within range of this year's personal computers, but it is not the final
solution, because it probably is not optimal against someone who does
not choose codes with a uniform distribution, and there is the issue of
altering one's strategy if one plays second and has to beat a certain
score. I think finding the actual solution is challenging but doable,
and I doubt anyone is working on it.

A more exciting variation is to guess simultaneously, so one might alter
one's strategy if worried that one's opponent will guess correctly
shortly, even though this might reveal that the opponent is close. I
don't think a solution of that is feasible in the near future.

Reference: Kenji Koyama and Tony W. Lai, An optimal Mastermind strategy,
J. Recreational Math. 25(4) (1993), 251-256.
