As for a diagram, here is a simple example of 2 connected-subgraphs
that are then connected by a single line-segment:

*--*--*--*--*
...|...|.......
*..*--*~~*--*
..........|...
*..*..*..*

("|" and "--" are vertical and horizontal line-segments. "~~" is the
horizontal line segment that is later drawn to connect the 2 separate
