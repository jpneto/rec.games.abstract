This seems to me to be a perfect time to do a little bit of advertisement.
People have been mentioning nomic and I thought it would be remiss of me to
fail to give you some of the links I have. First off, there's a very
recently launched game of nomic on usenet at alt.games.nomic which also has
it's own webpage at <http://www.interalpha.net/customer/jefferis/nomic/>. I
also play Ackanomic which is very well established (about two years old)
and great fun. That's on the web at
<http://www.telusplanet.net/public/alfvaen/acka/index.html>. Lastly, since
you've already mentioned Mornington Crescent, there's Mornington Nomic
which is a cross between Mornington Crescent and nomic at
<http://www.cms.dmu.ac.uk/~c94kd/mornomic.html>.

If you think nomic sounds interesting then give one of them a go.
