The most interesting thing about all this is not necessarily whether
you will win or lose in 2012, Christian (Yes, I think you have a
chance, but given that the bar is set at 10 out of 10 I would tend to
favor the bots), but how much programming effort has gone into
Havannah. I'm sure the challenge prize accounts for some of it, but
not all of it. Other modern games I can think of that have attracted
significant efforts from programmers include Arimaa, Lines of Action,
Amazons. I believe there is also a challenge prize for Arimaa.
