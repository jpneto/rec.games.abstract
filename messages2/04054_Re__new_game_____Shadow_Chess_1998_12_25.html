I don't believe that Shadow Chess as described in this thread is Kriegspiel.
In Kriegspiel, you aren't told the positions of your opponent's units; you must
deduce them yourself from as the referee tells that that your own move attempts
are either legal or illegal. In Shadow Chess, as I understand it, the referee
tells you an enemy unit's position as soon as you attack it, or or as soon as
knowing its position is necessary for you to know the legality of all of your
possible move attemptst. Therefore, in Shadow Chess, you get more information
faster than in Kriegspiel. I have personally not played either game.
