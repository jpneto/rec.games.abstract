Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship - Day 5 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 23 03 1996 00:00:00 GMT

Don Lafferty and Ron King remain tied 2-2 after 20 games of the 1996 Man vs.
Man World Checkers Championship being played in Bridgetown, Barbados. They
are now at the half way point of the match. I would like to thank Hallam
Hope from Bridgetown for providing me with these updates.
