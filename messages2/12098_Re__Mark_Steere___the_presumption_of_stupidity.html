I would need to play MQ a bunch times both with and without the anti-
cycle baby-movement rule to decide whether's it's a bazooka or not.
At this point I cannot tell.

As far as the game's representation goes, it makes much more sense to
me to use the chess representation of the queen as a single unique
piece rather than a stack. The stack only came about because the game
was intended to compete in the stacking games context, but now that
that contest is gone and forgotten, the "queen as a stack" concept
seems esthetically much less satisfying than the "queen as a queen"
representation.
