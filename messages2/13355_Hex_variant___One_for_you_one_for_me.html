Here is a new variant of Hex.
Instead of dropping one stone of your colour a player MUST drop 2 stones :
- ONE OF HIS COLOUR
- ONE OF HIS OPPONENT COLOUR.

The move is mandatory.
The goal of the game remain unchanged : connecting your sides.
