INTRODUCTION
------------
Summit is a "mind game" for two or more
players. No equipment is required.

RULES
-----
The "token" is a 3 digit number that ranges
from 000 to 999. The token starts out as
000. Players take turns increasing the
token in the following manner:

On your turn, you increase a single digit
of the token by one. Then you zero all of the
digits to the right of the increased digit.

For example, 235 can be "rounded up" to one of
these three numbers: 236, 240, or 300.

As another example, 652 could be increased to
653, 660, or 700.

The numeral 9 cannot be "increased" to zero.
Zero is not considered the equivalent of 10.

When you decide on a move, just say it out
loud.

The first player to move will have the choice
of 001, 010, or 100. If he chooses 010 as his
move, the next player will have the choice of
011, 020, or 100.

FOUR-PLAYER EXAMPLE GAME
------------------------
Player 1: 010
Player 2: 011
Player 3: 012
Player 4: 013
Player 1: 020
Player 2: 021
Player 3: 030
Player 4: 040
Player 1: 041
Player 2: 100
Player 3: 200
Player 4: 210
Player 1: 220
Player 2: 221
Player 3: 222
Player 4: 230
. . .
. . .
Player 1: 997
Player 2: 998
Player 3: 999 (Player #3 wins the game)

OBJECT OF THE GAME
------------------
The first player to reach 999 wins.

SUMMIT VARIATIONS
-----------------
In a variation of Summit, one could allow
several winning numbers in addition to 999.

For example, players could win with a straight,
a 3-of-a-kind, or a "blackjack", as explained
in the following paragraphs.

A straight would be three consecutive numbers,
ascending or descending. For example: 456, 987,
012, and 765 would be winning numbers.

A 3-of-a-kind would be a token with 3 equal
digits. For example: 111, 222, 333, etc. would
be winning numbers.

"Blackjack" is a token whose digits add up to 21.
For example 579 would be a winning number because
5+7+9=21.

Note (!): Combinations of the above variations may
help to thwart simple schemes for winning every
time. The exact variant should be agreed upon at
the outset of each game.

Start with 00 or 0000 for a shorter or a longer
variation of Summit.

Your opponents get to throw ice cubes at you while
you're trying to think of a move.

ORIGINS
-------

in the Internet newsgroup, rec.games.abstract;
