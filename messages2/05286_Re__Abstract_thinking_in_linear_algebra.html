This is exactly right. There is no consideration of what
multiplication means, except the obtaining of answers. The
same is true for addition.

>I distinctly remember by 3rd grade "teacher" making me stand in the
>corner when I explained that I did, for example, 8 X 4 by turning into
>4 X 8 and then 2 X 16 = 32. Like I was excited I'd figured out how

I would not call it that, but more the use of commutativity
and associativity, probably combined with knowing that
2*x = x+x. Teachers are told how to teach, but do not
understand what they are doing. They do not even learn
that there can be more than one way to calculate something.

>> And probably the worst travesty is that this stuff is called "math"

It is worse than that. Many high school mathematics teachers
are often almost as bad. They cannot understand that mathematics
does not consist of definitions and formulas and algorithms to
be slavishly followed.
