INFORMATION & RULES FOR
TEXAN'S $MONEY$ BACKGAMMON
TOURNAMENT ON F.I.B.S.

Hello Backgammon Players!

This tournament will be a 32 person, double elimination tournament. Each
name received will be put into a hat and your opponent will be randomly
chosen. You may wind up playing somebody a couple thousand ratings
points
better than you or you may be playing somebody a couple thousand rating
points less than your own, (it's a draw of the hat)

RULES

ALL GAMES ARE TO BE PLAYED ON F.I.B.S.

1. Tournament starts officially when you receive E-mail from me saying
so.

2. All players will be notified on who they are playing.

3. You have exactly two weeks (14 days) to contact your opponent and
play
your match.

4. All match lengths will be 7 pointers, except the semifinals (9
pointers)and
final (11 pointer)

5. All winners are to e-mail me the results of their match ASAP.

6. This is a double elimination tournament. Once you lose twice you are
out
of the tournament. A bracket will be set up on the main page for all to
follow
during the duration of the tournament.

7. To enter tournament a $15 (US Dollars) entry fee is required. Send
Check
or money order made out to RANDY AVEN, or if you are out of the US and
it's cheaper for you to send cash (which I DO NOT recommend), you may do
so, to the following address:

Randy Aven
Texan's Backgammon Tournament
4817 Carolwood Drive
Plano, Texas 75024 USA

(Please e-mail me to let me know you have sent your fee and I will
reserve a
spot for you, but if fee is not received within 7 days of email, then
you will be
removed until fee is received.)

8. Cash prizes will be awarded to the First, Second, & Third place
winners of
the tournament. ALL ENTRY FEES WILL BE DISTRIBUTED. Of 32 entries,
there will be $480 for distribution to 1st, 2nd, & 3rd place winners at
70%,
20%, & 10% takes. To see how bracket will be set up click on BRACKET.

9. GOOD LUCK & HAVE FUN!
