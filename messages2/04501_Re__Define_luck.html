>In my opinion, a game involves luck if either:
>
>a) It contains explicit randomizers, or
>b) There are no Nash equilibria in pure strategies.

I suppose I should add that my definition is not designed to work with
games which explicitly involve physical skills, artistic skills, memorization,
trivia knowledge, and similar factors. Although, if anyone cares, I think
that most popular sports (baseball, football, basketball, hockey, &c.)
contain very little luck other than the coin toss, though they all
contain some of (b), and they also have elements of incomplete
information which I'd have to consider lucky. (For (b) in sports, consider
pitch selection in baseball or play calling in football. For elements
of incomplete information, consider the effect of wind.)
