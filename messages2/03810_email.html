Try to find collections of Martin Gardner's articles from Scientific
American. In those you should find "sprouts" as well as other games.

Dots and Boxes is a very good paper and pencil game. There are several
levels of stratgey that can be learned by young children.

Nim is another game they might like. There are a number of variations
on how the game can be played. One common one is sometimes called 3, 5, 7.
You can use pennies to make rows of 3, 5 and 7 pennies. Players take
turns removing at least one coin from a row and whoever takes the
last penny loses.

If you can find it, take a look at Winning Ways Volume 2: Games in
Particular. The book itself is way beyond the grade level you are
teaching, but many of the games are not.
