It looks to me like a three in a row is formed in the
lower diagram by (3,1),(2,2),(1,3) where (x,y) is used
with x=1...5 and y= 1...5. This is before the play
at b, so b cannot be used to score.

However, the following seems to score five:

00X0X
00XX0
XXc00
00XX0
00X0X

This scores one each in the directions N, NE,
SE, S, and W, using same metric as others on
the N-S line of five.

But shouldn't one line of five score *three*? It has
the three triplets (1,2,3), (2,3,4) and (3,4,5).
