I have introduced not one, but TWO
similar but different games. The same
holds for Nim and anti-Nim:

in Nim you win by taking the last stone;
in anti-Nim you lose by takieng the last stone
-- two similar but different games.

In my games it's nnot the last stone, which
is critical, but the stone, which removal
cause equality of all plates.

BTW, if at the start there was no stone on
one or more of the plates then the justice
(resp. injustice) game is reduced to Nim
(or, respectively, to anti-Nim).
