Our language is such that it presumes a context, a framework within
which the subject can be pointed out. A subject without context, like
eternal life as "understood" by christians et al, is meaningless. To
lend it some meaning, it is then understood as "a _very_ long time".
But infinity has nothing to do with time, whether short, long or
_very_ long.

Moreover, how can something that had a beginning, like time, have no
end?
