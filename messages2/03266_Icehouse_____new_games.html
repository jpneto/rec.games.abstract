Got something unexpected in the snail-mail last night.

Five NEW games that can be played with Icehouse pieces.

Icehouse is a fascinating oddity, a turn-less, board-less game played
on any surface using a set of pyramid playing pieces. Check out:

http://www.gamecabinet.com/ads/icehouse.html

Anyway, the turn-less ness of Icehouse became a problem for me,
couldn't open up to it.

One of these new games, Aracana, looks quite good. It uses a Tarot
deck and combines some ideas from Magic-esque games. The Tarot cards
become different territories where minions (the pyramids) can move. A
minion can take an action based on the type of card it occupies, or on
a card played out of hand. Looks interesting.
