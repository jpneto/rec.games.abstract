Accepting Games Submissions

From: markg@anshar.shadow.net (Mark G.)
Date: 12 Mar 1995 13:05:56 -0500

We are now accepting submissions for original and unique games to sell
out of a mail-order catalog. We would like to offer a variety of games in
boxed and book formats. Our plan is to cover the costs of printing and
shipping while giving the designer a commission (5-10%). The designer
will always keep the rights to his game and will be able to sell their
game elsewhere as well. We would like to specialize in strategy and
adventure games but would consider any game that may be put in a boxed or
book format. Thank you.
