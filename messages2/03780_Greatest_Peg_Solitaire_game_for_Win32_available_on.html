Main features :
---------------

Here are Peg solitaire 1.97 main features :

1. Offers 15 different boards.
(French, English, Squares, Wiegleb's, Hermary's, Triangulars, ... ).

2. Contains about 300 solved problems.

3. Takes into account "marked" Pegs (Pegs that don't move during the play).

4. Uses "DeBrujin's", "Draugthsboard", and "Sets of Pegs" unfeasibility
criterion, which are useful to prove many problems unfeasibility.

5. Shows "Inverse solution" for a solved problem.

6. Shows number of Sweeps and longest Sweep number for a solution.

7. Players can design their own problems, can solve them, ... or can let
the computer do the job ... they can also save and load saved solutions,
they can print solutions.

8. Search engine is improved, and solves every problem designed by players
in a few (centi)seconds. (in fact, rather 95% should I say ...).
Solutions are computed and are given "As is". They do not possess intrinsic
distinctive feature.
