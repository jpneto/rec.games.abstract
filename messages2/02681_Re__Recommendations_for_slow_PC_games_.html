Greg, I think you are in need of some of the old Infocom adventures.
They are almost entirely text-based, take very little RAM or disk space,
and are very VERY good. The classics include Zork (1, 2, 3, etc), the
Enchanter series, Planetfall and Stationfall, and the Hitchkikers' Guide
to the Galaxy. You can pick them ALL up on a CD-ROM called The Lost
Treasures of Infocom, and download them to the old machine froma friend's
CD PC. The absolute BEST value around.
