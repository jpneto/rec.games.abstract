I'm looking for any info (history, rules, variations, anecdote,
social value, players, etc.) about a very common African game called
'MANCALA' or 'WARI' in Ghana (?!) and 'BAO' in Tanzania (but it is played
in whole Africa I understood).

If no bell rings, the board consists of about 12-20 hollows (it can
be played on the ground with small hollows dug out, too) in two rows.
Stones or seeds are used as pieces, and they are accumulated and moved
in a cycle through the hollows, according to specific rules (can't
remember them) to achieve a specific goal (can't remember it).

Any reference, explanation, experience is welcome. Please e-mail.
