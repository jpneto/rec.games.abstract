Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship - Day 8 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 27 03 1996 00:00:00 GMT

Don Lafferty leads 5-3 over Ron King after 32 games of the 1996 Man vs. Man
World Checkers Championship being played in Bridgetown, Barbados. King won
the 31st game to narrow the lead to 4-3 but Lafferty regained control with a
win in the 32nd game. At most 8 games remain in the match.

This update has been provided by Hallam Hope from Bridgetown. More information
may be found in http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/~chinook.
