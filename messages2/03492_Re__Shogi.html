No.

Promotion occurs if you _move_ a piece and it starts or ends (or both)
its move within the prompotion zone. Dropping is not a move. Dropped
pieces always land "right side up".

Promotion is usually voluntary. But if non-promotion would mean that
the piece could never move again (a pawn, knight, or lance that had run
out of board), then it must be promoted.

The restrictions on dropping are:

you may not drop a pawn on a file where you already have a pawn (a file
with a promoted pawn(s) is ok)

you may not drop anything where it can never move again (a pawn, knight,
or lance that has no more board to move to)

you may not drop a pawn so as to give checkmate
