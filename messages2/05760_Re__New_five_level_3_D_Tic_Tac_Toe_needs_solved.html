cubic diagonal rows. That comes to 109 "winning combinations for the
system", not 252 as you claim.

'3 years of play at all levels', you have played to a draw only once.

PLAYED one game), I cannot understand how this can be. Were any of
these wins forced by forming a double four? Or were they more like:
"Oops, I didn't see you had 4-in-a-row there."

I'm skeptical that there are "several chess masters" who are unable to
see one move in advance.
