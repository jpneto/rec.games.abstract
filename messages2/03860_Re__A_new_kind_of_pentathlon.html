For several years I used to run, as a social event, something we called
the "Chocolate Lake Tri-triathlon". Our inspiration, like yours, was
that conventional triathlons challenged only the body.

The event varied from year to year, but was always something like this:

Start by the dock, where as the clock starts, you are handed a list of
six words. Run up the hill, hop on your bike, and cycle about half a
kilometre.

There, jump off the bike, pick up the pastels and sketch pad, and do a
self-portrait. Then run about 300 m to the other side of the lake.

At that side of the lake, pick up the page of puzzles, solve as many as
possible as quicly as possible, then swim the 200-300 m across the
lake, back to the dock.

Once back, write a poem, using the six words you were given at the
start, about your experience in the race.

It wasn't physically that challenging, I admit, but it was fun. Four
couples were the core group of participants (sometimes as teams, not
individuals), and one member of each group was a go player - but we
never worked go into the contest. Lawn darts one year, though...
