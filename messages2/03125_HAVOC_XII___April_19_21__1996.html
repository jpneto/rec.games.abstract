HAVOC XII

BattleGroup Boston, Inc. is proud to sponsor our twelfth annual wargames
convention, HAVOC XII. The convention will be held on April 19 - 21, 1996
at the Royal Plaza Hotel in Marlborough, Massachusetts. We would be
pleased to have your participation in this event.

Havoc XII is a historical and miniatures wargames convention. Past Havocs
have included Ancients, Medieval, Renaissance, ECW, 7 Year's War (on both
sides of the Atlantic), American Revolution (on land and at sea),
Napoleonics, American Civil War, American Far West, Franco-Prussian War,
Colonials, WWI, WWII (the sequel), Moderns (on land, at sea, and in the
air), Fantasy, and Science Fiction and Future (on land, at sea, in the
air, and in space).

We expect to run the following tournaments: Ancients, Advanced Squad
Leader, Warhammer 40K, Magic: The Gathering, and Star Fleet Battles.
