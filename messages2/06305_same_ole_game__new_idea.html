Last time I look around, we all age, the world never stay the same,
and fighting is integrated with loving. Wouldn't that be a surprise to
all those anti-war demonstrators?

Now the game construct can easily represent all those elements listed
above, ageing, change, fighting and loving.

Age can be represent with a steady loss of power, health, or magic
