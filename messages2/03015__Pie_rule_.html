I guess we're familiar with the "I cut, you choose" rule for cutting pie,
and with the analogous way to help even out a game, namely I set up a position
(typically close to the standard start position) and say which color moves
next, and you choose which color to play. I gather this has come to be
called the "pie rule"? But it could equally well be called the "choose your
weapon" rule, which is what I say before a duel, showing you a pair
of dueling pistols, right? Surely that's a more well-known instance of
the rule than slicing pie? The people I know usually take the small slice
anyway, saying something about a diet. :)
