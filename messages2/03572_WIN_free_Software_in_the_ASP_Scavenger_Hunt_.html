The ASP Scavenger Hunt
------------------------

The Association of Shareware Professionals sponsors a monthly web
based scavenger hunt. Clues are found throughout the web, and the
winners receive ASP CDs and free registered versions of software from
ASP members totalling hundreds of dollars.


To join the hunt in progress, go http://www.seanet.com/~yngvi/scavenge.htm
