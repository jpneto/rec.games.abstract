Triccs - Case Study in Game Mutilation

From: Mark Steere <marksteere@gmail.com>
Date: Sun, 2 Dec 2012 15:00:23 -0800 (PST)

Reading the rules to Christian Freeling's latest game, Triccs, was torture. Sheer torture. Triccs mutilates a perfectly good game, Othello, with an arbitrary, complicated move protocol. Of course the Triccs rule set is only the beginning. Now commences the unrelenting promotional campaign of how "great", "perfect", and "quintessential" Triccs is. A triumph of "oneness".

Christian is forever obsessing over the swinging shadow of "Mt. Doom". Swing faster oh shadow. Faster!
