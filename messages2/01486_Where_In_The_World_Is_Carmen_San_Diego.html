Hello,

My 9 year old son received a board game called "Where In The World Is
Carmen Sandiego". He couldn't figure out the instructions, so he
asked me to. I only got a rough idea of how to play from the
instructions. The instructions consist of disjoint clues which do not
contain all the information necessary to describe the game.

I'm sending a letter to the manufacturer, in the hope that they can
explain it better. Since this might take a couple of weeks, I thought
I would also post this.

If you really know how to play the game, or if you have your own way
of playing the game, I'd like to hear from you.
