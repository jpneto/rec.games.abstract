Hi Luigi,

Captains, when not capturing, slide to a hexagon where they line up
with an opposing piece. They are not required to move to a hexagon
where they can be captured.

I chose to not allow the Captain to make teh long slide after a
capture, and I suppose the game would be playable that way. But I
didn't want it to be too great an advantage to obtain the first
Captain, and I was concerned that the long slide after capturing,
combined with the linear capture, would be too strong and would make
it too easy for the first player to promote to set up a devastating
multiple capture. I didn't want the game to come down to a race to
promote first.

You are correct about the drawing positions, and I'm surprised that
both me and Christian missed it (the egg is all on my face, as the
inventor). It is clearly possible for two players to cooperate to
move such that the game doesn't end. I believe that in all such
positions, one player can always force the win, but nothing in the
rules compels it to happen. I will have to reconsider.
