Announcing BoardSpace.net, a free & non-commercial server devoted to
board games. Boardspace.net is currently serving Zertz, Zertz+11, and Lines
of Action. More games will be added, at least partly in response to the
players' requests.

Boardspace has been operating since May 2004. It's a hobby of mine, with
no commercial aspirations. I guarantee it won't ever be be polluted with
advertizing or turned into a paid site. So come enjoy a game.
