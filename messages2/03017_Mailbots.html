The following is a list of Four Leaf Clover Documents
available by autoresponder (Mailbot):

To receive a file from our autoresponder you simply type in the name
of the file and then our GEN address:

Example: filename@fourleaf.entprepreneurs.net

We will be constantly updating and adding files. Also, we will be consolidating them.

You should receive the files to your email address in a few seconds. You only have
to put the filename address in your TO: section of your Email program. GEN autoresponder
ignores all the rest, though your EMail program may require a subject and/or content. You
can put anything in those areas.

They are in ASCII format. Soon, we will have an FTP site were anyone will be able
to download, ASCII, Windows Write and Word for Windows 6.0 documents.
