SURVEY

____On-line Real Time Communication____


The purpose of this survey is to collect data regarding the use of on-line
real-time communication tools and its perceived effects. The information
collected during the period of research, will be analysed and used as a basis
for the formulation of hypothesis, on the effects of on-line real-time
communication.

The research is being conducted by Diana Silva, as a requirement for the
Honours component of the degree, Bachelor of Information and Communication
Technology.
The project is currently being supervised by Michael Lawrence-Slater,a
lecturer at the University of Wollongong.


The survey will take approximately 15 minutes and all answers will be strictly
confidential. I apologise if some of the questions become repetitive, please
try to answer each section individually.

Thank you for taking this survey, your cooperation is very much appreciated
