Absolute Patience is a superb collection of 400 solitaire card games.
Play classic games like Klondike, Freecell, Spider, but also many
solitaires found nowhere else.
Most games come with optional rules for a grand total of 600 variants.

Absolute Patience features :
- Photo-realistic card graphics in 3 sizes.
- High quality sounds.
- Multi-levels unlimited undos.
- A performant analyser to assist you during play.
- Detailed scores and statistics.
