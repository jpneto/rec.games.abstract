Here goes one little game of Gonnect:

GONNECT[7x7]: Joao Neto - Jim Gillogly
1. b5 2. d4 3. d5 4. e5 5. c4 6. d3
7. d2 8. e2 9. e6 10. e6 11. e7 12. f7
13. e4 14. f4 15. c3 16. e3 17. c2 18. e1
19. d7 20. f6 21. d1 22. e4 0-1

notes:
1. b5 [Try to balance the game, in order to stay black]
2. d4 <Threaten immediate N connection with good defensive potential.>
4. e5 <Appears to be forced to prevent strong E connection.>
6. d3 <Rookwise connections in potential win direction are quite
threatening.>
8. e2 <Also possible is c2, threatening E-W direction.>
9. d6 [Try to avoid immediate white connection with
North. After 9.e6?: d6 c5 f6...]
<However, # may get mileage out of the ko at 9. e6 10. d6 11. c6...
depending on how ko works in Gonnect.>
10. e6 <Try to nail down the N connection.>
11. e7 [It turned into a race game, who connects 1st? white on the lead...]
14. f4 <The tactics feel rather go-like.>
