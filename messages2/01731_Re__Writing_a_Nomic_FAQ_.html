>What is Mao? A Nomic variant?

I've never actually played Nomic, but I'm told that Mao originated from it with
the major changes that
A) New players are not told the initial rule set (which is slightly different
from the Nomic one, and varies slightly depending on which group is playing it)
B) When someone goes out, they invent a new rule, which they do not tell the
other players.

I believe that Mao was developed by some Canadians, and introduced to the
world in Summer 1992. It is certainly rapidly becoming widespread in the UK,
being played in at least 3 universities, and several schools.
