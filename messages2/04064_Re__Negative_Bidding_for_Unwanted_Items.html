> I'll give this a try the next time I play Medici. There are often a number of
> sets of goods that nobody wants, because they have negative value (in that they

Negative bidding is certainly an option in such cases.
However, one should be aware of the game design's
intentions and the change of fun. Sometimes items
must be sold be all means, sometimes it just makes
strategy simpler. Negative bidding can be elegant or
too elegant, depending on context rules.
