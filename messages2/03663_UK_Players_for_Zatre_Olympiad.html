UK Players for Zatre Olympiad

From: Paul Evans <pevans@sfcp.co.uk>
Date: 22 07 1997 00:00:00 GMT


The Zatre Federation will be holding its Olympiad as part of the Mind
Sports Olympiad in August. Perner Publishing Group (which publishes
Zatre) is keen for there to be some UK representation in this event -
ideally a UK team chosen from some form of preliminary event, but maybe
just a few representative players.

If you are interested, but don't know the game, Jeremy Lane of PPG UK
can supply copies to learn/practice on. Contact him direct at
JEREMY@primedgn.demon.co.uk or on 01993 843737 and he can send out the
game immediately.

Please let me know if you're interested.
