I am auctioning a game called Arcadia. This is an abstract game that
has no board as such, but instead creates a board with small hexagons
as the game is played. The hexagons are of high-strength plastic,
about 1.25" (3cm) across from edge to edge. They are rigded in such a
way that by placing three together, you create a platform upon which a
fourth hexagon can be securely stacked, 1/3 on each of the base three.

The game was sold in individual colors - I have three: blue, red, and
gray. Each color comes in a separate drawstring bag, and each contains
a copy of the rules. Each bag consists of 40 hexagons - some have
markings on them to denote rank.

Rules for two different games are included: Genera and Fusion. Genera
is played with a single color; Fusion requires a separate color for
each player (hence you could play with two or three with this set).
Both games involve scoring on the upper levels, so initial play is
primarily set-up for later scoring.

I bought the game 4 years ago at GenCon. I don't know if Arcadia Games
is still in business - I've never seen it anywhere else. I believe I
paid $10/bag for the game (while the parts are plastic, they are
high-quality, rigid - almost unbreakable - plastic).
