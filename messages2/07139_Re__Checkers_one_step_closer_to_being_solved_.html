And so it may, but whether that makes sense in a draughts context is another
question. Arie van der Stoep argues, in excruciating but plausible detail,
for a derivation for Fr. (JEU DES) DAMES rom Fr. (JEU DE) DAMES from MFr.
DAM = embankment, from MDu. DAM = embankment, originally denoting the
crownhead or promotion row. See his doctoral dissertation Over de herkomst
van het woord damspel, Leiden University, 1977.
