Twixt at the WBC

From: twixtplayer@yahoo.com (David Bush)

On Sunday, August 1, the last day of the World Boardgaming
Championships, near Baltimore, I will be running a Twixt "demo" from 9
am to noon. I will be offering handicaps, playing simultaneously on my
three boards. There will be free puzzle sheets and flyers for the Mind
Sports Olympiad, to be held in London later in August. If anyone is
feeling ambitious, we could glom my two 3M sets together with a ninth
panel I made, and play a monster 36x36 game...

I will also probably be around on Saturday, looking for opponents in
either Twixt or something else. I will be bringing: Battle for Moscow,
Broadside, Equations, Hex, Jungle, Master Mind, Meditation Game, Nine
Men's Morris, Othello, Ploy, Shogi, Sleuth, Tablut, Universe.

Thanks for your time!

David Bush
