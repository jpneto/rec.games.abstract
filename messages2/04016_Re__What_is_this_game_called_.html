I don't think it has any other well-known names, but one of the names
is "logic/weighing/balance.p in the rec.puzzles archive" :-) (see the
rec.puzzles FAQ for instructions for accessing the archive). The
answer there tells you how many balls you can handle with n uses of
a scale ((3^n-3)/2, by the way) and gives you instructions for
finding the odd ball.

The puzzle doesn't appear to have any useful application, apart from
being shown in every undergraduate computer science course in the
world as a precursor to sorting algorithms which attempt to minimise
comparison operations :-)
