The problem: There are lots of potentially interesting games out
there on the web which are going nowhere because they lack
a critical mass of players.

Proposed solution: Form an informal group of gamers, and coordinate
"mass attacks" on interesting games once in a while.

If you are interested in participating, or have a candidate game
in mind, drop me a line and I'll add you to my list of interested
parties.

At the moment, my goal is to solve the the meta-problem of getting
a critical mass of players interested in solving the problem of
getting a critical mass of players.
