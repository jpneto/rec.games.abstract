> So why the name of this group? Basically, the charter and purpose of
> the group were fairly clear right from the start of the discussion
> period: we wanted a group in which to discuss perfect-knowledge games
> which didn't have groups of their own (the last, by the way, is the
> only reason why games like chess and go are not on-topic here). The
> name caused us some difficulty, and a number of choices were
> considered before rec.games.abstract was chosen. It isn't by any means
> perfect as a name, but within the limitations on how newsgroups are
> named, we couldn't find a better name. (Some examples of such
> limitations: rec.games.perfect-knowledge is not allowable because name
> components are restricted to 14 characters in length.
> rec.games.perfect.knowledge would not be acceptable because newsgroup
> names are supposed to be hierarchical, with each component qualifying
> the previous ones: rec.games.perfect.*, if it exists at all, should be
> a hierarchy about "perfect games", and "perfect-knowledge games" are
> not a sub-topic of "perfect games". Similar problems exist with
> rec.games.knowledge.perfect.)
