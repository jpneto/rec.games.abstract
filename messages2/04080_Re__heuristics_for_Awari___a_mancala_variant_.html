Actually, it was not clear to me what heuristic was being applied in the code.
There was no documentation, and it's been a little too long since I've used
basic to want to dig through it. :-)

I already have an alpha-beta search implemented. Typically I search about
200000 positions each second on a PII-300. But my heuristic for how good a
position is is simply the difference between the number of stones one guy has
captured and the number of stones the other guy has captured. Well, also I
add a large bonus if the position is provably winning, e.g. there are not
enough stones left on the board for the other guy to catch up.

So what I am looking for is some other, additional factors that are good to
take into account when assessing a particular position, and the relative
weight they should be assigned compared with the worth of a stone.
