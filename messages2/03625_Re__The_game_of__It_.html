That's not how horserace betting works -- instead horse races use
a paramutual pool.

The easiest way to explain is that if there's P dollars bet (in total)
on the outcome of the race, and M of those dollars are bet on me,
then those bets are calculated as though it were at odds of (P-M)/M:1 odds.
I.e. if half the money/people bet on me, then they get odds of 1:1. If
only a tenth of the money/people bet on me, then they get odds of 9:1.)

This is made a little bit more complicated by the fact that (a) the bookies
need to make money, and (b) with modern tote betting, you make your bet
at the odds current *when you made the bet* and not against the final
pool.

... they'll all get no profits (effective odds of 0:1) unless some of the
non-It marks bet.
