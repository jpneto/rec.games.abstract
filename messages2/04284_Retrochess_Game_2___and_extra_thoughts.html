Taking the question of QSCGZ of what are we doing at the start
I thought of another variation on this theme:

a match is made of two prog. retrochess games. In each board,
one player tries to delay the ending (ie, avoid reaching the initial
FIDE position) and the other tries to get it. The player that
finished first in his board wins! This way, there is already a
tactical purpose for the first unmoves
