Hiho,
Well the World Championships got underway this evening at the
Rydges hotel in Melbourne Australia. All the 14 participants were present
at the opening coctail party, as well as representatives from Mattel and
Anjar. During this function, the tournament director Emmanuel Lazard drew
the players for the first round (It looks rather like a likely finishing
position, don't you think :) ) Following is the list of players as they
were drawn from the box, and the table determining who plays who during
the 13 round round-robin contest. The first 7 rounds will be played on
Thursday begining at 9:00am (Australian time IOS +10 hrs) and the remaining
6 will be played on Friday, with the finals to be held on Saturday before
the concluding dinner to be held at the Royal Melbourne Zoo.
With any luck, I will have some results for you all at about the same time
tomorrow.
