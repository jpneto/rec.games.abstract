I have not followed the previous discussion, as I was on a holiday.
However, I assume that Suicide Chess is the chess variant where the pieces
move as in chess, captures are forced, and the first player which cannot move
wins (either because he has no pieces, or his pieces cannot move) wins. The
king is not a special piece.

For that game I once wrote a program in one day (together with Barney Pell
who was visting me) based solely on proof-number search. This program shows
within just a few seconds that the following three opening moves lose:
1. e2-e4, 1. d2-d3 and 1.d2-d4.

So if we are talking about the same game, Bill, I'd like to take you on
on your offer to play e4 against my program. This may give me a chance to
get even after you beat my Awari program (written together with Maarten van der
Meulen) by uncovering a mistake in it :-).

Victor Allis.
