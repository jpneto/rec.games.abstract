A lot of people have written me asking me for hints, or instructions.
Well, here is the deal-
There are basicly 2 ways to play the game.
You can either really 'hack' it (which is entirely legal),
or you can solve the codes (or puzzles) which are presented to you
at each level.
Since there are no rules, any way you reach the goal is allowed.
So far, no one has been able to really 'hack' it, but if someone
does, they will find instructions on what to do to prove they did it.
Most people go up the levels by solving the codes. I guarantee that each
code
has a solution that is solveable, and you don't have to know me to solve it.
11 of my current 12 current players with security level 4 clearance are
complete strangers,
from all over the world, and I have not helped them in any way whatsoever.
So it is dooable.
Most people cannot pass the first level because they do not recognize the
'code'
or 'puzzle' when they see it.
If you recognize it, you will immediately know the answer.
This is why I cannot give hints or a 'spoiler'.
If I did, it would be very easy to pass, and the game's purpose is to
provide
an intellectual challenge, not to be easy.
