Well, I might be confused, but as far as I can see, by Leroy's rules,
player 1 wins if the repeat happens on an odd-numbered move, and
player 2 wins if the repeat happens on an even-numbered move.

So consider the strategy 1,1,2,1.

If player 1 adopts that strategy, then player 2 defeats that by
choosing 1,1.

If player 2 adopts that strategy, hmmm ...

Ok, I see -- player 1 loses by force.

player 1), but actually it's a winning strategy for player 2 (which I
