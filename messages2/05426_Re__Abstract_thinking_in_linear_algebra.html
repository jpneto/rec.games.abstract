This is not the wrong forum. In my opinion, and this was
true then, the problem was that the approach taken was not
abstract enough. I think that Venn diagrams are a poor way
to teach, but might be used to give an alternative approach.
But I think that not enough abstraction was used; the ordinal
properties of the integers are easy to understand, and while
the operations are not as simply, or should we say cutely,
defined, they become easier to work with.

You probably had an approach which was dumbed down in the
vain hope that the teachers could handle it. When it was
introduced, pointing out that the teachers who could not
quickly learn the abstract concepts did not have any
understanding of mathematics might have worked. The new
math arose from clear evidence that learning arithmetic
operations taught no mathematical concepts.
