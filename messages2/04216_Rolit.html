Have any of you tried the game Rolit by Pressman? It's just a 4-player variant
of Othello / Reversi where little balls are put into pits and rolled to your
color when outflanked. I've heard of an extensive literature about 2-player
Reversi, but nothing so far about more players. I would guess that the 2nd
move advantage would be very different in the 4-player game, and in fact, even
in a 3-player game it's not too unlikely that one player will be eliminated
early on. Thoughts?
