Richard's PBeM Server (pbmserv@gamerz.net) exists so that we can play
a variety of games by eMail.

The latest addition to the server is DarkChess. You only see those
parts of the board to which one of your pieces can move or capture.

The complete list of games currently supported are:

Abalone Amazons Andantino Ataxx and Backgammon
Hexxagon
Chess,
QuickChess,
Capablanca Chex Connect4 and Connect4x4 Connexxions
and Connectris
GrandChess

DarkChess Dots and Boxes Entropy Fanorona Go

Gomoku Hex Jungle Lines-of-Action Neutron

Othello, Reversi
Oddthello and Pente and Philosopher's Plotto
Reverse-Othello Keryo-Pente Football

Quadrature Qubic Renju Rings Score4

Shogi and Tanbo,
MiniShogi Spangles Survival Susan Tanbo3D and
Hexbo

Terrace Trax, 8x8Trax and Tug-of-War TwixT
LoopTrax

For complete information on how to signup and play games via this server
send a message to pbmserv@gamerz.net with 'help' (minus the quotes) as
the SUBJECT line, or visit http://www.gamerz.net/pbmserv/
