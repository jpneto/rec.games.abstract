Nope, I'm sorry, but Mao and Eleusis are not the same game. They share a
similar spirit, namely that they involve the creative invention of rules
as the game progresses, but Mao is considerably nastier (and a lot more fun).
It is possibly the most interesting game I know, perhaps with the exception
of Nomic, which I have never actually played.

Hm... I hesitate to post the rules of the game to a newsgroup. I feel
hesitant to even say what they are to my own mother. But I *suppose* that
if you wanted the rules, you could mail me. My reasoning is that although
I violate a tenet of the game by writing down the rules, the spirit of Mao
benefits overall by having the game become more widespread.
