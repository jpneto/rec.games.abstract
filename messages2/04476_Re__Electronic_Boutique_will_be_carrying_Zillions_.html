Before I go on, let me start off by saying the program plays A NEAR
UNLIMITED NUMBER OF ABSTRACT STRATEGY GAMES COMPETITIVELY.

And then, on and on you hear bitching about how come companies supply
games with great eye candy but braindead AIs. I assume you would
rather have a braindead AI and great graphics, or a challenge from an
AI that cheats up the wazzoo.

Well, bucko, I have a hoard of PC games, both with great graphics, and
good AI, and some that are just plain fun (braindead AI, but cool
eyecandy). I also have a Dreamcast, N64, a PSX, and whatever else. My
PC is a P2-450, with a TNT2 Ultra board, along with a Voodoo 2. I have
respect for this program and own it.

But, I also have respect for Zillions. If you haven't play it, or
looked over it, then you don't have a clue. This isn't just a "one
size fits all" package where you get X games and that is it. It is a
program that lets you add a near unlimited number of abstract strategy
games to it, and you can code your own. In most cases, the AI will
trounce you badly.

So, back to my original post. If you are one of these types of people,
Zillions would interest you:

1. A person looking for a versitile strategy game with a challenging AI
that doesn't cheat, for whom eyecandy doesn't matter. This can also
include a hardcore strategy gamer.

2. A fan of abstract strategy games in general.

3. A fan of an obscure abstract strategy game for which there is no AI
program out there.

Those who should avoid Zillions:
1. People who want a braindead computer AI.

2. People who want neato animation, a great 3D engine, and eyecandy up
the wazzoo.

3. People who can't stand abstract strategy games.

4. d00ds.

Ok, am I to assume you are all 4 of these?
