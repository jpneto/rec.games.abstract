> A while ago I heard about a game where the idea of the game is to make
> up new rules until you are the winner.
>
> Does anybody know what this game is called and what the initial rules
> are, or where I can find out what they are.

The game is called Nomic. Although it was first invented in a form that
can be played by players sitting round a table, there are currently many
games of Nomic being played with modified rules via Email. Many such
games have a lot of documentation available on the web. For a list of
sites check
<http://www.webring.org/cgi-bin/webring?ring=nomicring;list>

If you don't have web access, the Nomic FAQ is posted regularly to this
newsgroup, so just check whether you can find a copy, or wait until its
next posting.

It's good fun - if you want to try it, let me know (I'm playing in
several Email Nomics of varying intensity and complexity).
