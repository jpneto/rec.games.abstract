Octi is an abstract strategy game in which the players confront the
following trade off: move a piece, add another piece to the board,
or promote a piece currently on the board. Simple rules, quick to
learn, but enough depth for someone looking for a new game addiction.

Check out the rules or play other players on-line (best on Fridays and
Sundays) at www.octi.net.
