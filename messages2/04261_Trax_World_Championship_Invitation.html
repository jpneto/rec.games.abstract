Invitation to all Trax players:

The Trax World Championship is held every second year on even years. It
consists of a series of tournaments:

Regional Qualifier Tournaments (select 2 candidates from each region)
Candidate's Tournament (selects a challenger)
Challenge Match (the challenger plays the incumbent champion).

The Regional Qualifier Tournaments are open to all players in a
geographical region. This is an invitation to play in your regional
qualifier.

Regional Qualifier Tournament Directors:
Zone 1: North and South America: Mel Nicholson, mel@CSUA.Berkeley.EDU
Zone 2: Europe, Africa & Middle East: Martin Pedersen, tusk@trax.dk
Zone 3: Asia, Australasia, Oceania: Donald Bailey,
d.g.bailey@xtra.co.nz

To enter, or for more details on the format of the regional tournament,
please contact the tournament director for your region. Entries close
February 29, 2000.
The web page for the World Championship series is
http://www.traxgame.com/tourneys/wchamp.html
