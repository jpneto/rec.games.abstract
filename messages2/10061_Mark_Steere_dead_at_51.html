Mark Steere dead at 51

From: Jackspritz <schackowitz@live.com>
Date: Thu, 11 Nov 2010 01:34:22 -0800 (PST)

Today Mark Steere died in his Palo Alto home from a rare disease,
Arimaa Gipfia and a humiliating drug overdose. Of course Steere was
known worldwide for designing the top-selling board games, Sorry and
Trouble, Take That edition. Some of you might also remember him as the
actor who played Goofball Goofball Ghali in Hogan's Island the
musical. In spite of Steere's death, a man appears to be impersonating
Steere online, using a facebook account with his name and posting
lude and insulting material to various online forums in an attempt to
diminish Steere's prominence. Mark Steere's family has requested that
the accounts be closed and the perpetrator step forward and formally
apologize for his actions. Steere will be remembered for his soft
temperament, jolly disposition, philanthropic acts and goodwill to all
men.

RGA has an exclusive special, Mark Steere, The Man Behind The Dream.
Our chief cultural correspondent Bill Taylor is at Steere's home in
sunny Palo Alto California to take a guided tour and see how this
great man lived. Bill what do you have for us?
