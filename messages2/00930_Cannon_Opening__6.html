10 S5+1 ...
Normally, in Cannon vs Cannon opening, we do not open up the middle file by
advancing the solider. In this special case however, Red S5+1 intends to
threaten Black's 7th file Cannon. On the other hand, if Red play H8+7 R12;
C86 R2+4; Black even up the position.

danger.
