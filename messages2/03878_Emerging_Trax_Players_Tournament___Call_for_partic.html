EMERGING PLAYERS TRAX TOURNAMENT - CALL FOR PARTICIPATION

This tournament is being arranged for new and lower ranked Trax players who
would like to improve their play. To help in this, all games will be commented
on by top players.

ELIGIBILITY: The tournament is open to all new players, provisional players
(less than 20 games on Richards PBMServer) or established players with a
rating below 1750.

REGISTRATION: Players who wish to participate should register with me
(D.G.Bailey@massey.ac.nz) by Thursday 14 May.

FORMAT: All games are to be played on Richards PBMServer, with a 5 day per
move time limit. Offline analysis of the game is permitted.
Round 1: Players will be divided into pools of 6-8 where they play each
other player in that pool. The top half of each pool will
progress to round 2. The bottom half will progress to a
consolation round (round 2C).
Round 2: Again players will play a round robin in pools of 6-9 players.
The top 2 + ties from each pool progress to round 3.
Round 3: A single pool. The top 2 players (ties separated on countback)
participate in a final match.
Final: The top two players from round 3 play 2 further games against
each other, making a best of 3 (including their round 3 game).
Round 2C The consolation round will be a round robin in pools of 6-9
players, with the top 2+ties progressing to round 3C.
Round 3C The consolation final round will be a single pool. The top
player being the consolation winner (ties separated on
countback).

COMMENTS: All players will receive a commentary of their games once completed.
These comments will be provided by top Trax players, and will contain
suggestions on what is good and what is bad about each move, with
possible better moves given with reasons.

PRIZES: Winner will receive a Trax set, and "Trax strategy for beginners".
Runner up will receive a Trax set.
Consolation winner will receive "Trax strategy for beginners".
The new player (who started with a provisional ranking) who wins the
most games in the first 2 rounds will receive Doby III.

NOTES:
If you have never played Trax, the rules and many other details are available
from the Trax Resource Index: http://ia-spare.massey.ac.nz/trax/index.html
For rules, look under FAQ.

To play you need to sign up on Richard's PBMServer. For information on this,
see http://www.gamerz.net/~pbmserv/
