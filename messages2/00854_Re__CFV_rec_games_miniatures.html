And should be ignored until the official CFV is posted in news.announce.newgroups.

People, as strong as the urge is to 'get out the vote' resist the urge to post
a companion to your CFV which went to n.a.n. There will be a lag time of up to
a week (or more) and the moderator will adjust the CFV to went he posts it to
n.a.n; the guidelines say anything prior to this date is invalid. We already
have seen another case (comp.sys.atari.advocacy) which also ignored this and
posted a companion CFV; as it turns out there is a problem with their CFV and
it has not been posted; under the guidelines, all votes prior to the offical
CFV must be tossed.
