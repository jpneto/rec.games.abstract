The Most Challenging Puzzle I've Ever Encountered

From: Gabriel Velasco <gabriel_velasco@ibm.net>
Date: 06 01 1998 00:00:00 GMT


I recently purchased a puzzle that I think is called Atomic Chaos. It's
made up of 6 tubes of varying length, each containing from 1 to 6
colored balls depending on the length of the tube. The tubes are
arranged vertically in a circle in order of size. You can twist the top
halves of the tubes to line up with different tubes, and you can turn
the whole thing over so that the balls drop out of the bottom halves of
the tubes into the top halves of the tubes. Then, you can twist the
tubes around again to redistribute the colored balls into different
tubes and in different orders. The puzzle starts with all the balls of
the same color in the same tubes. You give it a couple of twists and
flips and it's pretty well mixed up. The goal is to get them in order
again. This is an extremely challenging puzzle. (At least for me, it
is.) Has anyone else played with this? Has anyone come up with some
canonical moves for it?
