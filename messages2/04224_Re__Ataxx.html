Richard Rognlie <rrognlie@gamerz.net> writes:
> The Rules for Ataxx

> The object of the game is to have the majority of your colour discs on
> the board at the end of the game.

You didn't define when the game ends. That seems fairly important.
What if my opponent can't move, and I choose to make moves that make the
game last forever, because if I allowed the board to fill up, I would
lose? What if the board repeats in some other way (admittedly this
doesn't seem very likely).

A ko-style rule would probably suffice. As long as you don't allow
positions to repeat, you can say that the game ends when the board is
filled.
