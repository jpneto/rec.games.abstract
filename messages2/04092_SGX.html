Please register your web site with SGX.

SGX (Simulation/Gaming Exchange) is a clearing house
for web sites related to simulation/gaming. It is a
kind of specialised search engine for simulation/gaming.
One of its main features is that the entries are
controlled by the data provider (i.e., people like
yourself). You will see this and more when you
visit the site.


The URL is http://sg.comp.nus.edu.sg/ <http://sg.comp.nus.edu.sg/>
