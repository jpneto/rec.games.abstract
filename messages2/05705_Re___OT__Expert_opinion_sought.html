Not an abstract game, but here are some observations:

- Why not have a link to a description of the game? I eventually edited the
URL to find the description at bigfun.net/meba ; but it appears that either
the game, or flash, prevents my browser from going to new URLs. So I had to
quit the browser and restart it. I think this is probably a problem with
task management in your program.

- The description that I *did* find seems like more of a sales pitch to
possible investors than to a player. For example:
Colors are bright, sophisticated - tangerine, pale raspberry and
sour-apple green. Surfaces are shiny and round - in the Big Fun
style.

The music starts out charming and cheerful - sweet carbonated
refreshment. As the game gets on, the musical cocktail gets more
intoxicating - with some panic in the mix - and the tracks edge
more techno. Check other Big Fun games.

Why should a player want to be told that the colors are sophisticated?

- The claim of "instant play" was not realized on my machine. For 30
seconds or so all I saw was a black box in the middle of the window, and a
link to where I could make a comment.

_ conclusion: the game was very responsive; and it looked like maybe it
was interesting. But it was hard to tell. I'm still not sure how I'm
supposed to play it. It seemed to play too fast; certainly too fast for an
intro level; I was unable to figuer out what my goals as a player were.
