Hello,

Titans is a PBEM run each day. The game client shows the world map and
disposition of forces, the forces consist of large evolving robots - you
get to evolve them, move and fight.

Ideal for war gamers who enjoy a bit of detail to their units, and a bit
of long term strategy coupled with go by go combat.

We are well in to Beta testing now and need loads more testers - its all
free, and testing continues for another month. The game client is written
in Java so you can even play it at work (no install required!). It can
take as little time as a few minutes to play a go if you are rushed.

Go on, give it a shot, and help us out in the process...
