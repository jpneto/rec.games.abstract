MILLIONS HAVE CHANCE TO WIN ON INTERNET'S VIRTUAL GAME SHOW

More than 100,000 Compete for Prizes Usually Found on TV Game Shows
via Riddler.com Game Site on the Internet

Interactive Imaginations has launched Version 3.0 of its increasingly
popular Riddler entertainment site. <http://www.riddler.com> Using
the latest multimedia Internet software, single or multiple players
will now be able to compete for prizes like cars and cruises for the
first time. The games are comprised of riddles, crossword puzzles,
and trivia contests. Prizes are sponsored by Toyota, Snapple, Random
House, Apple,
Microsoft, NBC, Encyclopedia Britannica and Royal Caribbean Cruises,
among others, and include two Toyota RAV 4 sports utility vehicles,
hundreds of CDs from Capitol and Atlantic Records, "long-weekend"
vacations to Miami Beach, Apple Powerbooks, one-year subscriptions to
Britannica Online, software from Microsoft, and Royal Caribbean Cruise
among other prizes.

The Riddler crossword puzzles, which range from easy-to-hard, are
edited by crossword puzzle world champion Stanley Newman, and actually
allow puzzle fanatics around the world to play against each other, in
multi-player competitions. And just in case cyber-surfers felt left
out in the cold, the Riddler site offers a
competitive Internet scavenger hunt, co-sponsored by Internet search
engine Lycos. At the Riddler site players are given a series of clues
to enter as search phrases through Lycos. The clues will send players
through dozens of web sites looking for Riddler "caps," the standard
currency for all the Riddler games.

So click on over to http://www.riddler.com and let the games begin!
