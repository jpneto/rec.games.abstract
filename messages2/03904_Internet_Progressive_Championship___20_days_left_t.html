Only 20 days remain to sign up for the
World Internet Progressive Chess
Championship, the free email championship
to determine who is the best progressive
chess player in the world!

Progressive chess is the fantastic chess
variant where white gets 1 move, black 2,
white 3, black 4, and so on. At first glance,
it seems deceptively simple, but actually
there are tactics and subtleties galore that
make this game particularly well suited
for correspondence play.

Think you have what it takes to win the
championship? Then sign up by sending
an email to hyatt@cs.utk.edu with your
first and last name, email address, and
experience with the game (if any.)

Beginners are particularly welcome. I
expect most of you never to have played
a progressive chess game before. That's
no problem!! So come on and sign up!

For more information about the championship,
and about progressive chess in general,
come visit the WIPCC 98 web page at
http://www.cs.utk.edu/~hyatt/wipcc98/

Did I mention the prizes include a year's
free membership in the Internet Chess Club?
(http://chessclub.com)

See you in the championship!
