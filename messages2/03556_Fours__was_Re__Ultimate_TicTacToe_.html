hey - this is almost a game i came up with years ago. only i played
mine on a hexagonal board and scored it differently:

=============================CUT-here==================================
Fours
invented by Nathan A. Smith
Copyright 1997 Nathan A.Smith

3-in-a-row: too easy, zero points
4 one point
5 5 points
6 15 points
7 35 points
8 70 points
9 126 points
...and so on.

some of you may recognize the 5th diagonal row in Pascal's Triangle.

N!
----------- is the number of different groupings of 4 items taken
(N-4)! 4! taken from N items.

SO THEREFORE the above point table is condensed down to:

1 point for each different uninterrupted 4-in-a-row combination.

where "uninterrupted" means no opponent stone in between.

you play it with Go stones on an order-11 hexagon (331 spots).
NOTE that 11-in-a-row (one whole edge) is worth 330 points.

only 2 rules!

1. you alternate turns placing a stone on an empty spot until
either no empty spots remain or one player resigns.

2. whoever has the most uninterrupted different 4's wins.

==========================================================================
