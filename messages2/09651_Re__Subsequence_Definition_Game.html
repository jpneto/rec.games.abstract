Okay, I see a problem. Players can increase their scores if they had a
definition such as this:
a(k) = 3*k + 1
by stating that their definition is instead:
a(k) = (real part of z)*k + imaginary part of z, where z = 3 + i.

So, maybe we should forbid complex numbers in the definitions. Or
maybe we should allow such definition-making sneakiness?
