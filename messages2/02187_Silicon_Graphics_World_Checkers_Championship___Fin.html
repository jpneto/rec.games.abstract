Silicon Graphics World Checkers Championship - Final Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 25 Aug 1994 16:34:06 GMT

Here are the results for the final two games:

Game 19: Lafferty 1/2 Chinook 1/2
Game 20: Chinook 1/2 Lafferty 1/2

Final point total:

Lafferty: 1 Win 1 Loss 18 Draws 10 Points
Chinook: 1 Win 1 Loss 18 Draws 10 Points

According to the match rules, Chinook retains its title. Congratulations
to Don Lafferty for his fine play.

The Chinook team also expresses their appreciation to Silicon Graphics
for sponsoring the match and to the Boston Computer Museum for providing
an excellent venue.
