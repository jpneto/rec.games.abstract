>3. Could the brain resources of the Internet be mobilised to create such
> game and if so how to go about it?

Yes. We can build operating systems as net collaborations, which are
almost certainly the most complex non-physical systems it is possible
to build. The only chance is that a "perfect" game would be more
complex than an O/S, but since most O/S are so complex that
individuals cannot comprehend the whole, the game may not actually be
playable by humans.

I believe the usual approach with this sort of project is to form a
newsgroup, from which a mailing-list splits off, further mailing-lists
are created for sub-sections of the design. Two FTP sites are created,
which rapidly drift out of sync, along with the ninety-four definitive
home pages. The mailing lists fragment further and the newsgroup
devolves into a slanging match between two to eight disparate groups
of religious zealots with totally opposing ideals while all the actual
workers leave to go elsewhere.
