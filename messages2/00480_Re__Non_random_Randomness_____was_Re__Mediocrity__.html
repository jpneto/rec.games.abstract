Just thought someone should make the distinction between non-random
(i.e. deterministic) and non-UNIFORMLY-random, which is what you folks
are referring to (I think :) ). Things can be random without having
all outcomes be equally likely (see the sum of a roll of two dice).

I have no reason to doubt Neil and Matthew's statements if you replace
non-random with non-uniformly-random. But if you think Monopoly is
deterministic, then either you're playing with different rules than I
am, or you need new dice. :)
