(This newsgroup is intended for games of perfect information - though I
don't know how one is meant to find out. Your game is close to the
opposite.)

You don't specify this, but I assume that each Wannabee is told that it
is a Wannabee, and the It is told that it is It.

So five players do have perfect information, and the other 95 initially
have none. I don't see many of them being foolish enough to make bets.
