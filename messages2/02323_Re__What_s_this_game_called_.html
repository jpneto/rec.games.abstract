There was some discussion of this game hereabouts around six months ago.
Most people called it "The 50 point game", though one person who seemed
to know whereof he spoke called it "Footsteps".

There were suggestions of ways to generalize it to 3, 4, and 6 players
using 2-D boards tiled in triangle, squares, and hexes. It's an
interesting concept-- I haven't had a chance to play it. It strikes me as
similar to GOPS, the card "Game of Pure Strategy".
