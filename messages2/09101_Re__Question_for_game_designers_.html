I think that would be Hy Breasil
(http://www.diku.dk/hjemmesider/ansatte/torbenm/HyBreasil/).

I generally prefer multi-player games over two-player games (the
interactions are more complex in multi-player games), and the fact that
Hy Breasil can be played on several different boards and with a varying
number of players gives more replayability than my abstract game
designs.
