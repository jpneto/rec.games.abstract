REMINDER:

"Giveaway chess", or "Suicide chess", has pieces moving as at chess, but
the king is a standard piece with no special status (and can be taken).
Captures are compulsory, (capturer's choice if more than one is available).
Promotion is (promoter's choice) to any of the five major pieces (KQRBN),
with no restriction as to number of each on the board. The winner is the
first player who can't move (usually as all his pieces have been taken).
------------------

It is quite cute, though not especially challenging.

. . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . .
. . . . . . . . White to move and win.
b . . . N . P B
. . . N . . P .
. R . . R . P .
. . . K . . . .

(Black has only the one piece - the bish on the left.)
