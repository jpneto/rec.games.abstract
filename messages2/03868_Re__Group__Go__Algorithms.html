If only the Euler number of groups is important, then a simple, nonrecursive
counting algorithm is possible.

This comes up in image recognition where the "number of blobs" is an
interesting number, without needing to identify the specific properties
of the individual blobs.

This also comes up in Lines of Action, where number-of-blobs=1
is *almost* the normal win condition.
