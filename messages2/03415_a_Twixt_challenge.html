I recently lost several games of Twixt on Richard's server at:

pbmserv@eiss.erols.com

I hereby assert that I shall no longer be such an easy mark!
I invite one and all to challenge me to a Twixt game. If you're unfamiliar
with the server, send a blank message to it with the SUBJECT line:

help

and then send another blank message to it with the SUBJECT:

help twixt twixt.rules

You could also email me if you're curious. I offer anyone who hasn't played
me before any handicap they want, up to 6 rows, plus first move. Also,
the first player who manages to defeat me in an even game (no rows of
handicap, 'swap' option in effect) will have his/her choice of payment:

*I will post on this newsgroup, and on the server mailing list, an
embarrassing (to me) poem describing how incredibly superior you are
to me.

OR

*I will play you any other game available on the server, even (gack)
Firetop Mountain.

OR

*Perhaps you would have a better idea. As long as it does not involve
money, I'll probably do it.

I should add that with all the Twixt games I already have going, I may
not respond very quickly- maybe one move per week. Also, only one game at a
time per opponent, please. I'm not particularly worried about paying you
off because YOU'LL NEVER BEAT ME IN AN EVEN GAME! NEVER NEVER NEVER!
HAH! And that also goes for all my current opponents who are playing me an
