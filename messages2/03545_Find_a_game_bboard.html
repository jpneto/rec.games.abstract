Wanted: a bboard-like (java?) application to help email game players
find each other, without being flooded by "I need a game" mail. The
general idea is:

Maintain a database of players and email addresses.
When you're looking for a game, sign in and toggle the "looking" bit.
Names (and other relevant information) of players seeking games are displayed
To start a game, sign in and send a proposal to the chosen opponent.
(the email addresses of players are not displayed publicly to prevent spam.)

That's the minimum, but obviously, much more can be added to
enhance the community effect.

I'm looking for likely starting points; existing programs that have a
framework compatible with this idea.
