Couple of abstract games to try out at HigherGames.
(www.highergames.com).

- Cinko
- Speed Chebache
- Checkers 2000
- Cube Checkers

These game are possible to play without the computer but can be
difficult governing the rules without one, especially Cinko and
Chebache.
All games were created with Flash so platform should not be a issue,
at least in most cases. All have AI built-in players except Chebache.
