jpn@di.fc.ul.pt (Joo Pedro Neto) writes:


> I invented a Hex variant, called Nex with the following rules:
>
> =================================
> [NEX] On a Hex board, each player may either:
> * Drop a stone of his color plus a neutral stone
> * Turn two neutral stones into stones of his color and then turn
> another stone of his color into a neutral stone.
>
> Goals like hex: make a path with your stones or with
> neutral stones between your two edges
> =================================

> Anyway, this idea (drop a 1 friend + 1 neutral OR flip 1 friend + 2
> neutrals) can be extended to many different games. I think that games
> where each move is a drop are excellent targets like Go, Gomoku or
> Hex. Do you people have any suggestions?

The only (minor) problem I see is that you can establish connections
for both sides in one move by dropping a neutral stone, so you don't
have the unique-win property of Hex. What happens if two connections
are established simultaniously? Is the game a draw, or does the
player who made the move win?
