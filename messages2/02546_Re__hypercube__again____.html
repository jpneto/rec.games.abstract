in three dimensions, rooms are normally oriented so that the
floor of the room lies in a plane tangent to the equipotential surface
of the local gravitational field.

so, in four dimensions, the floor, which would be a cube, would lie
within a space tangent to the eqiupotential hypersurface of the local
gravitational field.
