Decide on a subset S of the natural numbers. This decides the "shape"
of the "playing field".

The players alternate naming members of S.

They cannot name a number which is a multiple of an earlier number.
(Thus N is only legal as a first move)
The player naming 1 loses.

If one uses S={2^i*3^j}, this corresponds to a two-dimensional game of
Chomp.

Playing on the full set of natural numbers is impractical since there
is always more primes and the game will never end. (Think infintity-
dimensional Chomp...)
