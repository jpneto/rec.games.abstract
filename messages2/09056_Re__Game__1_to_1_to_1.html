Yes, 1 is coprime to all integers, including even to itself.


I think it might make some sense to have a rule change that players
use only odd integers. (1 then 3 then 5 then 7 then 9, etc.) Otherwise
it might be too easy to play defense by players laying down walls of
even numbers around their 1's. (I know that he/she could not return to
his/her own 1, in that case. But too many games would end by all
players but one forfeiting, instead of by a player visiting all of the
1's, if the rules were that players are to write even integers on even-
numbered moves. In that case, the name of this game wouldn't be very
appropriate.)
