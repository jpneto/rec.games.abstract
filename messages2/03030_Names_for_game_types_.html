A game which has neither elements of chance nor information known
to some players but not others is called a game of perfect information
(If I remember right--it's been awhile. Please correct me
if I'm wrong). In a game of perfect information, such as chess, the outcome can
theoretically be proven to be one fixed outcome with ideal play
by both players.

I'm wondering if there are terms for the other possible types of
games based on the information in the game? i.e.,

games with elements of chance but no hidden information (e.g.
backgammon, Monopoly)

games with hidden information but no chance (e.g. Stratego)

games with hidden information and chance (e.g. almost all card games)
