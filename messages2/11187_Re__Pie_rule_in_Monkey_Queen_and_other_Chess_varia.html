An "overwhelming" first move advantage will not make any game popular.
The magnitude of the errors beginners make at Chess largely
outmeasures the first move advantage. By the time they're pro's it
matters of course, but the Chess community at large doesn't seem to
see it as a problem, nor do the results indicate there's a major
problem (as they do in Draughts).
Regarding not wanting a game that people only want to play if they can
move first, you don't have to make one, nor play one so I don't see
any major problem there either.
