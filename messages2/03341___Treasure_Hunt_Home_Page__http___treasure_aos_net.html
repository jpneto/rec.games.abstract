Are you looking for a new and completely original way to have fun and
stretch your mental powers out a bit? Well that's what we're here for.
We have created a fictional organization and lore for you to play in and
relate to over the internet. However, don't count on your web prowess
alone to solve these puzzles. This is a game designed to test you're
knowledge, ingenuity, and patience. We provide clues to events and
places that you must decipher and solve. If you are the first to report
back to us via E-mail with the correct information you will win a cash
prize and a trophy. The starting cash prize is $100.00, however, the
more
people who play and the longer the game runs, the more the cash prize
will rise over time. Among the clues will be a pointer to the exact
detail
we need. You can play from anywhere, all you need is great resouce
material or a nearby public library and the desire to solve difficult
puzzels. In each game we will be looking for a specific detail. You may
get close without winning. For instance if we are looking for the name
of
Paul Revere's horse and you come up with the name of the man who lent
Paul Revere the horse you will be close but you will not win. It will
always be a good idea if you think you know what we're looking for to
send as much detailed information as possible. We look forward to
featuring the winners on our page in the future.

The price for a given assignment is kept to a minimum, usually under
$3.00, to cover our expenses and the running of the games. You can pay
via credit card or through our 900 number. Once you pay for a given
assignment you never have to pay for information pertaining to that
assignment again. Each assignment is a separate charge. If we think a
given game is going on for too long we will provide additional clues to
all
registered players via E-Mail. Only registered players can win. Please
feel free to browse the page in advance of taking an assignment. There
is
no charge for looking around and learning all you can, only for taking
on
an assignment.
