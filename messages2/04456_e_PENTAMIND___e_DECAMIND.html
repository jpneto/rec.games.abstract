I'd like to propose this funny idea that I elaborated with some
friends during the last mounths (pedro, marconte, janus...).

I mean a poly-competition were it is not important to be a very strong
player in a game, but a poly-games lover.

So I suggest a list of 15 games, so 15 tournaments, but if you are
interested you MUST play at least 10 tournaments (the best 10 results
are your score in the e-decamind, the best 5 in the e-pentamind). It
is strongly suggested to play this tournament if you can play at least
5 move a week for every match.

I don't know how many people could be attracted to this event,
probably not so much, so it is a little bit difficult to imagine how
every single event will be organized.
As you can imagine a tournament with 8 people is very different with a
tournament with 40... anyway the idea could be:
every tournament has stages where about 6-8 people play a round
robin... then the first 1 or 2 player of every group play another
round robin and so on.


Groups will be organized with the help of the Richard's ratings, when
possible. The goal is not to have Kasparov and Karpov in the same
group in the first round robin.


The final classement will give points with the rule:
100(p+1-n)/p WHERE p = NUMBER OF PLAYERS , AND n=YOURCLASSEMENT


So 100 points to the winner, the second one it depends to "how many
people"...


Obviously it is not possible to be helped from software... This is the
reason why we choose rothello and not othello (and so on), anyway it
is obvious that it is not possible to check anything... we trust. No
prices... just fun! I hope you well understand this point.



There is some choice about the list of the games... I suggest a vote,
and you can vote, if only you are sure to play the e-decamind (I
