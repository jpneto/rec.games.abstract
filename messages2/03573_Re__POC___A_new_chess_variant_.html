W.Taylor@math.canterbury.ac.nz (Bill Taylor) wrote:

>P.O.C. - Progressive Orthodox Chess
>=====================================

>A bright new chess variant.

>This game was invented by Joao Neto and myself, to answer two joint
>needs we felt in a chess variant:-

>(1) the game record should be exactly as for orthodox chess;
>(2) it should be possible to play moves for your opponent's pieces.

>We finally managed to reconcile these goals, and (seemingly) create
>a very interesting new game, by using a simple variant of the
>PROGRESSIVE concept. Hardly a variant at all, really...

>RULES
>-----
>White has the white pieces, Black has the black pieces.
>A player loses when his king is checkmated.
>All individual moves must be legal chess moves, but are played
>by the players in increasing series, (progressive-chess style).

>Firstly,
>White plays one move (a white man), then
>Black plays 2 moves (a black man then a white), then
>White plays 3 moves (b,w,b), then
>Black plays 4 moves (w,b,w,b); and so on.
>------


Actually, this is not a new game. These are the exact rules
to *Progressive 007 Chess*, invented by, er, well, me, in 1995 --

http://www.cs.ruu.nl/~hansb/d.chessvar/G007.html#progressive

I have played the progressive version a only few times. The Detente
version got a lot more play at the time.
