Even if I'm not a big fan of chess retroanalysis,
I wonder what you think about this chess variant:

===========================
Impossible Chess

1.The FIDE rules apply, except in the following:
2.A moved non king piece can be transformed in any non king piece, if and
only if:
1.The result position is an impossible FIDE position, and
2.Some of the player's pieces must participate on it (Eg, White must
create an impossible position using at least one white piece). There is only
one exception: if the piece changes color to create the position, however
this only counts for that specific turn.
3.A player looses, if he creates a possible board position.
===========================

note: When rule 2 says "any non king piece" this means that the piece may
change its own color (and in that case, the player cannot move it anymore,
since it belongs now to the other player).

note: Rule 2.2 is necessary. If not, if a player creates an impossible
position, the other player would take advantage of it to play normally.

notation: move=x means that after 'move', the piece is transformed into
piece 'x'. If the piece also changes color, append an '^' symbol.

The 'Not So Impossible Chess' variant: Change in rule 2.1 "impossible FIDE
position" by "possible FIDE position" and remove 2.2

===========================
