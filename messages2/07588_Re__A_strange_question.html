literature that's available might not be my cup of tea (e.g., I started

But that's OK, because I realize that I have another bad habit besides
failing to think things through: I rely too much on reading. I'm
reluctant to try anything new unless I've first read an instruction
book on it. I don't trust myself to learn via hands-on experience, so
I always look for books first. And sometimes I never get past the
book-reading stage.

So, the lack of literature on NMM is probably a good thing for me. It
means I'll just have to play the game, watch closely, and learn what
works and what doesn't.

Chess has an abundance of literature; checkers has some; NMM has
little. And that suits me just fine.
