> The n-player card game Toepen (seehttp://www.pagat.com/last/toepen.html) uses something a bit like a
> doubling cube.
>
> Each hand of this game ends by one player winning the last trick, each
> other player then loses one or more lives. Initially the hand is being
> played for one life. At any time, any player who is not the last player
> to have knocked may knock. Each other player must then chose whether to
> fold, paying whatever the current stake is; or to remain in, playing
> for one life higher than previously.
>
> Ok, it's not a doubling cube, it's a +1 cube. You could use a doubling
> cube in the same way.
