Well, I have since tried a bit of play-testing on a 6x6 board. I think
some additional rules to force confrontation are necessary, like:

you must.

Still trying to work out a decent game-ending condition... perhaps
the rule above makes wipe-outs possible.

Another possibility is that each side makes *two* moves at a time. That
would make the game more offensive.
