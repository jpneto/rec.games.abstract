I'm sure you are partly correct. But some sneaky bastards will be smart
enough to take this 'known sewuence' and insert at just the right point
a different move, and the other (first time he encounters it) will make
his automatic move, and lose.

Thus we make sure they all have to think carefully during their
'automatic' sequence, hence not making it automatic anymore.
The game plays on till a draw is truly inevitable.

There's this rule about the three moves that are repeating that creates
a draw. We need to get rid of that draw as well. Simply make it not be
allowed to repeat a move after moving a piece back to it's original
position. And yes, that will force players into a loss, thus making for
less draws, and of course less positions that are agreed upon as being a
draw.
