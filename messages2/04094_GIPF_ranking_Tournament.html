***** GIPF ranking tournament 1999 *****
==========================================

On the games fair " Spellenspektakel VII " in Eindhoven, the Netherlands
the first Dutch GIPF tournament has been held.
GIPF is a great strategic game you dont want to miss.
look for more information about GIPF;

http://www.gipf.com/

The tournament was dominated by the Belgian players. The undisputed
winner was Aksel De Meester with a 100 % score.
