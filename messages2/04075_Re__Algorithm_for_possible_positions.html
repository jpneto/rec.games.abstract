The logic of this escapes me, and you are wrong; it's actually very close
to one.

Consider a board with all 32 original pieces left. Since no captures has
been made, each pawn has to be on it's "own" rank and have a different
coloured pawn "in front" of it. So, on each rank, there are 15 possible
ways to (legally) arrange the two pawns (count them!). So there are
15^8 ways of legally arranging all the pawns.

In how many ways can we arrange the pawns if we ignore legality? Well,
first we place 8 white pawns on 64 squares without regarding order, and
that can be done in 64!/(56!8!) ways. Then we place the black pawns on
the remaining 56 squares, which can be done in 56!/(48!8!). In total
this yields about 6.29e18 ways to arrange the pawns!

So, it's safe to say that the ratio of legal to illegal positions with
the 32 original pieces left on the board is considerably less than
15^8/(6.29e18-15^8) which is about 1 to 2.5 billion! Your ratio,
illegal positions to total positions, is higher than .999999999592.
