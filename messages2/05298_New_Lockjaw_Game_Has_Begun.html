New Lockjaw Game Has Begun

From: JohnnieJYoung@hotmail.com (Johnnie J. Young)
Date: 18 Feb 2002 02:08:38 -0800

The new online mystery game Lockjaw has begun. According to Wired,
the game is going to revolve around the secret activities of a
fictional company called GanMed Biotechnical and its work toward
developing a highly controversial genetic therapy to halt the aging
process.

So far, we have discovered the following in-game web sites:

http://www.ganmed.com
http://www.grrl-e-grrl.com
