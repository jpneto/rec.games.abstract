Well, you've seen this but other r.g.a. people haven't. From my March
column in NOST-Algia:

I've played 3-handed Lines of Action (on a
board of hexagons) and 4-handed, but it recently
occurred to me that an old Ralph Betza idea allows
any number to play. His miniloa, which I think
appeared in NA 168, is played on an n-by-3 board
with pieces on the 1st and 3rd ranks. In 3x5, for
example, Black would be on a3/b1/c3/d1/e3 and
White on a1/b3/c1/d3/e1.
The generalization is not hard to see: for n
players to keep the same number of pieces as in
ortholoa and roughly the same board density, use a
6n-by-5 board, treat it as a cylinder (right and left
edges adjacent) to make all players' positions
equivalent, and offset top from bottom by [n/2].
Thus five players would play on a 30x5 board with
White on a1/c5/f1/h5/etc., Chartreuse b1/d5/g1/i5/
etc., and so on.
