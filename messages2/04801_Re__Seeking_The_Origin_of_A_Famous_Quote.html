Tony:

Although I have always had a great deal of respect for Dr. Tartakower,
both as a man (a true hero of the French Resistance to the Nazis during
the occupation during W.W.II) and a chessmaster, I must nevertheless
disagree with him on this! I believe that a far better formulation is
something like the obvious, almost tautological:

" Strategy Is your Plan for What To Do, Tactics Is How You Accomplish
It."

Why? Because, at least in Go, there is *always* something to do -
although the significance/value of that something, of course, can and
does vary widely from position to position and over time.
