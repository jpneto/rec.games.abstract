Can Internet Play God? - In search of perfect board game.

From: Dr Bronia Grycz <destiny@gmtnet.co.uk>
Date: 04 05 1996 00:00:00 GMT


A score of years ago I was discussing, over a few pints of beer,
the various proofs of the existence of "God". Among these one
medieval "proof" in particular caught my imagination:It was based
on the symmetry in the following statement:

SATOR
AREPO
TENET
OPERA
ROTAS

They apparently believed that no man could possibly devise
such "perfection"

This set me off on a quest for a "perfect" board game. One which
would abstract and model the salient aspects of human life and of
human interactions. About which a few centuries hence the cyber-cafe
philosophers would pronounce: The perfection of this game is the proof
that God exists.

Alas, I failed to devise such game and now seem to be going in circles,
in itself a proof that I am no God. This finally brings me to the object
of this posting: Could the combined knowledge and skills as accumulated
within Internet (a cyber-god, perhaps?)accomplish the task?

My questions are:

1. Does anybody there know about a board game that could lay claim
to be perfect?
2. What the essential attributes of such game would be?
3. Could the brain resources of the Internet be mobilised to create such
game and if so how to go about it?
4. Could all this be possibly done in time for the year 2000 as our
contribution to the new millenium?

I am well aware that this posting invites all sorts of "witty" comments
but I also hope to attract serious views of high-calibre gamers.
For these I thank in advance.

Bronia Grycz (Destiny Games)

SATOR AREPO TENET OPERA ROTAS
