Sun Oct 31st Japanese chess (shogi)

Founding meeting of the association of shogi players in Finland will
be held after
the prizegiving. Everyone interested in shogi is welcome to join.

In Mamba, chess, xiangqi and shogi you can enter the tournament from
the second round
(advance notification required). The unplayed first-round game is
counted as a draw. In case of
odd number of players some players will play one game less.

Prizes will be give after the last round of each tournament. If not
otherwise indicated in the
tournament specifications, each player will play at least four games
(rounds). The final number of
the rounds and the time of the tournament finish depend on the number
of players. Each player
has at least 15 minutes for the start of the game and 15 seconds
increment after each move (if
digital clocks are available). The starting times of rounds are
approximate because the duration of rounds may vary. The increment may
be reduced during a game in the longest games.
