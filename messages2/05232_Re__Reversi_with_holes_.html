Torben gidius Mogensen (torbenm@sjofn.diku.dk) wrote:
: I have earlier proposed another variant: Each disk has a cross printed
: on both sides. When a disk is placed, it can be oriented so the cross
: is like an x or like a +. To turn over a line of the opponents disks,
: the disk of your own colour at the other end must be oriented in the
: same way as the newly placed disk. The disks that are turned over can
: be oriented in any way that the player who does the turning wants.

How does this make the game different? Who/Whom exactly determines
what orientation the captured pieces take? If the player doing the
capturing determines the orientation of the captured pieces then
wouldn't he simply orientate *all* his pieces the same way? I think
you should make it clear that the player whose pieces were captured
is the one to determine orientation. If White captures several Black
pieces then the player playing as Black is the one to flip over his pieces
and turn them to White *and* orientate them the way he wishes. Am I
correct???
