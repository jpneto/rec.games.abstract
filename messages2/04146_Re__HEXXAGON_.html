This game is a variant of the 1990 arcade game ``Ataxx''. This game had
the moves you describe, but was played on a 7x7 board of square cells with
some squares missing (which ones were missing varied from game to game.)
The starting position was

X.....O
.......
.......
.......
.......
.......
O.....X

, and cells were considered to have eight neighbors. There is a long list
of Hexxagon & Ataxx variants at http://www.pressibus.org/ataxx/ .
