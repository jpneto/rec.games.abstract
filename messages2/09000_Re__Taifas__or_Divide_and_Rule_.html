> TAIFAS
>
> An equal goals game without draws. For two players, played with black
> and white stones on a quad board. Each player takes possesion of all
> the stones of one color. On his turn, starting with Black, a player
> must place a stone of either color on an empty square. The pie rule is
> used: after Black's first turn, White has the option of swapping
> sides. When the board is full, the player with the most zones wins. In
> case of a tie, the owner of the last completed zone loses.
>
> Definitions:
>
> A player's zone is a square or an orthogonally adjacent group of
> squares, not occupied by that player, that borders orthogonally only
> on his own stones or on his stones and one or more of the board's
> edges. It is equivalent to Go's concept of territory, but without
> distinction between empty squares and squares occupied by the
> opponent. In the following example, both X's and O's have five zones:
>
> x o x . o
> o o x o .
> . x o . .
> x . x o o
> . x o x .

..but this isn't the end of the game, as the board isn't full yet?


>
> Variant:
>
> Alternatively, you can value every zone 1/n points, n being the number
> of squares that the zone contains. The player with the most points
> win. This adds an additional layer of strategy, but it may be too
> complicated.
