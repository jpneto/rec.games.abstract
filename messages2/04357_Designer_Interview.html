As part of its warm-up to the 4th Mind Sports Olympiad, the MSO is
presenting a series of feature stories and interviews on its website that I
thought might be of interest to this newsgroup.

An in-depth interview with Kris Burm, the inventor of the GIPF project and
a full-time abstract games designer is now up. He describes the problems of
game design, the challenges facing abstract games in general, and gives the
MSO a big dose of constructive criticism.
