If you are interested, I could send you (or anyone else who asks) some
annotated games that refute your claim. The first few plays of a
decent game are not concentrated in one area. You are not required to
link on every move. Just because you put some pegs down, does not mean
you have to include them in your final bridge. In fact, I could send
you a game where I deliberately blew off a large group of mine just to
gain a tempo elsewhere on the board. And I won. You have the whole
board at your disposal; why not use it?

You might be interested in the quarter-board Twixt puzzles at
http://www.gamerz.net/~pbmserv. Go to the Twixt section and scroll
down to find the link. These puzzles will show you many important
tactical patterns. Our discussion here is about opening play on the
full 24x24 board, but you have to understand tactics first in order to
appreciate why good players spread their pegs around in the opening.

If you're still convinced you are right, we could always settle the
issue with an email game...
