Oh, too bad it sounds smelly in Dutch. In English, one meaning is to
strike repeatedly, or to beat. It's sort of a synonym for Wallop,
another related elimination game I invented, which can be summarized
as Pommel starting with a filled board (Clobber-type setup) and all
pieces already promoted. I rather liked the name Pommel and don't
want to have to think of an alternative.
