DESTINY (Stonehenge Ritual) : Amended Rule "Changing the Life Orbits"

From: Dr Bronia Grycz <destiny@gmtnet.co.uk>
Date: 02 05 1996 00:00:00 GMT


Following the replacement of the condemned red/green dice
by the ordinary 1 to 6 dice, the Rule on changing the Life
Orbits now reads:

.. When the last player completed his moves, he transfers
Fortune from the Orbit of Childhood onto the nearest free
location on the Orbit of Youth (marked 2). If there are two
such locations, he throws a die and moves clockwise if odd,
anticlockwise if even, number is thrown.

There is, of course no change in the Rules of the "Chiftains
Game" where the choice of the location ( i.e. clockwise or
anticlockwise) is up to the reigning Fortune's Favourite,
who has paid dearly for this privilege.

I hope this is not too confusing. Cheers and enjoy your game!
