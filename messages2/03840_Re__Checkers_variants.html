I'd recommend getting a copy of R. Wayne Schmittberger's wonderful book "New
Rules for Classic Games." There are many fascinating checkers variants in it.
AND you'll get LOTS of other great games for chess sets, go sets, cards, logic
games, pencil and paper, etc. It's a MUST-HAVE for games enthusiasts.
