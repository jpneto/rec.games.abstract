I was about to reply and say the same thing and I'm glad to see that
someone else said it first. I can't create or envision any board
position or scenario where this would work. Also, since it then would
indeed be the "sucked" player's turn next, he could always simply
retreat the Marshal back to where it was if he/she wanted!


As far as an incentive to attack, I agree... there wasn't much incentive
to do so. One variation to help combat this, which I mention on my Rule
Variations section of my Stratego page, is basically increase the
strength one (or more!) levels on all attacking pieces. A Colonel
attacking now has the power of A General, a General attacking now has
the strength of a Marshal, etc.
