6?? I'm not sure what I was thinking but there are only two.
The Fano plane is more symmetric than it appears from a casual
glance at the 2-d diagram. All points and all lines (including
the "circular" line) are equivalent.

The two positions with no move where at least three distinct
vertices have one seed on them are as follows. The first position
is obtained by placing a seed at any two distinct vertices and
then placing a third seed at any vertex that is not on the unique
line determined by the first two vertices. For the second
position, pick any line L. Then place one seed at each of four
vertices not on line L.

Given an arbitrary position, it's not exactly obvious when
you can move to a position with no following moves. Hence,
the above characterization is pretty useless when it comes
to playing the game.
