The two lines you cut from my post were a reference to the relevant paper in
the Journal of Recreational Mathematics. This is also in the sci.math FAQ. To
understand the context of the paper, it might help to read the two earlier
papers on Mastermind in that journal.

In some variations, the value of a tie is not 1/2, since the order for the
next game is determined. In order to determine the value of a tie, hence
perhaps to play correctly, one needs to know how well correct play does.
