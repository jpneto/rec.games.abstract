CHARTER

The unmoderated newsgroup rec.games.computer.puzzle is for
discussion of computer games of the "puzzle" type. These are
generally games which have very little luck or manual dexterity
involved in their participation, but focus primarily on mental
excercises. These are not large-scale strategic or modeling
games, but rather games with small subgoals that require numeric,
verbal, visual, perception, trivia, or lateral thinking ability.
Popular examples of such games at the time of proposal are
"Myst", "The 7th Guest", and "3 in Three".
Action puzzle games like "Tetris", "Breakthru!", and "Puyo Puyo"
that require timing and reflexes but also have a strong intellect
component will also be discussed.

This newsgroup should not be for the discussion of writing
computer programs to solve puzzles, but for discussion of the
games already out there. The newsgroup is also not a forum for
discussing high-strategy games like RPGs and Simulation games.

RATIONALE

Previously these games have been classified under headings like
"Action" (for most platform games, even though there is little
dexterity involved); "Adventure" (where there is a loose
storyline that connects the puzzles; and "Misc". Many journals
and magazines now do include "puzzle games" as a separate
category of computer and video games.

On Usenet, these games have in the past been discussed in
platform-specific newsgroups such as
comp.sys.ibm.pc.games.adventure or comp.sys.mac.games, when in
practice most games are now released simultaneously for many
platforms. The proposed group would provide a single forum for
discussion.
