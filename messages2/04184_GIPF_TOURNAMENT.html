3th Open GIPF TOURNAMENT IN ANTWERPEN, BELGIUM 5th DECEMBER 1999 !!

You dont want to miss the battle of the GIPF stars!


What? What is gipf? Gipf is one of the greatest abstract games
of this decade. If you frequently visit rec.games.abstract you
should consider visiting the gipf homepage (see below).

Where ? De Nieuwe Zurenborger
Dageraadplaats 4
Antwerpen

When ? 5 december 1999

Who? There are 3 categories:
Group 1 for the experienced players (rating
1500 and higher) begins at 10:00, 7 games at 25
minutes per player. The fee is 350 BEF.
Group 2 for the less expecienced players
(rating 1500 and less, beginners) begins at
12:00, 6 games at 20 minutes per player. The fee is 200
BEF
Group 3 is for under-14 years old. NO FEE!

What do i get? Exciting games, friendly people, good competition,
oh yes: money prizes up to 15.000 BEF for the no 1 in
the first group.

Information? check out the GIPF page www@gipf.com
