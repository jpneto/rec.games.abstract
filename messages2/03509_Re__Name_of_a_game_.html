There are two key elements to the existential proof that Hex provides
a first-player win:

1) The presence of an extra token cannot harm the player.
2) There are no ties.

(1) isn't true for pente because a poorly-placed stone could lead to
a capture. And (2) isn't true for any of the t-t-t variants, AFAIK.
