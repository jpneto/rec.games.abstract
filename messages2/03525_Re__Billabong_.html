Billabong is a sort of Halma/Chinese Checkers gone mad game. The rules
can be found at:

http://www.gamecabinet.com/rules/Billabong.html

The game you describe evokes to me another game, "Mudcrack Y", which is
a connection game. A triangle is broken down into lots of irregular
bits. 2 players alternate filling in bits with their own color, trying
to create a pattern which connects all three sides of the triangle.

Oops. I hope you didn't reinvent this one.
