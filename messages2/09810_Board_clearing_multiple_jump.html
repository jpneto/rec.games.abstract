Cage at GamesByEmail:

. . . . . . . . O .
. O . . . . . . . .
O . . . . X O X . X
. O . . X . X . X O
O . . . . . . X . .
. O . . X . . . . .
. . . . . X . . . X
. O . . . . . . . .
. . . . . X . . . .
. . . . . . . . . .

The record before this was 7. 6-jumps are not unusual.

Jumps are only orthogonal in Cage and like-colored checkers can never
be orthogonally adjacent. This and the inward crush of checkers
creates the perfect environment for big jumps.
