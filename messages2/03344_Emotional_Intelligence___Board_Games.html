Emotional Intelligence & Board Games

From: Dr Bronia Grycz <destiny@gmtnet.co.uk>
Date: 21 07 1996 00:00:00 GMT


There is a growing school of thought which maintains that
success in life depends on a person's "emotional IQ" rather
than on his conventional IQ level.

The emotional IQ evaluates factors like

1. self awareness
2. impulse control
3. persistence
4. zeal and self motivation
5. empathy
6. social deftness

My question is:

Are there any board games which emulate aspects of life in
a way that the success in those games depends on the mix of
the above qualities rather than on the conventional IQ level?

Please, this is a serious question, not an invitation to
"Mensa bashing", into which my previous thread "Board Games
Experts on Internet" degenerated through no fault of my own.
