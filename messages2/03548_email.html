Since it is known that the first player can win on a 4x4x4 board, I
expect that the first player can win on a 4x4x4x4 board using the
4x4x4 strategy on a single hyperplane. You might have to make a move or
two to block an opponent's forcing move outside of the hyperplane. But
by not defending in the initial hyperplane, the first player should
be able to force a win even faster.

I think you need the board to be 5x5x5x5 to be interesting.
