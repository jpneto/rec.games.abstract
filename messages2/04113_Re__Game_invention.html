If the playing pieces can be easily adapted from other common games or
household items (for example, pawns or tokens from race games like Pachisi,
coins or little bits of colored paper), then the only parts have left to
publish are the board and rules. For this, you might want to contact someone
who is putting together a book or magazine of games and publish it that way
instead of as a stand-alone.
