Hi!

I find the list very interesting and 'good'
but would like to make a general point from
a personal viewpoint:

if the perfect game turns out to be a role
playing game, a game without winners/losers,
a game with chance happenings having a great
effect, a game in which because of its general
or particular setup - vid Starweb - a player
may have no chance to stop or even get to the
winner, or a game with unequal setups, then
I don't want to play it.

So, by extension, there is no perfect game.

To try to make a full attempt at defining perfect
one must construct a 'tree', perhaps starting
with:

A. role-playing
B. miniatures
C. strategy (a la Starweb, Death & Sorrow, etc)

etc.

and with perfect being defined/confined to
just the one type at a time.

Or, perhaps, starting with what the great
games are and asking what they have in
common, then wht would make each perfect,
and what the perfect-making in common is.
