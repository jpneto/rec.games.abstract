This is the right newsgroup for Othello related articles. rec.games.othello
has not (to my knowledge) gone past the RFD phase.

There are two sites:

The Internet Othello Server
telnet://faust.uni-paderborn.de:5000
where you can find various human and computer opponents at all hours of the day.

The Gateway To Othello
http://ronneby.hk-r.se/othello/othello.html
has an Othello game that can be played through Mosaic.

Hope this helps.
