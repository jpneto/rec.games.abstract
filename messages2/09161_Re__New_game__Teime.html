In the version I play, one person is trying to form a Y of the type
luigi describes, and the other is trying to block its formation. This
eliminates the race aspect, but still leaves the blocker with a
similar connection goal.

But for those for whom anything but isomorphic or identical connection
goals is distasteful, this version of the game will not satisfy. I
love isomorphic/identical goals where they are possible, but I also
wholeheartedly embrace asymmetry in those cases where it leads to an
interesting and balance-able game.
