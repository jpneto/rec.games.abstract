In article <Cw1rwE.KKv@usa.net>, bfinn@cscns.com (Bob Finn) writes:

I have played three excellent new abstract games from the UK: Fibonacci,
Latrel, and Pinpoint. They are all available from Just Games in London (71
Brewer Street, Piccadilly Circus, London W1R 3FB, Telephone:
071-437-0761). Also, Games and Puzzles (321 Essex Road, London N1 3PS ...
Tel (071) 226-4496). has been resurrected, and although it is not quite as
good as the old version, it is a serious and interesting game magazine. It
has reviews of all of the games I mention above. It has articles and game
designs by Prictahrd and Solomon. You should definitely check out Just
Games which sells many interesting German games (see especially Manhattan
and Auf Heller Und Pfennig). Have a great time searching. If you have more
specific questions about the games I mentioned, let me know.
