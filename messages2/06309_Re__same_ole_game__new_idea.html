Some more notes

*The breeders that are on the current game level are going to only
show one random trait, i.e., out of all three elements, life, speed,
attack, the breeder will only have one of those traits visible to the
players, so one visible trait, a high value of attack, may sway one
player to go in that direction, whilst the other player pursues high
values of speed; the two hidden traits are only known at the end of
the game level.




*the breeders will be in certain random locations on the game level/s.
Some are easily to reach; others are harder to reach; some are easy to
mate because of arriving first; some are to be fought over because of
the two players arriving at the same time. The decisions of the
players will be juggled, e.g., 1. pursue, highly desirable
traitattack value 6x, 2. not worth pursuing, too far away to reach in
time, 3. not worth pursuing, will have to battle, low life value.





*The aging and wear-and-tear process. In each level the character ages
and steady lose amounts of life value. It is the same with the wear
and tear process. Level of life are never replaced or returned to
maximum levels. So if a player's character reaches the end of a level
with a life of 5, and if and just if that player wanted to go to the
next level and not mate, and share his element "teach," he would have
to carry that life rating with him. By mating with a breeder the
character offspring's life rating will return to maximums level if not
increase significantly.



*Why the breeders are needed. In the higher levels, the levels favor,
i.e., they sometimes favor speed or sometimes favor attack. The design
of those levels can be easy, e.g., the levels that favor attack have
many obstacles, and the ones that favor speed have no obstacles and
favor maneuverability. The point is that the beginning perfect
character is not able to get to the higher levels because of the aging
and wear and tear process of the first level.


*I want to further the idea of "teaching and its minuses and pluses"
Teaching could also be link to knowledge, attention, learning, etc.
The point is that it is based on the player's performance on the
current level. It is passed on to only one of the offspring with the
desirable traits that the player seeks, maybe one with more speed than
attack or more life than either speed or attack.
