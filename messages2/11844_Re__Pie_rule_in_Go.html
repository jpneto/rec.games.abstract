xalerwons (3kyu) suggests 3-10 or 4-10 point as the best starting
point to work from for pie rule. but the real question is why use the
pie rule. Is restricing moves as your balancing rule really less
artifical than just saying "white loses by 6, so lets just give him
the 6 points to make it up"? i say no, compensation is the most
natural non-natural balancing rule for games where the goal is to
score points. Move restriction is no where close.
