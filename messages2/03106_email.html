Tie-breaker is disc-difference. In case of identical scores (pts. &
diff.) at group boundaries, lots will be drawn to decide group
membership.

In the final rounds the group-local return-games will be played to
determine the ranking *within* the groups by considering *all* games
played in the tournament.

Winner(s) of the tournament will be the player(s) in group 1 with the
most points. Players in groups >1 can decide whether to play the
return-games, to watch the return-games in group 1, or - as suggested
by Vince A. Sempronio - to save their time for other things to do.

During the tournament, IOS channel 3 will be reserved for the td (mic)
to make announcements etc.; channel 4 is for talking about tournament-
related things.

In the first part of the tournament "h PBIII.res" will show the
current round-by-round pairings, results, and ranking. Thereafter,
"h PBIII.res[1-4]" will give information about the group standings.
