Not that it's interesting or relevant or anything but here goes....

Edith prepares a special dinner to honor Mike for his new job.
However, Mike unknowingly makes other plans beforehand with the dean
of the university he is going to work for. When they break the news,
Edith (and Archie) becomes very upset. Mike is forced to hastily
change his plans and he and Gloria sit down for Edith's meal, but it
soon erupts into another argument between Mike and Archie.
