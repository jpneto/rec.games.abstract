StarSquadron is a FREE game where players can play as SDF combat pilots or
GIDF ground troopers. The game includes :

* Open Ended
* 1 Turn a Week ( RPG )
* Play as a Pilot or Ground Troop
* Detailed Webpage for each character + Photo
* Loads of High Quality Graphics.
* Chat Room
* Guestbook
* Form Character Creation
* Weekly Updates
* AVI Video Advertisement

Openings in the game are few but there is a fairly high turn over rate.
Once you're in you can play continually untill either you leave or you fail
to respond after a set number of turns. We have a waitling list of players
who recieve the turn while they wait. Come and check out the site...
