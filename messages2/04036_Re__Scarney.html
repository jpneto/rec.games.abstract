I own a copy of "Scarne's Encyclopedia of Games" copyright 1973. the
4-in-a-row on a 5x5 board game is Teeko. There is a chapter in the book
devoted to a game called "Skarney." Skarney is a moderately complex
partnership card game using two decks and with a general goal of forming
melds. There is also a section devoted to "Scarney." Scarney is essentially a
solitaire game, though Scarne includes modifications so that 2 or more players
can compete via scoring methods. The game is played on a 4x4 grid with 16
pawns numbered 1-4 in each of four colors. It is essentially a jumping game.
After the pawns are randomly placed, one is removed to make an empty space, and
the player then makes horizontal or vertical jumps until one pawn remains or no
more jumps are possible, and a score is determined by the color, position, and
number of the remaining pawn(s).
