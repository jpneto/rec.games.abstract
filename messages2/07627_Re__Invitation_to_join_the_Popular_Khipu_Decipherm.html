But given that over the last century and a half, Americans in little,
non-descript Norwalk, Ohio built 2 train lines, a golf course and
finally _an expressway_ through the largest ancient earth work monument
_in the world_, there's plenty of arrogance/stupidity to go around.

Built by the Hopewell people of the Ohio River valley around the year
800-1000 CE, the earthwork made the Stone Henge look like a rock
garden. But now the best preserved remains of the earthwork serve as
somewhat odd _polygonal_ mounds on Norwalk, Ohio's golf course.
