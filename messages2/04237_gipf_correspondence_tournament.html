Ever heard of the game GIPF?

On 1/2/00 the first GIPF email tournament will be played.
It will be a Knockout tournament of 8 players.
You play 3 gipf games against your opponent.4 days for a move.
If you win you play in round 2 , if you're knockouted, well, lick your
wounds and participate in the second GIPF email tournament. It's only a
game ! (but a special game it is..)
