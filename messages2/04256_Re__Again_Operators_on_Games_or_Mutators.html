>Any ideas for another 2nd order Mutator?

Inspired by some 2nd order functions:

- Twice (apply the mutator twice).

- Limit (apply mutator repeatedly until fixed-point (limit) is
reached).

- Compose (compose two mutators. Mostly useful in combination with
other 2nd-order mutators).
