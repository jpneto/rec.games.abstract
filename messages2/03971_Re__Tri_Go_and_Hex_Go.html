A long time back someone (sorry, forgot who) wrote in
Games & Puzzles magazine about their invention of "Rosette",
a version of Go played on the vertices of a hexagonal board.

The board is a hexagon of hexagons, 6 hexes on a side.
The rules are those of Go with one addition. If you
can occupy all six vertices of a hexagon the space in
the middle form a a "spiritual eye" which can never be
occupied, hence making the group safe from capture.
The author described this rule as natural, aesthetically
pleasing and also good for game balance, as Rosette is a much
more attacking game than "Quadrilateral" Go.

Hopefully this post will jog the memory of someone better
informed than I am.
