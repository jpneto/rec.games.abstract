There are/were lots of books detailing step-by-step solutions of the cube.
If you want to really understand the cube I suggest you try to find an
edition of David Singmaster's 'Notes on the Rubiks Cube' which has
anecdotes, a step-by-step solution, a dictionary of useful processes, and
many mathematical results about the cube group. I don't know how to get
hold of this since my copy is dated 1981.
