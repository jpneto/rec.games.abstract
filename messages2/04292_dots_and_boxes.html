I am currently developing a program which people will be able to use to play
dots-and-boxes against other humans online, as opposed to just against a
computer opponent. For it to work well, however, it will be important that
there are computer players to keep people playing at times when no other
humans are online.
If anybody would be interested in developing a computer player (or 'bot')
for the server, send me an email and we can discuss details. It may be of
interest since your bots will be able to play other humans and other bots,
giving you a good indication of how good their AI is.
The game should be available at www.dots.org.uk for free when it is
finished.
