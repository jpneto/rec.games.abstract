I have three rare games-related books up for auction on eBay, all still
with high bids well below their actual value (Game of Tarot is at $81
and is almost never available for less than $350, History of Board Games
is at $38 and is probbaly worth $100-130, and Twelve Tarot Games is
at just $24.99 and is worth at least twice that). The auctions are
all ending Monday evening.

The Game of Tarot, Michael Dummett
The definitive history of the tarot deck and the games played with it
(running to 600 pages). Hard to find and, as noted, pricey.
http://cgi.ebay.com/aw-cgi/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=1494573324

Twelve Tarot Games, Michael Dummett
A companion to the above, this gives detailed rules and strategy
notes on 12 of the better (in Dummett's opinion, but who should know
better?) tarot games. Even harder to find than Game of Tarot, although
usually cheaper.
http://cgi.ebay.com/aw-cgi/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=1494572998

A History of Board-Games Other Than Chess, H. J. R. Murray
The first comprehensive book (to my knowledge) on the history and
development of all sorts of board games, beginning in ancient Mesopotamia.
Also very tough to find.
http://cgi.ebay.com/aw-cgi/eBayISAPI.dll?ViewItem&item=1494572361
