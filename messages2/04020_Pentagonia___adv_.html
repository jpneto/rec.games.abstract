Pentagonia has roots in the Men's Morris games. These include
Nine Men's Morris, and Twelve Men's Morris, widely played strategy
games of ages past, and Three Men's Morris, aka Tic-Tac-Toe...

It won't take you long to master the basics...

However, Pentagonia's five-sided game board, and
the Pentagonia rules, make it a much better game
than its Men's Morris ancestors are. The first move
advantage existing in the ancestor games has been
reduced substantially... making Pentagonia a game in
which the ability to think ahead, see patterns developing,
and reason, are essential to victory.
