As Jonathan said in another post, "chance" might be a better word to use
than "luck". How about chance for #1, guesswork for #2, and chaos for #4?
These three components of luck are important enough in my mind to make
distinctions between. For example, Cosmic Encounter is much more
influenced by chaos and guesswork than chance, whereas the luck in
Settlers is mostly chance with a little bit of chaos thrown in, and just a
smidgen of guesswork.

I'm not sure that #3 is important enough to warrant a word. Doesn't it
basically combine memory games with physical games? Is such a union
informative? I.e. do memory ganmes and physical games behave in a similar
way distinct from all other games to a significant degree? I could be
missing out on classes of games that are covered by #3, however.
