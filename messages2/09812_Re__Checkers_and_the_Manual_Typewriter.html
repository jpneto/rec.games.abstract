I agree with most of this Mark. I agree that a "robust" game has a low
or 0 percent draw rate and a low turn order advantage. I disagree
however that you cannot gauge how high a draw rate will be in a given
game before playing it hundreds of times. Often, there are ways to
estimate how large a draw rate or turn order advantage will be based
on the mechanics alone. Cold games that aren't trivial and easy to
analyze manually are usually not susceptible to turn order advantages,
rather the advantage is held by the player in possession of the tempo
and the tempo is often changing hands.
