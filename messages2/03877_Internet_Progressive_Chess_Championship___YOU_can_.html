The World Internet Progressive Chess Championship
(http://www.cs.utk.edu/~hyatt/wipcc98/) is now taking
registrations! Come play in the FREE email chess
championship for one of the most popular chess
variants in the world!

What's progressive chess, you ask? It's the variant
where white gets one move, black two, white three,
black four, and so on. If you deliver check, you
end your series. Your first move must be spent
getting out of check. For information on how the
game is played, see http://www.cs.utk.edu/~hyatt/wipcc98/progintro.html.

Already over 25 people have signed up, and there are
still two months left to go! Come compete for prizes
which include a free year's membership to the
Internet Chess Club (http://www.chessclub.com/)!

To enter the championship, just send an email
to hyatt@cs.utk.edu with the following information:
* Your name (first last)
* Your email address
* Your regular chess rating (if you have one)
* Your experience with progressive chess (if any)

Beginners are VERY WELCOME! Even if
you have never played a game of progressive
chess before, go ahead and give it a try!

See you in the championship!
