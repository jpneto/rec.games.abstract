Chosen Chess

-----------------------------------

All FIDE rules apply, except:

- No Check or Checkmate.

- Castling is allowed when King and Rook are in starting position with no
other pieces between. Castling is considered a King move.

- In the first move, White chooses a movable piece of his army (in order to
remember chosen pieces, two little markers like draughts pieces could be
useful); then Black do the same.
In the subsequent moves, players:
1) move their chosen piece
2) choose another of their pieces
Its mandatory to move the chosen piece, and to choose ANOTHER piece (e.g.:
you cannot move the Queen, then choose again the Queen to be used the
subsequent
move; you must change the piece. However, you can move a Rook, then choose
the _other_ Rook).

- Victory conditions:
- Capturing the opponents King
- Stalemating the opponent:
- Capturing the opponents chosen piece
- Blocking the opponents chosen piece
- Baring the opponents King (cutting the possibility to choose
another piece)
