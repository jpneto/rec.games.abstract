Hello everyone,

Recently I send the special message to create the usernet group
"alt.games.draughts". The Charter below outlines the proposition
for use and rules. Although I have now sent the command, adoption
of this group is not always automatic, I need help from all
of you out there. Please start using this group by pointing
your news browser to "news:alt.games.draughts" and start posting
messages
to it. Post a request to your sysop/ISP/net service provider to
add this group in their list of available groups. If at first
messages do not get through, dont give up - persist, over time,
and with use, the net servers will take the group up, if it
is in operation.

------------------------------------------------------------
Charter:
"alt.games.draughts" is for the discussion about the game
of Draughts or Checkers - in all its variants - not only the
8x8 "Checkers" game known in the US/UK , but also
other 8x8 variants (Pool Checkers,Spanish, etc..) and
the 10x10 International variant.
This group exists to discuss all topics related to this
issue - Computer playing, Interesting games and
positions, current events and activities, and so on.
Irrelevant, over-repetitive or automated commercial
advertising is prohibited.
Posters are requested to be civil and refrain
from abuse.

Justification of Readership:
There is quite an active Checkers community already
in operation on the Web, but suprisingly no formal Usernet
discussion group as of yet. There are many WWW
pages dedicated to the game of Checkers and
Draughts.
