Although two-player games are also multi-player games, I
get the impression you're discussing games where
N > 2 and possibly N >> 2.

There's a fairly well-known phenomenon in N > 2 games
called "King-Making" (at least, that's what I've heard it
called -- I'm not sure I've ever seen anything very formal
on this) in which a player will not be able to actually
win the game, but he alone can decide which other player
will win.

I'm still not exactly sure whether this is a bug or a feature.
