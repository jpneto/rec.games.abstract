New Releases from Avalon Hill (March 1995)

From: kjm@panix.com (Kevin Maroney)
Date: 29 Dec 1994 11:59:22 -0500

According to Capitol City Distribution, Avalon Hill has a set of three
interesting boardgame releases due in March.

First is a reissue of _Starship Troopers_, the classic game based on
Robert Heinlein's novel.

Second is _Panzerblitz II_. It is unclear from the description whether
this is simply a reissue of PB with larger components or if this is a new
game; I think the latter. (Unfortunately, Cap City does not list game
designers in their product listings-- wouldn't it be a hoot it this were
by Dunnigan?)

Third is a new edition of Acquire, including "variant cards"-- almost
certainly the "Special Powers" variant by Sid Sackson.

_Starship Troopers_ and _PB II_ have a suggested retail of $35 each; _New
Acquire_ is $30.

Also, the computer version of Avalon Hill's Civilization (entitled
_Advanced Civilization_), which should not be confused with Sid Meier's
_Civilization_ in any way, should be shipping in March as well.
