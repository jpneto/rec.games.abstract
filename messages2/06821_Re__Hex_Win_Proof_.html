More generally, suppose a square is partitioned into two subsets A
and B. Then must one of A or B have a connected subset which
has nonempty intersection with opposite sides? The hex win solution
would follow from this if the answer is yes, since two hexagons either
do not intersect or intersect in a common side.

Conjecture: no in general, but yes if A and B satisfy nice properties.
