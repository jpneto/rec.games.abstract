After rereading what you wrote, it seems less powerful than I

So, in other words, it is an experimental piece you would play with?
I would recommend having one baseline version of chess you use and
then plug the pieces in there to see how they go. I would personally
recommend adding drops and gating to regular chess to get the piece
in, but that is your choice. Then experiment. The best thing to do
is run it in Zillions and watch the AI play. This is because, unless
you have a bunch of willing test subjects, it is going to be hard to
find anyone to do it with.
