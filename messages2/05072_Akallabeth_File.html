Hello Tolkien Fans! Some of you may have heard of MUSH-type games
(Multi-User Shared Hullucination). These are exciting and rich
text-based role playing environments where one can take on the role of
a character and interact with many other players. Akallabeth MUSH
assumes the role of 2nd Age Middle Earth, where players can choose
from the Sea King race of Numenor, the high and mighty Elves of
Lindon, the ugly and brute races from Mordor, barbaric Hillmen, stout
and hardy Dwarves in the vast mansions of Khazad-Dum, or those of the
Indepenent Culture and Restricted Races (Application Only), free to
roam, trade, and interact with most cultures. RP is based around Umbar
and Mordor, where the region is set off by conflict almost daily. All
cultures are within geological holding, and RP can become very
invoking and exciting. I urge you to check out
members.xoom.com/Akallabeth and see all that we can offer. If
you&#8217;re intrigued by the less-written 2nd Age of Tolkien, stop by
and check out Akallabeth MUSH. Telnet in, MUSHClient, SimpluMU,
Wundermu, or Pueblo, just to name a few clients that may get you
connected (www.mushclient.com). Here&#8217;s the address and port
number: mush.donet.com, port: 3175. Please come and indulge in the
many opportunities for character growth and fun RP in a safe,
well-coded, and rich atmosphere. Thanks! Questions or comments can be
directed to rstmyers@hotmail.com.
