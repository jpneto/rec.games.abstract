If you have a Java-capable browser, check out:

www.playsite.com for Backgammon, chess, Gomoku, Hex (my 2nd favorite
game), and Othello.

www.midigames.com for several cool games.

I also second the recommendation to check out www.gamerz.net for a
play-by-email server. Lots of games, including Twixt (my favorite). If
you don't have a browser, send an email to pbmserv@gamerz.net with the
SUBJECT line: help
The body of the message can be blank.
