>Hello all. Has anyone considered playing a game like so:
>
>Two players, Alice and Bob, are playing a game such as chess, checkers,
>go, etc. But they make their moves "simultaneously:" For any given
>board position, Alice writes down her move on a sheet of paper
>(momentarily keeping it secret from Bob), and Bob does the same at the
>same time.
>
>Once Alice and Bob have commited to their moves in this manner, Alice
>tells Bob her move and Bob tells Alice his; the moves are played out.
>
>If there is contention (e.g. in go, Alice and Bob place a stone in the
>same location) then a fair coin is tossed to determine who actually gets
>to place the stone, and the loser gets a free move.
>
>I haven't thought this out too much, but I think that it might
>interestingly change the dynamic for some games, such as Hex, where the
>first player generally has the advantage. (In the simultaneous version,
>there is NO "first" player.)
>
>Anyone have any comments?
>
>-Mark (mspinell@vt.edu)
