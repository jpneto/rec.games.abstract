I've been playing Coil by email, and found that the contract phase was
too long for email play. Since I'd like a game that plays equally
well in whatever environment one wishes to play it, I've modified the
rules according to a suggestion from Bill Taylor. Doesn't change play
very much beyond shortening the contract. Revised rules here:
