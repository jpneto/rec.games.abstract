We have just been through this acrimonius fiasco in the sci.chem
newsgroup.

One solution is for readers to ask the RFD author to remove the
offending .misc proposal from the RFD and issue a 2nd RFD.

This happened in rec.video when the readers strongly objected to
rennaming to rec.video.misc and the .misc proposal never got into
the CFV so all the aggro and agony of the voting process never
happened.

Mind you there's not much you can do if the RFD proponents decide to
press on come hell or high water.

The Usenet pundits made a big thing in sci.chem of getting things
changed at the RFD stage - not the CFV stage when things are set in
concrete.

Best of luck.
