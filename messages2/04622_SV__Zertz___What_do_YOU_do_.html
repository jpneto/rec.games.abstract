Here's a start...

The opening is really hard, it is fairly logical to start with a white
stone, since it makes greater chance of being able to capture 2 whites after
4 stones have been played on the board. Usually, a good strategy after a few
turns is to "exchange" 2 whites for 4 or 5 stones, by jumping them around.
This also works well as a tactic to finish of the game, isolating 2 whites
when you have 1 white, and can afford to give a few stones to the opponent.
Also, clearing the board is an important strategy, as on a clear board it is
hard to sacrifice anything. [about tournament version, but should work well
for basic game too]. The tournament game is complex enough so that I haven't
seen any tendency of wins for first/second player.

For a long time, I have been hoping to add some strategy for this great game
to my homepage http://www.abstractboardgames.com , but unfortunately this
hasnt happened yet.
