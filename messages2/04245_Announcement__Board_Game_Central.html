I've recently opened a new web site dedicated to board games:

http://boardgamecentral.com/

Currently the site is focussed primarily on traditional abstract board
games and family board games, but I intend to include sections for
newer German games in the future.

The site has links to information, software, online games, and books
for each of the games it covers. There are also sections for free
games, and general boardgaming links.

Board Game Central is my third gaming-related site, joining Solitaire
Central (http://www.solitairecentral.com/) and The House Of Cards
(http://thehouseofcards.com/).

Hope you'll take the time to visit and check the site out. All
comments are appreciated!
