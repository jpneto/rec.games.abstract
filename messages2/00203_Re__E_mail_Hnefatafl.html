I've played a smaller version of this called Tablut. It was hugely
biased towards the King's side, and I can't recall the other side ever
winning. While I've never played Hneffatfafflf, I recall reading a
book that drew the same conclusion. The author suggested the
following idea:

One player puts out a number of moves for the King to win, e.g. "King
to win in 17 moves."
The other player decides which side to play. If the King's side has
not won within the alloted time, it loses.
