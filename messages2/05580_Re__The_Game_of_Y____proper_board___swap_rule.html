It is very hard to write good programs for playing connection games.
In addition, the Zillions program engine is not at all designed for
this kind of game.

The result is usually a poor player (it usually looks like it plays
completely randomly until you give it a winning opportunity in 1 or 2
moves).

However, the Zillions files can still be used for online games via
TCP-IP, if there had only been players to play them with, or just for
having a board on the computer to record or analyze games.a
