Tiles: New release from BoneGames

From: bonegames@aol.com (BoneGames)
Date: 06 06 1995 00:00:00 GMT

BoneGames would like to announce the release of their new card game TILES.
TILES is a two player abstract, card game where opponents score points by
forming blocks of their own color. The player with the most points win.
TILES is not a computer game. You will need to download the TILES file and
print out the pages (5) in order to play the game. TILES is available for
free in either postscript or pdf format. TILES can be downloaded at our
www site located at:

http://www.3i.com.

Biskup at

bbiskup@aol.com.
