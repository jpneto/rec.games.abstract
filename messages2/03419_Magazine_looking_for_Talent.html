Magazine looking for Talent

From: ...ad infinitum Magazine
Date: 09 10 1996 00:00:00 GMT



The ...ad infinitum Alternative Fiction Magazine is looking for aspiring
artists, writers, and reviewers.

The ...ad infinitum Alternative Fiction Magazine is an internet magazine devoted
to giving net wanderers a unique outlet for their creative writing talents.

The magazine is open up to all kinds of imaginative fiction. We have pages
devoted to:

* Articles: These range from gossip over a new series that may or may not be
coming out to an in depth look into pieces of interest to our readers.

* Fiction: Fan-fiction and Original fiction accepted. Anything from poetry
to short stories to novellas, from fantasy to science-fiction to just
plain surreal will be looked at.

* Reviews: We look closer at books, movies, and TV shows.

* Recreation: An art gallery where our viewers can display original art.
Also in this area are comics and other recreational items.

* Editorials: From time to time the staff of the magazine have an opinion
they feel they have to share with everyone. If they have something to
be said, it will be found here.

* Behind the Scenes: Here is information on who we are and who our sponsors
are. We travel all over the world to go to science fiction and media
related conventions. Upcoming convention plans will be listed here.
Also, if we have questions for our viewers, or something that we think you
need to know, this is where we will leave a message.


If you are creative, show off your talents by submitting something to us.
We are a non-profit organization, so we gain nothing from doing this except
the warm fuzzy feeling of publishing peoples' works on the net.

We hope that as our popularity grows the magazine will grow to unseen bounds.
Check us out at http://www2.combase.com/~adinfin/

Please send all submissions to adinfin@phoenix.earthint.net
or
Follow the directions on the magazine.
