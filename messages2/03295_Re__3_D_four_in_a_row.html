In article <4o736i$rah@lyra.csx.cam.ac.uk>, cet1@cus.cam.ac.uk (Chris Thompson) wrote:
>I don't think I have ever seen the 3-player version in action.
>You will have to consider the possibility of alliances: almost
>certainly an alliance of two players will be able to ensure
>that one of them wins and the third player won't be able to do
>anything about it.

Assuming that each player is selfish there are still some interesting
tactics. For instance, if player A creates a line of 3 then player B can
be mean by ignoring this so that player C has to waste a move by blocking
player A (if C is really spiteful, he lets A win!). Sometimes this might
mean that player C has the unenviable choice of who out of A and B should
win. Also, it can be more difficult to win because a two-way line of 3 is
no longer sufficient.
