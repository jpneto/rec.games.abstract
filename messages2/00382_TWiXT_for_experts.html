author: Hartmut Thordsen

The strongest Twixt players come from Germany. Every year they
play a Twixt tournament by mail. They plan to write a book
about Twixt. The 'Kleines Twixt Expertenheft' is a preliminary
version. Every year (Oct. or Nov.) there is a Twixt tournament
sponsored by KLEE at the fair 'SPIEL' in Essen (Germany). There
you can meet the strongest Twixt players. If you are interested
in Twixt by mail contact: Klaus Hussmanns.
