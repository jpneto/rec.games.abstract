Let me guess - you're talking about the "Millstone" from Magic : The Gathering. If so, in my
experience using the Millstone to discard cards randomly is statistically ineffective. Since you
have no idea what cards are coming next, you are just as likely to get rid of bad cards as good
cards. However, the Millstone can have profound psychological effects. It can be very
frustrating.

Mike Harder
