QUESTIOS

In my Summary "Attributes of a perfect board game" (25/05/96)I
compiled a list of 10 attributes and asked four implicit questions:

Q1: Are the listed attributes fully representative of *perfect*?
Q2: Is the list complete?
Q3: Are they independent from one another?
Q4: Are they appropriately weighted?

There have been 10 more contributions,some with partial answers
to these questions.

ANSWERS

Q1 - There have been no objections to the attributes A1 to A10 nor
to the categorisation into subjective(S), objective (O) and
practical (P) aspects.

Q2 - The set A1 to A 10 was incomplete and 10 more attributes
have been proposed. The amended list is below.

Q3 - Only two contributors dealt implicitly with the question
of independence of attributes.Donovan Loucks defined a
*perfect* game as a *product* (hence independence) of
Attributes A3 (Rules) and A4 (Strategy). Patronius added
3rd dimension A6 (real life emulation).Note that if any of
these attributes assumes a zero value, the product is zero
and the game cannot be *perfect*.

Q4 - No attention has yet been given to weighting, i.e. the
relative importance which this attributes assume in the
definition of a *perfect* game.That imho can be done only
by a Delphi-technique after the final set of attributes has
been agreed upon. I have used this technique and found it
to be quite an effective tool in *difficult* decision-
making.


LIST OF ATTRIBUTES (Amended)


A *perfect* board game ought to satisfy

A(S) - SUBJECTIVE CRITERIA,i.e. it ought to

A(S)1- provide total enjoyment and entertainment
A(S)2- provide powerful aesthetic experience
A(S)3- provide challenge and satisfaction
A(S)4- be different every time you play it
people would never tire of it
A(S)5- give a sense of *drama* with distinct openong,mid- and
end stages (protasis,epitasis and catastrphe)
A(S)6- possess maximum synergy of all attributes


A(O) - OBJECTIVE CRITERIA, i.e. it ought to

A(O)!- have simple,clear and elegant rules with minimal exceptions
A(O)2- have elegant strategy, preferably not traceable by a computer
A(O)3- require significant player interaction
A(O)4- require enough skill to present hard choices among
possible moves
A(O)5- give all players the same chance of winning
but have a clearly defined winner.
A(O)6- symbolically reflect/model/emulate salient aspects
of human life and human interactions
A(O)7- have few, if any, imperfections
and withstand the test of time


A(P) - PRACTICAL CRITERIA, i.e. it ought to

A(P)1- be complete and clearly documented
A(P)2- be easily learned and take a reasonable time to complete
A(P)3- be highly playable and user-friendly
A(P)4- require a good memory and/or concentration and/or judgment
A(P)5- require the balance of intelligence and/or wisdom
and/or social skill to win it
A(P)6- teach people something
A(P)7- be *infectious* (self-propagating) or easily marketable.


Please, do you find the amended list complete and the attributes
essentially independent fro one another?
