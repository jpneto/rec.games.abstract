I assume a trivial position (i.e. someone just did move his pawn two spaces,
other player looses if he can capture en passant and wins otherwise, but the
first player could have avoided this by moving the pawn one space) are not
of interest. In any event, here is my attempt at a solution:

White has pawns on c2 and d2. Black has a pawn on e4, and
a king on e5. It is white's turn.

If white moves the c pawn, black does e3, white takes, then
black does K-d4.

If white moves to d3, black takes, white takes, black moves
K-e4.

If white moves d4, and en passant is allowed, black takes
with pawn, transpose to white moves to d3.

If white moves d4 and en passant is not allowed, black must
take with king. Then white does c2-c3, and wins.

I did not verify this with a board or program, so there might
be an error ; sorry if there is one.
