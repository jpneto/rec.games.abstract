There's "The Meditation Game," devised by Layman Allen, who also did
"Equations" and "Wff'N Proof" I believe. The game was given to me as a
freebie with my order from the Wff'N Proof people.

Their address is: 1490 South Blvd, Ann Arbor, Michigan 48104-4699

The board is a 7x7 grid, initially empty. One player begins by placing
a token on any of the 8 cells surrounding the central cell. Each
successive move consists of placing a token adjacent to the last
played token, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. 10 of the cells
on the board are green and 10 are orange. The object is to occupy
more of your color cells. The game is over when no more moves can
be made, or one player has occupied all 10 of his/her color.
The tokens are little blue rings.
