We played with about nine people; the problem was logistics.
It was hard to sustain excitement for several weeks over something
that took only a minute or so on most days, so we had lots of
"oh, yeah, I was supposed to propose a rule, wasn't I?" problems.

So we amused ourselves with rules like "Vadim's vote only counts
1/10" (We figured it out carefully to ensure that his vote was
utterly meaningless in any situation, but as valuable as possible
given that constraint.) Alas, even giving him new titles couldn't
sustain it for more than an additional month or so...
