solving small Othello boards

From: Jos Uiterwijk <uiterwyk@cs.unimaas.nl>
Date: 25 06 1998 00:00:00 GMT


I was informed that a few years ago someone solved a small variant
of Othello, i.e. the 6x6 board. This should be a win for the second
player. The solver was probably Joel Feinstein (sp?). Since we are
interested in solving games, especially in which zugzwang plays an
important role, I would be interested in any information in solving
small Othello (or any other relevant) games. This especially since we
want to give due credit to the persons deserving that. For your
interest, we are aware of the solutions of
- Connect-Four (Allen;Allis c.s.)
- Qubic (Patashnic; Allis c.s.}
- Nine Men's Morris (Gasser)
- Go-Moku (Allis c.s.}
- Domineering {Breuker c.s.}

Thanks in advance.

Jos Uiterwijk
