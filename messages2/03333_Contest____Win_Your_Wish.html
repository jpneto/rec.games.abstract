WHAT IS YOUR WISH, AND WHY SHOULD WE MAKE YOURS COME TRUE?

Criswell Design & Publication and Diversified Business Enterprises
is sponsoring an essay/art/poetry contest to convey your wish and
why it should come true for you. Whatever your dream is could be
yours if your entry is selected as the Grand Prize Winner. Let your
creativity or gift for words express your wish. Any form may be
used, whether it is written, photographs, drawings, etc. The entries
will be judged by a panel of 5 independent judges from the Phoenix
area. They will choose the Grand Prize Winner and three runners-up
to also win prizes. Please keep in mind that the winners will NOT be
chosen based solely on artistic ability. They will take into
account, however, the way in which you present your wish and your
creative expression of it.

This contest is for all age groups, but minors must have a parent's
permission to enter. Parents are encouraged to help their children
show their wish and why it is so important to receive it.

Also, you must give the name of the charity or organization you
would like to donate to, because part of the proceeds from this
contest will go to the charity of the Grand Prize Winner's choice.
Whether it's feeding homeless children, for the environment, or a
local church...whatever organization you feel should receive support
will get it all because of you.

The judges will also select other top entries to be compiled in a
work titled "Oh, I Wish." Even if you don't win the Grand Prize,
which is, of course your dream come true, you may be a featured
entrant in "Oh, I Wish."

Contest deadline is September 15, 1996. Winners will be notified by
October 15, 1996. All entries become the property of Diversified
Business Enterprises and Criswell Design & Publication and will not
be returned. Sponsors are not responsible for late, misdirected,
mutilated or illegible entries. The winners will also receive a copy
of "Oh, I Wish".

Submit your entry (no longer than one page for written entries and
11x17 for pictorials) with $5 in U.S. funds (to protect you, please
do not send cash) to Criswell Design & Publication. Make sure your
name, address and phone number is on the back of the entry. All
entries must be original work created by the participant,
unpublished and not under consideration elsewhere. To receive a copy
of "Oh, I Wish" please enclose $14.95 (plus $2.50 s+h) and allow 6-8
weeks for delivery after the September 15 deadline. If you would
like a list of winners and prizes awarded, please include a SASE
with your entry. You may also request a copy of the complete rules
and guidelines:
