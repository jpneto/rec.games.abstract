AIM:
The aim of the game is to give all counters to the opponent, by making
combinations, with three throws of three dice. The winner is the first who
has no more counters.

HOW TO PLAY:
At the beginning of the game, each player has 11 counters, and 10 other
counters are in a stock, between the two players.
The games goes on with to parts :
- the "charge" (loading) : during which stock counters are given to the
looser, until the stock is empty.
- the "dcharge" (unloading) : during which the winner gives his counters to
the looser.

Dices:
Each player has three throws of three dice. The purpose is to get at the end
of the turn a combination of dice allowing to get points.
For that, he can at each turn, keep the dice he wants , and throw only the
others at the next turn. Dice kept at the second turn, can be thrown at
thirds, if player wants.

Combinations:
- 421 : 4,2,1 in any order. During charge, gives all the counters of the
sock to the looser. During the dcharge, gives looser all the winner's
counter's but one.
- Threes : 222, 555 etc : gives 3 counters to the looser.
Pairs of 1 : gives the looser the same number of counter than the 3rd die
i.e 113 gives 3 counters.
