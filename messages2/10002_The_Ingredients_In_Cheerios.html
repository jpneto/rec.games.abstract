not that it's interesting or relevant or anything but here goes....

Whole grain oat, modified corn starch, corn starch, sugar, salt,
trisodium phosphate, calcium carbonate, monoglycerides, tocopherols,
wheat starch, annatto, vitamins & minerals: niacinamide, calcium
pantothenate, pyridoxine hydrochloride (vitamin b6), folate, iron.
