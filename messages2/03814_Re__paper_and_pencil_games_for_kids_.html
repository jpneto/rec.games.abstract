draw three dots on a paper.
Each player takes his turne by drawing a new dot, and connecting it
to two other dots (or twice to the same dot) by curves.

The curves must be drawn so that no curves cross.
No dot may be connected to more than 3 other dot.

The last player to do a legal move wins
