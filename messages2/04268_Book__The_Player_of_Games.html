It was nothing that someone said Chess was Life. Or,
let's say, similar statements from monomaniacs and
stories based on Chess or Go from some wise men were
at most Black & White photographs, now that I've found
that book published in 1988. How do we feel, we keen
players, explorers of rules, patterns, harmonics who
catch a glimpse of the universe in games and the other
way round, in those very moments, and beside? I've just
finished Iain M. Banks' novel, and oh boy, what a
breathtaking colorful success at showing how it is to
live deeply through games and to play through life.
The hero, "master of every board, computer and
strategy", just does that. And others around,
sometimes behind his back and yours. It's juicy.

Claude
