And some wonder why we comlain about the sorry state of the
mathematical/logical abilities of some students in the USA. This is no
puzzle...it is just a matter of incorrect wording in the original post.
This is clear by the looking at the problem in this manner:


Let's assume a universe with exactly $30. Then all we have to do is trace
the progression of the money through time.

1) The people get the room. The manager now has $30.

2) The manager sends the bell boy with the change. The manager has $25
and the bell boy has $5. 25 + 5 = 30.

3) The bell boy gives the change and keeps his 'tip'. The manager still
has $25. The three people each got $1. The bell boy kept $2. 3*1 + 2 +
25 = 30.
There is no missing dollar.
