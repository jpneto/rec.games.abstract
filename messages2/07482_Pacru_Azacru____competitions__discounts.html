Recently given the dubious distiction of being called an "osbcure gem"
(which seems hard when the latest set was only published late 2005), you can
play Pacru & Azacru at (free) Manchester, UK open competitions in July and
(not free) the Mind Sports Olympics in London in late August, and planning
for Ann Arbor USA at U-Con in November 2006.

Pacru 302 is not cheap (but then you are getting a family of 3 genuinely
different games) but you can get it very heavily discounted by playing it
on-line (on-line play is free) or offering your pronunciation of Pacru,
Shacru & Azacru. Or you can get it free by practising on-line and winning
a set at one of the competitions. See http://www.pacru.com for details.

We have also added a section on what the games are like, comparisions with
other abstract games. We welcome additional information or views on such
comparisions so freel free to contact us on that.
