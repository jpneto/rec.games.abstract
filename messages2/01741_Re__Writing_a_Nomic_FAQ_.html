The "Mao" I'm familiar with is a card game... sort of a parasitic meme,
if you ask me. The basic game is similar to Crazy Eights, except there
are various things you have to do in a certain way; naming one of the
Beatles if you play a four, for instance. It's not actually *fun* to play
except for watching the newcomers baffled expressions at the singing,
pantomime, et cetera which distract from the easily-guessed rules --
hence its memetic nature.
