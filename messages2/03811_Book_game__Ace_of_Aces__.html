I received this curious low-tech game in the form of a small book called
"Ace of Aces" involving WWI dogfighting. Two people play by each
deciding on an aerial maneuver then the books plot the resulting
relative positions of the planes. I only have the Allies book; I NEED
THE AXIS BOOK! Does anybody have this thing? Publisher and author:
Alfred Leonardi. Written in 1980, Manchester, CT.
