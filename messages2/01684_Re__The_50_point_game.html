An exact solution would be a probabilistic strategy, in every
position each player would have a probability with which they should
pick each possible bet. These type of solutions are "optimal" in the
sense that even if your opponent knows your strategy there is no
better strategy than the one they were using anyway, and vice versa.


I'm going to Brussel Sprout this game :-). Consider the game where
you don't have edges (i.e. infinitely long track). The players
keep playing until both are out of chips. If the token is in it's
original position, the game is a draw. Otherwise the player whose
side it is on wins.
