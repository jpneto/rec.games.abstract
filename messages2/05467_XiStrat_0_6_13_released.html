XiStrat (the full name is 'Extended Strategy') deals with turn-based,
networked multiplayer strategy board games on 3d-visualized polyhedra
(e.g., chess, go, reversi variants, etc.).

It contains a server, client GUI, autoplayer engine, utilities, and
documentation.
