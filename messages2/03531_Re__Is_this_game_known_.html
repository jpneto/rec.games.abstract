This sounds like a one-dimensional version of a game called Fencing
that was described in a sci-fi book years ago. The object was to
aquire area on a grid by secretly deciding on grid points. The player
who mapped out the *smaller* area got to add it to his total, that
way you have to second guess the other player and ballance greed
against the likelyhood of getting more area. There was more to
than that, such as exactly how to choose points, calculating area,
your new area had to be adjacent to your current area, etc.
I think the book was called 'Macroscope' or something similar.
