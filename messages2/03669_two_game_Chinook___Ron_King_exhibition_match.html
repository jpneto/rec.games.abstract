two game Chinook - Ron King exhibition match

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 28 07 1997 00:00:00 GMT


A two game exhibition match between the Man vs. Machine World Checkers
Champion (Chinook) and the current Human World Checkers Champion (Ron King)
will be held on Tuesday July 29 starting at 2:30 pm EDT in Providence,
Rhode Island. This event is part of the Fourteenth National Conference on
Artificial Intelligence. Called the "AAAI Hall of Champions", this features
an exhibition of game-playing programs capable of competing at human world-
championship level.

You can follow the move-by-move progress of each game by going to the Chinook
home page:

http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/
