I've been reading the first issue of Abstract Games magazine
over the past few days, and I want to say that it is terrific!

Kerry and Connie Handscomb have produced a magazine that is both
beautiful and informative. If you like pure strategy games (like
Trax and Lines of Action), I highly recommend that you subscribe,
and support their efforts. E-mail conniekerry@sprint.ca for more
details. (And no, I am not affiliated with them in any way).
