You mean 3612 out of 4032, since the two kings have different colors.

The white king can be in: 4 corners, each of which leaves 64-4 squares
for the black king; 24 edge spaces, each of which leaves 64-6 squares
for the black king; 36 central spaces, each of which leaves 64-9 squares
for the black king. Total 4*60+24*58+36*55 = 3612.
