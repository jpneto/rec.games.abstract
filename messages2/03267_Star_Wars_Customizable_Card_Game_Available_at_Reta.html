PARKER BROTHERS ANNOUNCES STAR WARS CUSTOMIZABLE CARD GAME

The fastest growing segment of the game industry -- customizable cards --
has a new player, with Parker Brothers introducing the Star Wars
Customizable Card Game. "The popularity of customizable card games,
combined with one of the hottest licenses, will make the Star Wars
Customizable Card Game one of our best sellers this year," said Parker
Brothers Executive Vice President of Marketing Bob Wann.

The Star Wars Customizable Card Game, a two-player game, was developed in
conjunction with Decipher Inc., which is also marketing compatible,
individual player 60-card packs and 15-card expansion sets. Parker
Brothers' two-player game is designed to introduce new players to the
excitement of customizable card games. Wann said he expects the Parker
Brothers game to appeal to a broad audience.

The Parker Brothers game contains two pre-customized 60-card decks and a
15-card expansion set drawn from the universe of 320 Star Wars cards
created by Decipher. The Parker Brothers game also includes six unique
cards available only in this edition, among them Luke Skywalker and Darth
Vader, which should appeal to experienced players and Star Wars fans
alike.

The Parker Brothers Star Wars Card Game is available at retailers nationwide.
