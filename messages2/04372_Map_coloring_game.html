I remember Martin Gardner talking about a map coloring game,
in which one person was the Maximizer, and the other was the
Minimizer, and they tried to maximize and minimize the colors needed
for on a map. Normal map coloring rules, of course.

Does anyone remember the exact rules to this game? Obviously
the Maximizer can't use anyone color they want, or else they will just
stick in a new color each turn. Can they only use colors that the
Minimizer has already used? Or is there a list of 4 or 5 colors that
the Maximizer can use, and the Minimizer loses if he needs an extra
color (i.e. a 5th or 6th)? The last rule seems the most plausible, but
then I have trouble imagining many maps being a fair game. It seems
like most maps would turn out to be very lopsided contests, giving
an easy with for Max or Min, depending on the map.

Does anyone know the rules? If you've played this, do you
know how well the game works? If my last proposed rule is correct,
how many colors does it work best with. Is there a number of colors
such that the Minimizer can always win? (i.e. can I color any map
with just eight colors, even with a hostile map coloring doing
half the countries?).
