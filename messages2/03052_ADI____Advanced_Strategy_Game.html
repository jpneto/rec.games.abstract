ADI -- Advanced Strategy Game
MUCH IMPROVED - new Freeware v.1.8 now uploaded.

ADI may be the most advanced strategy game ever created -- with the
simplest rules! ADI includes all of the strategic adventure of GO and
Chess as well as the vast realities of the most ancient Sanskrit Tibetan
culture. ADI uses the same board and stones as GO. This is a computer
version of the advanced strategy game. For the Apple Macintosh.

ADI v.1.8 is available in the Macintosh Entertainment forums on Compuserve
and America OnLine. Anyone with the means is welcome to put it into an
FTP site.
