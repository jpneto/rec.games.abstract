I wonder whether there is any way of getting quantum entanglement into
an abstract game. One could try to do things like the following:

Imagine a game mechanic where you have two units in separate parts of
the board. At any stage (or in response to various game conditions) you
can throw a die which has a persistent effect on both units.

For example, you have two pieces that move like Knights, but at some
stage you have the option to convert one into a Rook and the other into
a Bishop, but you don't know which will become which. Do you set up a
Bishop pin on your opponent's Queen, and hope for the best - or are
there better ways to handle this situation?

Anyway I just thought I'd throw this out as a novel play mechanism -
perhaps somebody can think of a game it (or some other quantum-inspired
idea) would suit.

Of course, it would be even better if the pieces became inextricably
linked in a complex way as the game went on...
