Open Othello Tournament


On February 3rd, there will be an open Othello tournament at
ios (or at JIOS if ios is down). Both computer programs and human
players are welcome to participate.

The tournament format will be 7-round schweizer system, with
the games played with time controls of 25 0 120 (25 min/player for
the game).

The tournament will start at 16:00 ios time (same as German
time). After 3 rounds, there will be a break for lunch, etc. The
tournament will end at about 24:00 ios time.

The addresses to ios and JIOS are:
ios: telnet faust.uni-paderborn.de 5000
JIOS: telnet 165.93.117.31 5000

If you want to participate, you need to get a registered
account at ios and JIOS. Type 'help register' at either server to
get information about how to register. In addition, you need to
be logged in at the server 15 min (that is 15:45) before the
tournament starts. I would also appreciate if those who plan to
participate informed me, but that is not necessary.


For more information, feel free to contact me.
