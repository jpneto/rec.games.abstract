I'm not sure I agree, although the situation is certainly much better than it
is in Chess, Checkers, and Othello.

The best Go program plays at a low kyu level, which is better than a lot of
human players, so I don't think it's fair to say that "most" human players can
defeat "any" computer.

Two board games were invented with the express purpose of being hard for
computers to play them well, and they're both very good games. Search the web
for the games "Octagon" and "Arimaa".
