Torben, I was curious why you chose to chop your hexes up the way you did.
I'm guessing that your comments regarding the "height/width ratio of the
tiles" and "standard trading card size" indicates that you would create
tiles from playing card stock. However, if you examine the central tile
in the configuration above, you'll see that its 6 semi-hexes must match up
with 12 semi-hexes on 6 other tiles. If you construct the tiles as in the
diagram below, you only have to match the 6 semi-hexes in the central tile
with _6_ semi-hexes on _4_ other tiles:

This configuration would make the placement of tiles much easier, while
your method would make laying out the board a puzzle in itself! One of
the most obvious advantages of such a system of tiles would be reduced
cost, since creating a cutting die for a sheet of hexagonal tiles would
probably be more involved than for a sheet of rectangular tiles.
