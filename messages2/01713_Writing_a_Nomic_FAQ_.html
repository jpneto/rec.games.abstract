Hiya,
There have been a number of enquiries in these newsgroups of late
about the game of Nomic by Peter Suber. As there is a growing amount of
Nomic-related activity on the net, I thought it would be an idea (maybe
even a good idea) to produce a Nomic FAQ, post it to a couple of
newsgroups once or twice, and then stick it on some out-of-the-way FTP
site and forget about it.

Does that sound like a good idea to you?

If you have anything to contribute to the FAQ, please mail me.
Especially if you are running a Nomic Game or some form of Nomic support
on the net (eg FTP, WWW, etc)

The Frequently Asked Questions to be answered, as I see them, are:

1/ What is NOMIC?
2/ Where can I get the rules?
3/ Is it being played on the Net? Where?
4/ What is/was Nomic World? Where is it now?
5/ What other Nomic-like games are there?

Is there anything I should add to this list?
Are there any books/references I should mention other than Hofstadter's
Metamagical Themas, and Suber's own Paradox of Self Amendment?

Thanx in advance,
