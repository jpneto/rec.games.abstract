Ralf Gering: "Emergo lacks the excitement of piece promotion, which is
essential to draughts variants. To me Emergo is a 'castrated' version
of column checkers."

Christian Freeling: "And why should having your men promoted during
the game give more exitement than having them all 'promoted' in the
first place? Is that logic? I could as well argue that rather than
being 'castrated', Emergo has a lot more balls to begin with."

Gotta go with Christian on this one. A game of kings seems ballsier,
though I must confess I've never considered the precise testicularity
of a game. This will be Ralf Gering's legacy. The ball sack index.

Christian Freeling: "I don't know precisely in which year which games
were invented, because the explosion of SE Fireworks in 2000 destroyed
all my possesions, including all registrations."

I for one am greatly relieved that Christian survived the blast, even
though his hair apparently did not.

Christian Freeling: "[Dameo] is uncharted in terms of strategy, and
doesn't have a 'draws problem'."

That's like saying, "I installed a set of new tires yesterday and they
haven't worn out yet." Checkers wouldn't have a "draws problem"
either if there was only a Dameo-sized body of Checkers knowledge.

Christian Freeling: "The problem with Int. Draughts [...]:
Draws all over the finals! Int. Draughts is in a midlife crisis and

school variants."

Ralf Gering: "Boyer already described in 1951 at least 20 different
