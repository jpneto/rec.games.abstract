is practically real time. GBE sends out the turn notification
immediately, and if you have a minutely notifier, you can stay on top
of the game. It's not quite as fast a real time but there's not as
much pressure to hurry up and move either. Your notifier icon can sit
there and flash until you finish whatever task at hand. Then a couple
clicks later you're at the game board again.
