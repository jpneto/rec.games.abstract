While not exactly what your friend is looking for, he might be
able to adapt some of the games or ideas from "Beyond Competition",
a neat book by Sid Sackson. It contains six pencil and paper
games that two or more players play to attain a cooperative goal.
The ISBN is 0-394-83605-7.
