I've been playing the HipBone Games by email, on a variety of mailing
lists, and as party games in person for a couple of years now, and I think
the bugs have been worked out. But there's a fuzzy area having to do with
the meaning of "links" which different people feel differently about.

The games can be played with "tight" links, ie only those links supported
by, say, a particular Encyclopedia can be claimed, or in a more open-ended
way, ie any links can be made as long as other players accept them -- as
with conversation, where your respondent's idea of what's "on" or "off"
topic will have some bearing on how far the conversation strays, whether
fictions are as welcome as facts, etc, ie a lawyer doing consultation on a
murder case will have very different "rules of conversation" from an Irish
uncle fond of the whiskey and given to telling tall tales to the nephews
and nieces...

> We supply a full report two weeks after we receive the game.

So... rather than strictly "catching rule inconsistencies", I guess what
would most interest me would be to hear how different players felt about
"tight" vs "loose" links, and "competitive" vs "aesthetic" approaches to
play. And if you keep records of the game(s) you play, and the various
moves, associative links etc you make, I'd be delighted to see what you
come up with.

Jaron Lanier, who coined the term "virtual reality", said recently:

:: The reason I never became a chess fanatic is that I realized the
:: game had a formal framework that would make it difficult to turn
:: it into a purely aesthetic experience. Since it's a game with a
:: formal sense of what winning is, it has limited options for
:: creative extrapolation. There's no such thing as freestyle chess,
:: in which making elegant moves is valued more highly than
:: capturing your opponent's king...

I think it's fair to say that the HipBone Games can be played as "tighly"
and by-the-book as a game of Scrabble, but that they excel when they are
played "freestyle" -- in such a way that "making elegant moves is valued...
highly".

Hoping this will provide you with an hour or two of delight...
