This game is now in place on pbmserv@gamerz.net as snakepit (hexagonal board)
and snakebox (orthoginal board).

The maximum number of "heads" is defaulted at 2. But at challenge time you can
specify however many you wish. A head "spawns" on each move until max heads
is reached. and then you have to kill them by making them unable to move.

FWIW, kiwibill (as Bill Taylor is known on pbmserv) has been routinely cleaning
my clock.
