As if you were interested, there's a quiz show in the UK which is based on
that idea (can't remember the name ATM). The contestants have to name words
containing the consonants the computer picks in that order (actually, it might
have to have the named letters first and last, I can't remember) within 4
seconds, and they get bonus points for naming the longest word in the round.

I've never really watched it so can't vouch for the validity of that, but it
works quite well. A time limit would be a good idea, as, given enough time,
anyone could think of words for virtually any combination. For instance, you
could probably think of something for XBH eventually, but it might take a
while...
