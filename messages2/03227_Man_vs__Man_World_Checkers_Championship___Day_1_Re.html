Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship - Day 1 Results

From: lake@cs.ualberta.ca (Robert Lake)
Date: 19 03 1996 00:00:00 GMT

The Man vs. Man World Checkers Championship is underway in Christ Church,
Barbados. It is a 40 game (3 move ballot) match being played between the
defending Human World Champion, Ronald "Suki" King from Barbados, and the
1994 United States Champion, Don Lafferty. The match continues until Friday
March 29.

At the conclusion of play on Monday March 18, Don Lafferty leads 1-0 with 3
draws. Analysts say Ron King played a published loss in an opening called
the Denny at about the seventh or eighth move of the first game.

A more complete biography of the players is on-line at the Chinook home page:

http://www.cs.ualberta.ca/~chinook

Daily updates will be posted to rec.games.abstract and the Chinook home page.
