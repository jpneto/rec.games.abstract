How about a HoardMoves(G,n) operator? At any time instead of moving you may delay up to n moves and then make them all
at once in the future. For instance, in HoardMoves(Go,1) you could skip a turn and then play the two moves in parallel
on some future turn. A group would have to have 3 eyes to be alive. (Assuming moves were made in parallel. You could
always say that the two moves must be made in sequence which keep the 2 eyes rule.)
