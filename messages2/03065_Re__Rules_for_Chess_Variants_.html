I remember one mentioned in Games magazine many years ago. This
variant was called "Survival of the Species". Before the game, one
type of piece is chosen. The winner is the one who captures all of
the opponent's pieces of that type (i.e. all 8 pawns, both bishops,
etc.). In this variant, unless the king is chosen, it has no special
meaning and simply becomes a somewhat weak piece. "Check" is not
called when you threaten a goal piece.
