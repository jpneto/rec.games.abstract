Yes, I believe that this is very much so, on a fairly high level. However,
considering ratio of the points white get and black get for a game, this is
no longer true, as there are so many (~50%) draws in chess, depending a bit
on the level of play.

Of course this could be evened out using a rule that one player can setup a
position, and set a starting player, and the other player gets to choose
which side to play. But then, chess seems to be made to be a draw anyway, so
why not now :-)

Then, for 100 games, 40 white wins, 50 draws and 10 black wins, we get 65
white points and 35 black, a ratio of around 2-1.
