Trax Challenge Match Results

From: Richard Rognlie <rrognlie@erols.com>
Date: 11 07 1996 00:00:00 GMT


The Trax Challenge Match for 1996-1997 between Dan Pless (current Trax
World Champion) of the United States and Donald Bailey (former Trax
World Champion) of New Zealand was played from July 7th to July 10th,
1996.

Qualifications for the right to challenge Dan began in February with a
series of local and regional tournaments which culminated with the
candiates tournament which started in May.

Donald defeated each of the other regional candiates to advance to the
Trax Challenge Match (a best of 11 series).

Donald won the series 6-4 in a hard fought battle between the two people
many consider to be the foremost players of Trax.

For further information on Trax, please see the Trax Resource index
http://www.massey.ac.nz/~DBailey/trax/trax.html
