Cute, but the problem is marred by the fact that the order of White's
moves is not forced. There are 3 solutions, since White can play g5 on
move 1, 2, or 3: g5-Rb6-Rg6-g4-g3, Rb6-g5-Rg6-g4-g3, Rb6-Rg6-g5-g4-g3.

By the way, back when I used to play giveaway chess, we considered
stalemate (i.e. player unable to move in turn but not out of material)
a draw. I don't know what the official (e.g. NOST or AISE) rules say.
