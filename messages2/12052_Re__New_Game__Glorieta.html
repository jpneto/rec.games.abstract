Right. This is the big question for this version. I included the game
of chicken here because the version of the game without it (ie the
version where you keep playing neutrals until someone makes a loop)
feels over-tactical to me, in the realm of say, go-moku. The neutrals
are powerful and as they build up they swiftly bring the game to a
close. It typically ends for me around move 8. That's short. Since
my motivation for focusing on this objective was to realize the
potential I saw for strategy, I didn't bother to mention this
version.

However, perhaps I shouldn't dismiss it quite as readily as I have,
because it looks like good play will stretch it out some, and in any
case there are people who like tactics-dominated games. Maybe I'll add
it to the post.
