Hello everyone,

After about four years researching for a book about hnefatafl, I still
haven't got a book written. I have, however, been writing a smaller
scale booklet, an illustrated collection of articles, containing
proposals for reconstruction of various different historical games, with
the reasoning behind them. I've put a draft up on the Links section of
my hnefatafl web site:

http://tafl.snigfarp.karoo.net/tafl/

It's the bottom link on the page. It's a PDF, about 1.2 megabytes in
size. It's designed to be printed onto both sides of A4 paper, which can
then be folded into an A5 booklet of 48 pages. The four articles I've
put it in are: Reconstructing a Game for the Ballinderry Board;
Reconstructing the Alea Evangelii; Reconstructing Tawlbwrdd;
Reconstructing Tablut.

I hope someone finds it useful.
