*shudder* I'm not a lawyer, but I've been living with a specialist in
Intellectual Property Law for long enough for some knowledge to rub off:

1. You automatically have copyright in any artistic work, so
"copyrighted" is misleading.

2. Copyright only protects the expression of an idea, and not the idea
itself, so there is nothing to stop competitors from copying your idea
with different graphics. If you want to protect your idea, then you
need to look into patents.
