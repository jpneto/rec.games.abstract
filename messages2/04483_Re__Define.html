The usual definition in this group is two person, perfect information
games. "Perfect information" means that if you look at a board, you know
everything about the state of the game; no randomness, no hidden
information.

I can't think of any game that would be abstract by your
definition. Maybe ghost? (Players say a letter, one each at a time. The
player who spells a word gets a point, but if someone says a letter that
prevents the current letters from spelling a word and is challenged,
_taht_ player gets a point. lowest score wins.) Anything with physical
components would no longer be abstract.
