It is that time of year again...

The 2nd Annual Trax PBeM Tournament!

It will be played via Richard's PBeM Server (pbmserv@netcom.com). If you
are interested, and are not already a user of Richard's PBeM Server, send
a message to pbmserv@netcom.com with 'help' as the subject. You will
then need to 'signup' with the PBeM Server.

Once you have signed up with the PBeM Server, send a note to me
(rrognlie@netcom.com) that you wish to participate in the Trax tournament.
I'll divide the pool of players into N groups of ~6 players each. You
will play each member of your pool in a round robin. The top 2 players
(including ties) will advance to the final round.

Play will begin 1 October.
